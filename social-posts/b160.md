## LinkedIn

Every AI tool you use daily — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot — runs on the same architecture.

It's called the transformer, and it was introduced in a single research paper in 2017.

Here's what you need to know:

- **Self-attention** lets AI understand that "bank" means something different next to "river" vs. next to "money" — simultaneously, not sequentially
- **Multi-head attention** lets the model capture grammar, meaning, and context in one pass
- **The same design** now powers weather forecasting, protein biology, image generation, and code completion
- **Training costs fell 90%** since 2022 — powerful AI is now accessible to UK SMEs for pounds per month
- **Key limitation**: transformers struggle with precise arithmetic and long chains of logic — know this before deploying
