What IS a memecoin?

A cryptocurrency whose value comes from social consensus, not technology or utility.

No real use case. No unique tech. Just community + memes + the expectation that more buyers arrive before the early holders sell.

📦 Launch — anyone can do it in minutes on Pump.fun
📈 Hype — influencer mentions, Reddit co-ordination, trending lists
🚀 Peak — early holders sell
💥 Crash — 70-95% decline in weeks or months
💀 Death (or very occasionally, survival and revival)

**Tweet 4:**
The risks UK investors often miss:

Rug pulls — team dumps and disappears
Liquidity risk — your own selling crashes the price
Concentration risk — 5 wallets holding 50% of supply
Anonymous teams — no accountability, no recourse

>90% of Pump.fun launches end in >90% losses within 2 weeks.

HMRC treats memecoins the same as any other crypto.

Every swap = taxable CGT event.
Swapping one memecoin FOR another memecoin = CGT event.
Using a memecoin to buy something = CGT event.

18-24% CGT on gains above £3,000 per year.
