# Social Posts: b161 — AI and Climate Change

## LinkedIn

AI won't solve climate change on its own — but it's becoming one of the most powerful tools we have.

Here's where it's already making a measurable difference:

- **Weather forecasting**: Google's GraphCast predicts 10-day global weather in under 1 minute — the same task previously took hours on supercomputers
- **Energy grids**: AI demand forecasting improved accuracy by 20% for National Grid ESO, reducing wasted renewable power
- **Emissions tracking**: Satellite AI can now identify methane leaks from individual oil wells and landfills worldwide
- **Agriculture**: Precision nutrient management could cut UK agricultural N₂O emissions by 15–20%
- **The tradeoff**: Training GPT-4 used ~50 GWh — equivalent to 4,500 UK homes for a year

The net case for AI in climate is still strongly positive — but the energy question matters.
