## LinkedIn

Liquid staking is one of the most popular DeFi strategies right now. It's also one of the most misunderstood.

The pitch sounds simple: earn ~3-4% yield on your Ethereum without locking it up. Get a liquid staking token (stETH, rETH) you can use elsewhere.

But there are risks that often get glossed over:

**Depegging** — Lido's stETH briefly traded at ~95p in the pound during the 2022 crypto crisis. If you needed to exit at that moment, you realised a real loss.

**Smart contract risk** — These are complex protocols. Lido holds over 9 million ETH. A bug could be catastrophic, and no audit is a guarantee.

**Slashing socialisation** — When validators misbehave, losses are spread across all depositors. Your balance can drop without you doing anything wrong.

**HMRC treatment** — Staking rewards are income, not capital gains. Taxable at your marginal rate when received. Detailed records are essential.
