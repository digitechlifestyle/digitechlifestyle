## LinkedIn

UK crypto investors keep asking the same question: can I hold Bitcoin in an ISA?

The answer is no — but the reason why matters, and the workarounds are worth knowing.

HMRC explicitly excludes cryptoassets from qualifying ISA investments. The big platforms won't offer it. With CGT now at 24% for higher rate taxpayers and a £3,000 annual exempt amount, the tax bill on significant gains can be substantial.

What CAN you do inside an ISA?

→ MicroStrategy shares (holds 500,000+ BTC, price tracks Bitcoin closely)
→ Coinbase, Marathon Digital, Riot Platforms — all ISA-eligible
→ Some crypto-adjacent investment trusts (confirm eligibility first)

What about crypto ETPs on the LSE? Listed since March 2024 — but the FCA's retail ban still applies. Professional investors only.

Is ISA eligibility coming? HM Treasury is considering it. Industry groups are lobbying hard. No confirmed timeline — but plausible within 2-3 years if the regulatory framework matures.

For now: alternatives exist, proper tax planning matters, and HMRC is actively enforcing via data-sharing agreements with UK exchanges.
