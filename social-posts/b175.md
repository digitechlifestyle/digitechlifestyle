## LinkedIn Post

AI is cutting contract review from 90 minutes to 10 minutes. Here's what's really happening inside UK law firms:

**The tools being deployed right now:**

→ **Luminance** (Cambridge spin-out) — reviews contracts, flags anomalies, compares against standard terms. Used by 600+ organisations. 90-min review = under 10 minutes.

→ **Harvey AI** at Allen & Overy — generates redlines and summaries, not just flags. 50% reduction in research time on standard legal questions.

→ **Lexis+ AI** — answers natural language research questions with UK case law citations in seconds.

**What the SRA actually requires:**

Solicitors must:
✓ Understand the tool's limitations
✓ Verify every AI-generated citation against primary sources
✓ Ensure client data isn't used to train third-party models
✓ Have a qualified solicitor review all AI output before it reaches a client

AI output sent directly to a client = professional conduct breach.

**The access to justice question:**

Citizens Advice estimates 1.5 million people in England and Wales face a legal problem each year they can't afford proper help with.

AI tools can draft standard documents affordably. They cannot replicate the strategic judgment of a solicitor in a contested dispute.

The gap is closing — but it hasn't closed.
