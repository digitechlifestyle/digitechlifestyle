Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is the fix.

It connects AI to your own document library — in real time. Every time someone asks a question, the AI retrieves the most relevant chunks from your files, then answers using that context.

**Tweet 2:**
How RAG works in 3 steps:

1️⃣ INGEST — your documents get split into chunks, converted into meaning vectors, stored in a vector database

2️⃣ RETRIEVE — when you ask a question, it finds the most semantically similar chunks (meaning match, not keyword match)

3️⃣ GENERATE — the AI answers using those chunks as context, with source citations

**Tweet 3:**
Real UK deployments:

→ NHS: clinical staff query guidelines and drug interactions in plain English (30% less time searching)
→ HMRC: exploring RAG for tax guidance search
→ Clifford Chance, Linklaters: precedent search in seconds

Small businesses can build this for under £100/month.

**Tweet 4:**
RAG vs fine-tuning — quick guide:

RAG = access your docs at query time. Best for large, changing knowledge bases. Cheap to update. Auditable.

Fine-tuning = train the AI on your data. Best for consistent style/format. Expensive. Gets stale when info changes.

Most UK businesses should start with RAG.

**Tweet 5:**
