# Article to Video Workflow

This is the Digitech Lifestyle production workflow for turning one article into a full YouTube content package.

## Goal

Turn one DigitechLifestyle.com article into:

- 1 long-form YouTube video
- 3 to 5 YouTube Shorts
- 1 thumbnail
- 1 YouTube description
- 1 pinned comment
- 1 blog backlink
- 1 newsletter mention

## Step 1: Choose Article

Pick one article from DigitechLifestyle.com.

Best starter categories:

- Bitcoin basics
- XRP explained
- AI tools
- Crypto wallets
- Scam alerts
- Stablecoins
- Blockchain guides

## Step 2: Convert Article to Script

Use the prompt in `prompts/video-production-prompts.md`.

Target:

- 8 to 12 minutes
- 1,800 to 2,500 words
- Beginner-friendly
- Educational tone
- No hype
- No financial advice

## Step 3: Create Voiceover

Options:

- CapCut free AI voice
- Local open-source TTS
- ElevenLabs later if budget allows

Save voiceover in:

`audio/`

## Step 4: Create Visuals

Use:

- Canva Free
- CapCut stock visuals
- ComfyUI AI images
- Screenshots from your own website
- Royalty-free icons and backgrounds

Save images in:

`images/`

## Step 5: Assemble Long Video

Use:

- CapCut Free for beginner workflow
- FFmpeg for automation later

Save exported video in:

`video/`

## Step 6: Create Shorts

Cut the long video into 3 to 5 short clips.

Shorts ideas:

- Best hook from the video
- One myth explained
- One warning or scam tip
- One simple definition
- One future prediction or key takeaway

Save shorts in:

`shorts/`

## Step 7: Upload to YouTube

Include:

- SEO title
- Strong description
- Link to DigitechLifestyle.com
- Affiliate disclaimer if needed
- Hashtags
- Pinned comment

## Step 8: Repurpose

Post on:

- YouTube Shorts
- TikTok
- Instagram Reels
- Facebook Reels
- X/Twitter
- Newsletter

## Safety Rules

- Do not promise profits
- Do not say any coin is guaranteed to rise
- Add education-only disclaimer
- Avoid fake urgency
- Avoid misleading thumbnails
- Check facts before publishing
