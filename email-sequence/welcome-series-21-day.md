## Day 7 — Slow down (Joe's draft 3, polished)
**Subject:** The most expensive word online is "hurry"

Hi,

One of the biggest mistakes beginners make online is rushing.

They see a video, advert, influencer post or "limited-time offer" and feel they need to act quickly. That is exactly what scammers and bad marketers want.

A good opportunity will still make sense after you have checked it properly. A bad opportunity usually falls apart once you ask serious questions.

Before you sign up, buy, invest or connect your wallet, slow down and check:

- Is this regulated?
- Is this trusted?
- Is this too good to be true?
- Can I find real reviews?
- Do I understand what I am doing?

Taking one extra hour to check something can save you weeks, months or even years of regret.

Our Scam Watch section tracks the traps we find, in plain English:
