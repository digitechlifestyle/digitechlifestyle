<p>Kain R. Heinlein will be a playable character in <em><a class="link-relationship" title="Games &gt; Fatal Fury: City of the Wolves [20 articles]" href="https://www.gematsu.com/games/fatal-fury-city-of-the-wolves">Fatal Fury: City of the Wolves</a></em>, and Andy Bogard, Joe Higashi, and Mr. Big will join Ken and Chun-Li from <em>Street Fighter</em> as downloadable content characters for Season Pass 1, <a class="link-relationship" title="Companies &gt; SNK Corporation [409 articles]" href="https://www.gematsu.com/companies/snk">SNK</a> announced. <span id="more-944206"/></p>
<p>Get the latest details below.</p>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>Arriving in South Town: Five Season Pass 1 Downloadable Content Characters</strong></p>
<figure class="aligncenter"><a class="link-image" data-featherlight="image" href="https://www.gematsu.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Fatal-Fury-City-of-the-Wolves_2025_02-16-25_023.jpg"><img class="lazyload" srcset="https://www.gematsu.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Fatal-Fury-City-of-the-Wolves_2025_02-16-25_023-320x180.jpg 320w, https://www.gematsu.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Fatal-Fury-City-of-the-Wolves_2025_02-16-25_023-480x270.jpg 480w, https://www.gematsu.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Fatal-Fury-City-of-the-Wolves_2025_02-16-25_023-768x432.jpg 768w, https://www.gematsu.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Fatal-Fury-City-of-the-Wolves_2025_02-16-25_023-1024x576.jpg 1024w, https://www.gematsu.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Fatal-Fury-City-of-the-Wolves_2025_02-16-25_023-1280x720.jpg 1280w, https://www.gematsu.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Fatal-Fury-City-of-the-Wolves_2025_02-16-25_023-1440x810.jpg 1440w, https://www.gematsu.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Fatal-Fury-City-of-the-Wolves_2025_02-16-25_023-1920x1080.jpg 1920w" data-sizes="auto"/></a></figure>
<p><em>Street Fighter</em>‘s Ken and Chun-Li, <em>Fatal Fury</em>‘s Andy Bogard and Joe Higashi, and <em>Art of Fighting</em>‘s Mr. Big will take to the streets of South Town throughout 2025 and 2026. Each downloadable content character will be available as part of the <em>Fatal Fury: City of the Wolves</em> Special Edition, which includes the base game for $59.99 USD on all platforms. Fans who pre-order will also receive the iconic <em>Fatal Fury 2</em> costume for Terry Bogard.</p>
<figure class="aligncenter"><a class="link-image" data-featherlight="image" href="https://www.gematsu.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Fatal-Fury-City-of-the-Wolves_2025_02-16-25_022.jpg"><img class="lazyload" srcset="https://www.gematsu.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Fatal-Fury-City-of-the-Wolves_2025_02-16-25_022-320x180.jpg 320w, https://www.gematsu.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Fatal-Fury-City-of-the-Wolves_2025_02-16-25_022-480x270.jpg 480w, https://www.gematsu.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Fatal-Fury-City-of-the-Wolves_2025_02-16-25_022-768x432.jpg 768w, https://www.gematsu.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Fatal-Fury-City-of-the-Wolves_2025_02-16-25_022-1024x576.jpg 1024w, https://www.gematsu.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Fatal-Fury-City-of-the-Wolves_2025_02-16-25_022-1280x720.jpg 1280w, https://www.gematsu.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Fatal-Fury-City-of-the-Wolves_2025_02-16-25_022-1440x810.jpg 1440w, https://www.gematsu.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Fatal-Fury-City-of-the-Wolves_2025_02-16-25_022-1920x1080.jpg 1920w" data-sizes="auto"/></a></figure>
<p><!-- AD: content --></p>
<p>Season Pass 1 characters will release during the following windows:</p>
<ul>
<li>Andy Bogard: Summer 2025</li>
<li>Ken: Summer 2025</li>
<li>Joe Higashi: Fall 2025</li>
<li>Chun-Li: Winter 2025</li>
<li>Mr. Big: Early 2026</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Dominate South Town with Kain R. Heinlein</strong></p>
<figure class="aligncenter"><a class="link-image" data-featherlight="image" href="https://www.gematsu.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Fatal-Fury-City-of-the-Wolves_2025_02-16-25_001.jpg"><img class="lazyload" srcset="https://www.gematsu.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Fatal-Fury-City-of-the-Wolves_2025_02-16-25_001-320x180.jpg 320w, https://www.gematsu.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Fatal-Fury-City-of-the-Wolves_2025_02-16-25_001-480x270.jpg 480w, https://www.gematsu.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Fatal-Fury-City-of-the-Wolves_2025_02-16-25_001-768x432.jpg 768w, https://www.gematsu.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Fatal-Fury-City-of-the-Wolves_2025_02-16-25_001-1024x576.jpg 1024w, https://www.gematsu.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Fatal-Fury-City-of-the-Wolves_2025_02-16-25_001-1280x720.jpg 1280w, https://www.gematsu.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Fatal-Fury-City-of-the-Wolves_2025_02-16-25_001-1440x810.jpg 1440w, https://www.gematsu.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Fatal-Fury-City-of-the-Wolves_2025_02-16-25_001.jpg 1920w" data-sizes="auto"/></a></figure>
<ul>
<li>Voiced by Brett Calo in English, Yuichi Nakamura in Japanese</li>
<li>A young idealist burning with ambition, Kain’s domineering resolve sees him tearing through numerous hostile organizations around South Town. While cleansing the town, he makes an unsettling discovery: his older sister, Marie, is being held captive. Realizing he cannot rescue her alone, Kain allies with Rock Howard to confront her captors. With a twist of fate, a dream for the future, and an unwavering resolve, Kain turns to the upcoming KOF tournament, where he hopes to return Marie to safety once at last.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Fight Early this Month with Eight Open Beta Characters and Four Stages</strong></p>
<figure class="aligncenter"><a class="link-image" data-featherlight="image" href="https://www.gematsu.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Fatal-Fury-City-of-the-Wolves_2025_02-16-25_015.jpg"><img class="lazyload" srcset="https://www.gematsu.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Fatal-Fury-City-of-the-Wolves_2025_02-16-25_015-320x180.jpg 320w, https://www.gematsu.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Fatal-Fury-City-of-the-Wolves_2025_02-16-25_015-480x270.jpg 480w, https://www.gematsu.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Fatal-Fury-City-of-the-Wolves_2025_02-16-25_015-768x432.jpg 768w, https://www.gematsu.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Fatal-Fury-City-of-the-Wolves_2025_02-16-25_015-1024x576.jpg 1024w, https://www.gematsu.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Fatal-Fury-City-of-the-Wolves_2025_02-16-25_015-1280x720.jpg 1280w, https://www.gematsu.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Fatal-Fury-City-of-the-Wolves_2025_02-16-25_015-1440x810.jpg 1440w, https://www.gematsu.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Fatal-Fury-City-of-the-Wolves_2025_02-16-25_015-1920x1080.jpg 1920w" data-sizes="auto"/></a></figure>
<p>Choose from eight fierce fighters in the open beta, including Kain R. Heinlein, the final boss of <em>GAROU: Mark of the Wolves</em>. The beta will feature four stages—Training, Freight Express, B. Port, and Training Ground—as well as online Ranked Matches, Casual Matches, and Room Matches and the offline Tutorial.</p>
<p>Playable characters include:</p>
<ul>
<li>Kain R. Heinlein</li>
<li>Rock Howard</li>
<li>Terry Bogard</li>
<li>Hotaru Futaba</li>
<li>B. Jenet</li>
<li>Mai Shiranui</li>
<li>Preecha</li>
<li>Vox Reaper</li>
</ul>
<p><em>Fatal Fury: City of the Wolves</em> introduces a stunning, comic-inspired art style that’s a visual treat for series fans, combined with fights fueled by the cutting-edge REV System to deliver dynamic matches. The open beta offers the public their first opportunity to experience the modernized <a class="link-relationship" title="Genres &gt; Action [2,499 articles]" href="https://www.gematsu.com/genres/action">action</a> of <em>Fatal Fury: City of the Wolves</em> at home, featuring two distinct control schemes tailored to engage both newcomers and seasoned pros.</p>
</blockquote>
<p><em>Fatal Fury: City of the Wolves</em> is due out for <a class="link-relationship" title="Platforms &gt; PS5 [8,873 articles]" href="https://www.gematsu.com/platforms/playstation/ps5">PlayStation 5</a>, <a class="link-relationship" title="Platforms &gt; Xbox Series [6,727 articles]" href="https://www.gematsu.com/platforms/xbox/xbox-series">Xbox Series</a>, <a class="link-relationship" title="Platforms &gt; PS4 [27,720 articles]" href="https://www.gematsu.com/platforms/playstation/ps4">PlayStation 4</a>, and <a class="link-relationship" title="Platforms &gt; PC [22,379 articles]" href="https://www.gematsu.com/platforms/pc">PC</a> via <a class="link-external" href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/2492040/FATAL_FURY_City_of_the_Wolves/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Steam</a> and <a class="link-external" href="https://store.epicgames.com/p/garou-city-of-the-wolves-556c5a" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Epic Games Store</a> on April 24.</p>
