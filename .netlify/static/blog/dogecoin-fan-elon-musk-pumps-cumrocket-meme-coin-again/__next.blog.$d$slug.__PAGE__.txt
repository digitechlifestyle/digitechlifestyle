<div style="position: relative; overflow: visible; font-size: 1.2em; line-height: 1.58;">
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">A lot of rockets have taken off under Elon Musk’s watch, but the tech CEO and SpaceX co-founder just boosted one billed as digital ejaculate—for the second time.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">The price of CumRocket (</span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/cumrocket" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">CUMMIES</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">) had jumped 68% Friday, hovering above $0.007 a day after Musk <a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://x.com/elonmusk/status/1874978009628950699" target="_blank" rel="nofollow external noopener">posted a meme on X</a> (formerly known as Twitter) bearing the obscure token’s name. </span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">In Musk’s post, the popular internet meme Pepe the Frog is depicted communicating with the IRS, discussing the tax implications of on-chain activity involving “cumrocket” and “NFTitties.”</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Musk’s post may have referenced the IRS’s postponement of tax reporting requirements on Wednesday, a </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/n-25-07.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">delay</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> that affords crypto investors relief until next year. But after Musk’s post, CumRocket’s price quintupled in less than an hour, rising 429% to $0.022 from $0.004. That peak was short-lived, but CUMMIES remains up substantially from before Musk’s tweet.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Since Musk </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://x.com/elonmusk/status/1113009339743100929?lang=en" target="_blank" rel="nofollow external noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">called</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Dogecoin his “fav cryptocurrency” back in 2019, his ability to drum up interest in assets that trade on little more than vibes has been well documented.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">And while Musk’s influence has grown notably since he spent $250 million to help President-elect Donald Trump win the White House, Musk still serves as an incidental kingmaker for meme coins in the cryptosphere.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Launched on BNB Chain in 2021, a British TikToker and software engineer who goes by &#8220;Lydia&#8221; created CumRocket. Adult actresses Lacey London, Shelby Paris, and Lauren Phillips served as ambassadors for the meme coin months after its release.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">CumRocket promoters have zeroed in on Musk’s posts before. In 2021, a group of enthusiasts believed Musk had endorsed the token through a </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://decrypt.co/72821/elon-musk-rocket-cum" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">string of emojis</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> on X. At the time, CumRocket pumped as high as $0.21, before crashing to $0.07 just three days later.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">On Friday, the CumRocket’s X account reiterated its love for the billionaire meme connoisseur, reprising the string of emojis that Musk had once used.</span></p>

<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">A federal judge in Manhattan found last year that Musk’s public statements regarding Dogecoin were “aspirational and puffery,” rejecting a class-action lawsuit brought by Dogecoin investors. After an acrimonious </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://decrypt.co/291862/dogecoin-investors-drop-248-billion-suit-elon-musk" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">back-and-forth</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, the lawsuit was dropped in November.</span></p>
<h2 class="sc-b2a202e4-2 bmropA gg-dark:text-white scene:font-itc-avant-garde-gothic-pro scene:font-light" style="margin-top: 2em; text-align: left; padding-bottom: 16px; margin-bottom: 16px; border-bottom: 1px solid #dfe2e4;"><b>Sam Bankman-Fried?</b></h2>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">When it comes to prominent CumRocket owners, FTX co-founder and former CEO Sam Bankman-Fried may have been among them. In 2023, Coinbase Product Director Conor Grogan </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://x.com/jconorgrogan/status/1620439556750462976" target="_blank" rel="nofollow external noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">linked</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> the disgraced crypto mogul’s defunct hedge fund to a purchase of 950,000 CUMMIES.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">The wallet linked to Bankman-Fried had purchased the CumRocket sum for $173,000 worth of BNB in June 2021, BscScan </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://bscscan.com/tx/0x3cc4c30f5ea86c44c46e9ca3a9c3d615f9f76679a1ba0965e0c2fe88983dbd84" target="_blank" rel="nofollow external noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">data</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> shows. When the wallet’s CumRocket holdings were later swapped for Ethereum over a year later, the tokens were only valued around $2,200.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Grogan did not immediately respond to a request for comment from </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Decrypt</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">.</span></p>

<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">CumRocket’s takeoff Thursday isn’t the first time that a meme coin has jumped on Musk’s Pepe-related posts. It’s not even the first time this week. </span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">The anthropomorphic amphibian, originally created by Matt Furie in 2005, </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://decrypt.co/resources/what-is-pepecoin-pepe" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">inspired</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> the launch of an $8.8 billion meme coin in 2023. Pepe (<a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://decrypt.co/price/pepe" target="_blank" rel="noopener">PEPE</a>) is the third-largest meme coin by market cap, behind Shiba Inu (<a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://decrypt.co/price/shiba-inu" target="_blank" rel="noopener">SHIB</a>) and Dogecoin (<a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://decrypt.co/price/dogecoin" target="_blank" rel="noopener">DOGE</a>)—the $53.8 billion meme coin that Musk has plenty of history with.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">When Musk changed his X name to Kekius Maximus earlier this week, dawning “Pepe the Frog Emperor&#8221; as his profile picture, a meme coin named Kekius Maximus (</span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/kekius-maximus" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">KEKIUS</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">) </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://decrypt.co/299337/dogecoin-hero-elon-musk-crashed-meme-coin" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">soared</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> in response. The token was modeled on an image that was first posted to X on Dec. 10.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Still, the meme coin’s run was short-lived. It tanked when Musk’s profile reverted back to his actual name and face.</span></p>
