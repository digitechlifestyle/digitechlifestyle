<div style="text-align:justify;">
<p>As AI becomes increasingly integral to business operations, new safety concerns and security threats emerge at an unprecedented pace—outstripping the capabilities of traditional cybersecurity solutions.</p>
<p>The stakes are high with potentially significant repercussions. According to Cisco’s <a href="https://www.cisco.com/c/m/en_us/solutions/ai/readiness-index.html">2024 AI Readiness Index</a>, only 29% of surveyed organisations feel fully equipped to detect and prevent unauthorised tampering with AI technologies.</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Continuous model validation</h3>
<p>DJ Sampath, Head of AI Software &amp; Platform at <a href="https://www.cisco.com/site/uk/en/index.html">Cisco</a>, said: “When we talk about model validation, it is not just a one time thing, right? You’re doing the model validation on a continuous basis.</p>
<div class="wp-block-image">
<figure class="alignright size-full is-resized"><img loading="lazy" loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="792" height="792" src="https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/dj-sampath-cisco.jpeg" alt="Headshot of DJ Sampath from Cisco for an article on securing enterprises in the AI era." class="wp-image-16886" style="width:207px;height:auto" srcset="https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/dj-sampath-cisco.jpeg 792w, https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/dj-sampath-cisco-300x300.jpeg 300w, https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/dj-sampath-cisco-150x150.jpeg 150w, https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/dj-sampath-cisco-768x768.jpeg 768w, https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/dj-sampath-cisco-125x125.jpeg 125w, https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/dj-sampath-cisco-200x200.jpeg 200w, https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/dj-sampath-cisco-285x285.jpeg 285w, https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/dj-sampath-cisco-250x250.jpeg 250w, https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/dj-sampath-cisco-100x100.jpeg 100w, https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/dj-sampath-cisco-60x60.jpeg 60w, https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/dj-sampath-cisco-400x400.jpeg 400w, https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/dj-sampath-cisco-600x600.jpeg 600w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 792px) 100vw, 792px"/></figure>
</div>
<p>“So as you see changes happen to the model – if you’re doing any type of finetuning, or you discover new attacks that are starting to show up that you need the models to learn from – we’re constantly learning all of that information and revalidating the model to see how these models are behaving under these new attacks that we’ve discovered.</p>
<p>“The other very important point is that we have a really advanced threat research team which is constantly looking at these AI attacks and understanding how these attacks can further be enhanced. In fact, we’re, we’re, we’re contributing to the work groups inside of standards organisations like MITRE, OWASP, and NIST.”</p>
<p>Beyond preventing harmful outputs, Cisco addresses the vulnerabilities of AI models to malicious external influences that can change their behaviour. These risks include prompt injection attacks, jailbreaking, and training data poisoning—each demanding stringent preventive measures.</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Evolution brings new complexities</h3>
<p>Frank Dickson, Group VP for Security &amp; Trust at <a href="https://www.idc.com/">IDC</a>, gave his take on the evolution of cybersecurity over time and what advancements in AI mean for the industry.</p>
<p>“The first macro trend was that we moved from on-premise to the cloud and that introduced this whole host of new problem statements that we had to address. And then as applications move from monolithic to microservices, we saw this whole host of new problem sets.</p>
<div class="wp-block-image">
<figure class="alignleft size-full is-resized"><img loading="lazy" loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="354" height="354" src="https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/frank-dickson-idc.jpeg" alt="Headshot of Frank Dickson from IDC for an article on securing enterprises in the AI era." class="wp-image-16885" style="width:239px;height:auto" srcset="https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/frank-dickson-idc.jpeg 354w, https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/frank-dickson-idc-300x300.jpeg 300w, https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/frank-dickson-idc-150x150.jpeg 150w, https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/frank-dickson-idc-125x125.jpeg 125w, https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/frank-dickson-idc-200x200.jpeg 200w, https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/frank-dickson-idc-285x285.jpeg 285w, https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/frank-dickson-idc-250x250.jpeg 250w, https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/frank-dickson-idc-100x100.jpeg 100w, https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/frank-dickson-idc-60x60.jpeg 60w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 354px) 100vw, 354px"/></figure>
</div>
<p>“AI and the addition of LLMs… same thing, whole host of new problem sets.”</p>
<p>The complexities of AI security are heightened as applications become multi-model. Vulnerabilities can arise at various levels – from models to apps – implicating different stakeholders such as developers, end-users, and vendors.</p>
<p>“Once an application moved from on-premise to the cloud, it kind of stayed there. Yes, we developed applications across multiple clouds, but once you put an application in AWS or Azure or GCP, you didn’t jump it across those various cloud environments monthly, quarterly, weekly, right?</p>
<p>“Once you move from monolithic application development to microservices, you stay there. Once you put an application in Kubernetes, you don’t jump back into something else.</p>
<p>“As you look to secure a LLM, the important thing to note is the model changes. And when we talk about model change, it’s not like it’s a revision … this week maybe [developers are] using Anthropic, next week they may be using Gemini.</p>
<p>“They’re completely different and the threat vectors of each model are completely different. They all have their strengths and they all have their dramatic weaknesses.”</p>
<p>Unlike conventional safety measures integrated into individual models, Cisco delivers controls for a multi-model environment through its newly-announced <a href="https://www.cisco.com/site/us/en/products/security/ai-defense/index.html">AI Defense</a>. The solution is self-optimising, using Cisco’s proprietary machine learning algorithms to identify evolving AI safety and security concerns—informed by threat intelligence from Cisco Talos.</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Adjusting to the new normal</h3>
<p>Jeetu Patel, Executive VP and Chief Product Officer at Cisco, shared his view that major advancements in a short period of time always seem revolutionary but quickly feel normal.</p>
<div class="wp-block-image">
<figure class="alignright size-full is-resized"><img loading="lazy" loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1000" height="1200" src="https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Jeetu-Patel-Cisco-Headshot.jpg" alt="Headshot of Jeetu Patel from Cisco for an article on securing enterprises in the AI era." class="wp-image-16888" style="width:231px;height:auto" srcset="https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Jeetu-Patel-Cisco-Headshot.jpg 1000w, https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Jeetu-Patel-Cisco-Headshot-250x300.jpg 250w, https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Jeetu-Patel-Cisco-Headshot-853x1024.jpg 853w, https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Jeetu-Patel-Cisco-Headshot-768x922.jpg 768w, https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Jeetu-Patel-Cisco-Headshot-167x200.jpg 167w, https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Jeetu-Patel-Cisco-Headshot-238x285.jpg 238w, https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Jeetu-Patel-Cisco-Headshot-208x250.jpg 208w, https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Jeetu-Patel-Cisco-Headshot-83x100.jpg 83w, https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Jeetu-Patel-Cisco-Headshot-50x60.jpg 50w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 1000px) 100vw, 1000px"/></figure>
</div>
<p>“<a href="https://iottechnews.com/news/waymos-robotaxis-hailable-anyone-san-francisco/">Waymo</a> is, you know, self-driving cars from Google. You get in, and there’s no one sitting in the car, and it takes you from point A to point B. It feels mind-bendingly amazing, like we are living in the future. The second time, you kind of get used to it. The third time, you start complaining about the seats.</p>
<p>“Even how quickly we’ve gotten used to AI and ChatGPT over the course of the past couple years, I think what will happen is any major advancement will feel exceptionally progressive for a short period of time. Then there’s a normalisation that happens where everyone starts getting used to it.”</p>
<p>Patel believes that normalisation will happen with AGI as well. However, he notes that “you cannot underestimate the progress that these models are starting to make” and, ultimately, the kind of use cases they are going to unlock.</p>
<p>“No-one had thought that we would have a smartphone that’s gonna have more compute capacity than the mainframe computer at your fingertips and be able to do thousands of things on it at any point in time and now it’s just another way of life. My 14-year-old daughter doesn’t even think about it.</p>
<p>“We ought to make sure that we as companies get adjusted to that very quickly.”</p>
