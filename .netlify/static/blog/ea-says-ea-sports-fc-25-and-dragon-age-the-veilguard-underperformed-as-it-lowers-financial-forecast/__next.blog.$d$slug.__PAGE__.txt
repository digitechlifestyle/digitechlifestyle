<p>&#13;<br />
EA has lowered its financial forecast for its current fiscal year, pointing to the &#8216;underperformance&#8217; of EA Sports FC 2025 &#8211; which it says accounts for the &#8220;majority&#8221; of the shortfall &#8211; alongside lower-than-expected interest in <a data-keyword="true" href="https://www.eurogamer.net/dragon-age-the-veilguard-walkthrough-tips-and-tricks-guide">Dragon Age: The Veilguard</a>.&#13;
</p>
<p>&#13;<br />
As noted in EA&#8217;s <a href="https://ir.ea.com/press-releases/press-release-details/2025/Electronic-Arts-Pre-Announces-Preliminary-Q3-FY25-Results/default.aspx">revised outlook</a> for the year ending 31st March 2025, the company was previously forecasting a  &#8220;mid-single-digit growth in live services net bookings&#8221;. However, it now expects a &#8220;mid-single-digit decline&#8221;, blaming the &#8220;majority of the change&#8221; on EA Sports 2025 &#8211; which saw a &#8220;slowdown in momentum&#8221; following its release in September.&#13;
</p>
<p>&#13;<br />
Additionally, EA has reported lower-than-expected interest in Dragon Age: The Veilguard, saying BioWare&#8217;s fantasy RPG &#8220;engaged&#8221; approximately 1.5m players during the quarter &#8211; a number it describes as being &#8220;down nearly 50 percent from the company&#8217;s expectations.&#8221;&#13;
</p>

BioWare is back.&#8221;&#13;
</p>

Eurogamer&#8217;s Chris Tapsell was less enthused by EA Sports FC 2025, however. &#8220;Another year where the annual tweaks settle in such a way as to recreate, or reposition, the same old problems that have been here since the beginning,&#8221; he <a href="https://www.eurogamer.net/ea-sports-fc-25-review">wrote in his three star review</a>. &#8220;This is EA Sports FC 25.
