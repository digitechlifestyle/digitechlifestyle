<div data-component="text-block" class="sc-18fde0d6-0 dlWCEZ">
<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fezwLZ">Artists in Devon say they are concerned government proposals to change copyright law could make it easier for Artificial Intelligence (AI) companies to use their work for free.<!-- --></p>
<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fezwLZ">The government has proposed law changes which would allow AI developers to be able to use creators&#8217; content on the internet to help develop their models, unless the rights holders elected to &#8220;opt out&#8221;.<!-- --></p>
<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fezwLZ">The Devon Artist Network said it was &#8220;very worried&#8221; about the proposals and Devon illustrator Sarah McIntyre said it would be &#8220;disastrous&#8221; for her. <!-- --></p>
<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fezwLZ">A consultation on the plans by the  Department for Science, Innovation and Technology (DSIT) has closed and a spokesperson said no decisions had been taken yet.<!-- --></p>
</div>
<p><h2 class="sc-518485e5-0 DkKTF">Destroying careers<!-- --></h2>
</p>
<div data-component="text-block" class="sc-18fde0d6-0 dlWCEZ">
<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fezwLZ">Ms McIntyre, from Bovey Tracey, said she would be impacted if the change was to happen.<!-- --></p>
<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fezwLZ">She said: &#8220;We&#8217;ve always owned our work, that&#8217;s just a part of British law. <!-- --></p>
<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fezwLZ">&#8220;I made this, it&#8217;s mine and I can earn money from it and if someone else copies it then that&#8217;s against the law.<!-- --></p>
<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fezwLZ">&#8220;But now they are saying everything we&#8217;ve ever created, all our artwork, we have to go back and opt out of it being used to train AI.&#8221;<!-- --></p>
<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fezwLZ">Ms McIntyre has written to Mel Stride, Conservative  MP for Central Devon, and asked for help.<!-- --></p>
<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fezwLZ">Stride said although AI presented &#8220;significant opportunities for innovation and economic growth&#8221; the government was &#8220;putting the creative industries at risk&#8221;.<!-- --></p>
<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fezwLZ">&#8220;Labour must press pause on its rushed consultation and rethink its approach to harness the benefits of AI without compromising the success of our creative industries,&#8221; he said.<!-- --></p>
</div>

<div data-component="text-block" class="sc-18fde0d6-0 dlWCEZ">
<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fezwLZ">Avenda Burnell Walsh from the Devon Artist Network said the group was also opposed to the plans.<!-- --></p>
<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fezwLZ">She said: &#8220;My car is parked on the road all night and day in the public domain but I wouldn&#8217;t expect to have to say to somebody legally you can&#8217;t have this car it&#8217;s mine. <!-- --></p>
<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fezwLZ">&#8220;You shouldn&#8217;t have to say that about your art either, should you?&#8221;<!-- --></p>
</div>
<div data-component="text-block" class="sc-18fde0d6-0 dlWCEZ">
<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fezwLZ">However some argue artists might benefit from the potential changes. <!-- --></p>
<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fezwLZ">Mike Phillips, a professor of interdisciplinary arts at the University of Plymouth, said AI could be used for artists to track down copyright abuses.<!-- --></p>
<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fezwLZ">He said: &#8220;It would be nice if some of the effort put into ripping stuff off was put into tracking stuff down. <!-- --></p>
<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fezwLZ">&#8220;That is something AI is good at, identifying things and recognising patterns in things and so maybe that would allow artists to use AI and seek the benefits from it.&#8221;<!-- --></p>

<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fezwLZ">It said the UK&#8217;s &#8220;current regime for copyright and AI is holding back the creative industries, media and AI sector from realising their full potential &#8211; and that cannot continue&#8221;.<!-- --></p>
