<p><img decoding="async" src="https://img.decrypt.co/insecure/rs:fill:1024:512:1:0/plain/https://cdn.decrypt.co/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/bitcoin-ethereum-red-down-market-gID_7.jpeg@png" /></p>
<div style="position: relative; overflow: visible; font-size: 1.2em; line-height: 1.58;">
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100">Bitcoin and other major cryptocurrencies are in the red Friday, with top coins falling to their lowest prices in at least a week as stock markets are similarly rattled by <a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://decrypt.co/312110/bitcoin-price-altcoins-inflation-february" target="_blank" rel="noopener">hotter-than-expected inflation data</a>.</p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100">The price of Bitcoin dipped to $83,609 early Friday afternoon, and is currently down about 4% on the day at a current price of $83,736. It&#8217;s down just 0.4% on the week, but the Friday slide has cooled rising momentum seen in recent days.</p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100">Other top coins have shown sharper losses over the past day, however, with Ethereum down 6% to $1,875 and XRP falling about 7% to $2.17. Both fell to weekly lows on Friday alongside Bitcoin.</p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100">Solana and Dogecoin are also in the red Friday, down 6% and 4.5% respectively over the past day, though both coins remain up over the last week.</p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100">Amid the declines, crypto liquidations have surged over the past day, tallying more than $450 million as of this writing per data from <a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://www.coinglass.com/LiquidationData" target="_blank" rel="nofollow external noopener">CoinGlass</a>. Long positions make up the bulk of the carnage at $402 million, and Ethereum is the biggest contributor to liquidations with $136 million worth in total. Bitcoin is next at $118 million.</p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100">Both the Dow and S&amp;P 500 indices are down more than 2% Friday, with traders reacting to continued inflation fears and the impact of Trump&#8217;s ever-evolving trade tariffs on other nations. The Personal Consumption Expenditures Price Index (PCE) <a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://decrypt.co/312110/bitcoin-price-altcoins-inflation-february" target="_blank" rel="noopener">rose 0.4% in February</a>, as reported Friday—a higher market than expected and the biggest monthly leap in more than a year.</p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100">&#8220;Today&#8217;s price action shows that investors are not ready for a long-term tariff engagement by the U.S.,&#8221; Mark Connors, chief investment strategist for Bitcoin investment advisory Risk Dimensions, told <i>Decrypt</i>. &#8220;Portfolio managers are taking risk off the table to fight another day, until the impact of tariffs have greater clarity.&#8221;</p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100">Nvidia&#8217;s stock is down about 1.5% on the day, while the Nvidia-backed cloud computing firm CoreWeave—which began trading Friday—is down by about 1% as of this writing.</p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100">But crypto stocks are taking much harder hits than the broader markets, with Strategy (MSTR) down about 10% on the day and Coinbase falling by nearly 7% as of this writing.</p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100">Connors also noted the Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta&#8217;s gloominess about GDP in the current quarter: &#8220;So today&#8217;s move validates what Atlanta Fed GDPNow forecast has versus what the Street has. That, to me, is the real signal out of today.&#8221;</p>
