<p><img decoding="async" src="https://img.decrypt.co/insecure/rs:fill:1024:512:1:0/plain/https://cdn.decrypt.co/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/FDIC-shutterstock_1431971627-16x9-1-gID_7.jpg@png" /></p>
<div style="position: relative; overflow: visible; font-size: 1.2em; line-height: 1.58;">
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">A redacted report detailing the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation’s internal communications about crypto-related banking activities has reignited calls for accountability in U.S. financial oversight. </span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Attorney John E. Deaton, a prominent crypto advocate and former Senate candidate, has urged Congress to investigate what he alleges is a coordinated effort by regulators to marginalize the digital asset sector through &#8220;Operation ChokePoint 2.0.&#8221;</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">The <a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://www.fdic.gov/foia/history-associates-inc-v-fdic-fdics-redacted-pause-letters-january-3-2025" target="_blank" rel="noopener">documents</a>, first released through a court order to publicly traded crypto exchange Coinbase in November, outline FDIC directives advising banks to pause crypto-related services while under review. </span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Critics, including Deaton, argue the directives reveal an orchestrated attempt to restrict access to essential financial infrastructure, potentially stifling lawful businesses within the burgeoning sector.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">“What we’ve learned thus far suggests coordinated, multi-agency action driven by political agendas rather than sound policy or law,” Deaton </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://x.com/JohnEDeaton1/status/1875619679114244416" target="_blank" rel="nofollow external noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">tweeted</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Saturday.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Deaton, who unsuccessfully </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://decrypt.co/247909/elizabeth-warren-face-pro-crypto-lawyer-john-deaton-senate-race" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">challenged</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Senator Elizabeth Warren in the 2024 Massachusetts </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://decrypt.co/290339/crypto-critic-elizabeth-warren-secures-fourth-term-as-massachusetts-senator" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Senate race</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> on a pro-crypto platform, described the implications of ChokePoint 2.0 as transcending the crypto industry. </span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">“This isn’t just a fight for crypto,” Deaton said. “It’s a fight against the erosion of institutional integrity and the unchecked power of unelected bureaucrats.”</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Warren, a vocal crypto critic, won reelection by a significant margin, maintaining her influence in shaping U.S. financial policy. Deaton, meanwhile, has continued his advocacy, pledging to lead an investigation into what he claims is regulatory overreach. </span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Actions like those outlined in the FDIC report could set a dangerous precedent by enabling agencies to stifle innovation and selectively enforce laws without proper oversight, Deaton asserts.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">The controversy echoes prior disputes involving Custodia Bank, which <a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://decrypt.co/250360/custodia-fed-attempt-crush-crypto-bank-political" target="_blank" rel="noopener">sued the Federal Reserve</a> after being denied a master account. </span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Deaton has labeled Custodia’s case as pivotal, warning it underscores the growing influence of regulatory bodies over private enterprises.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">The FDIC has defended its oversight practices, citing the need to evaluate risks tied to volatile markets. </span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">However, Deaton argues that emerging evidence suggests political motivations may underlie some regulatory actions rather than adherence to sound policy.</span></p>
