<p>A new rating on the ESRB website suggests that Resident Evil 6 may be coming to current-gen consoles.</p>
<p>A search on the US game ratings board’s website shows that one of its latest rating decisions is an M for <a target="_blank" rel="noopener" class="link-external" href="https://www.esrb.org/ratings/40367/resident-evil-6/">Resident Evil 6 on ‘Xbox Series’</a>.</p>
<p><a class="link-relationship" title="Games &gt; Resident Evil (series) [464 articles]" href="https://www.videogameschronicle.com/games/resident-evil-series/">Resident Evil</a> 6 was originally released on <a class="link-relationship" title="Platforms &gt; Xbox 360 [809 articles]" href="https://www.videogameschronicle.com/platforms/xbox/x360/">Xbox 360</a> and <a class="link-relationship" title="Platforms &gt; PlayStation 3 [970 articles]" href="https://www.videogameschronicle.com/platforms/playstation/ps3/">PS3</a> in October 2012, blending “action and survival horror” to deliver what <a class="link-relationship" title="Companies &gt; Capcom [882 articles]" href="https://www.videogameschronicle.com/companies/capcom/">Capcom</a> called “an epic and dramatic horror experience”.</p>
<p>The game was then ported to <a class="link-relationship" title="Platforms &gt; Xbox One [6,608 articles]" href="https://www.videogameschronicle.com/platforms/xbox/xb1/">Xbox One</a> and <a class="link-relationship" title="Platforms &gt; PlayStation 4 [7,956 articles]" href="https://www.videogameschronicle.com/platforms/playstation/ps4/">PS4</a> in March 2016, alongside ports of <a class="link-relationship" title="Games &gt; Resident Evil 4 (2023) [189 articles]" href="https://www.videogameschronicle.com/games/resident-evil-series/resident-evil-4/">Resident Evil 4</a> and 5, with graphical enhancements and all previous add-on DLC included.</p>
<p>Capcom then brought Resident Evil 5 and 6 to the <a class="link-relationship" title="Platforms &gt; Nintendo Switch [4,953 articles]" href="https://www.videogameschronicle.com/platforms/nintendo/switch/">Switch</a> in 2019, again offering all previously released DLC.</p>
<p>The rating of a new version suggests Capcom may be about to re-release a new current gen version of Resident Evil 6, perhaps with further graphical enhancements to take advantage of the current hardware’s extra power.</p>
<p>The fact the game has already been rated also makes it all but certain it will be a remaster, rather than the full remake <a class="link-relationship" title="Games &gt; Resident Evil 2 (2019) [166 articles]" href="https://www.videogameschronicle.com/games/resident-evil-series/resident-evil-2-2019/">Resident Evil 2</a>, 3 and 4 received in recent years.</p>

<p>What isn’t yet clear is whether a port of Resident Evil 5 – which was also released on Xbox One and PS4 in 2016, followed by Switch in 2019 – will be joining Resident Evil 6 and also be getting the current-gen remaster treatment.</p>
<p>Given that the ESRB doesn’t yet have a rating for a <a class="link-relationship" title="Platforms &gt; PlayStation 5 [7,244 articles]" href="https://www.videogameschronicle.com/platforms/playstation/ps5/">PS5</a> version of Resident Evil 6 at the time of writing, however – and given that an <a class="link-relationship" title="Platforms &gt; Xbox Series X | S [5,885 articles]" href="https://www.videogameschronicle.com/platforms/xbox/seriesxs/">Xbox Series X/S</a> release will almost certainly be accompanied by a PS5 one – it appears that the board is still in the process of rating whatever it is Capcom has submitted.</p>
