<p>The U.S. may have led China in the artificial intelligence race for the past decade, according to Alexandr Wang, CEO of Scale AI, but on Christmas Day, everything changed.</p>
<p>Wang, whose company provides training data to key AI players including OpenAI, <span class="QuoteInBody-quoteNameContainer" data-test="QuoteInBody" id="RegularArticle-QuoteInBody-1"><a href="https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/GOOG/">Google</a><span class="QuoteInBody-inlineButton"><span class="AddToWatchlistButton-watchlistContainer" id="-WatchlistDropdown" data-analytics-id="-WatchlistDropdown"><button class="AddToWatchlistButton-watchlistButton" aria-label="Add To Watchlist" data-testid="dropdown-btn"><span class="AddToWatchlistButton-addWatchListFromTag"/></button></span></span></span> and <span class="QuoteInBody-quoteNameContainer" data-test="QuoteInBody" id="RegularArticle-QuoteInBody-2"><a href="https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/META/">Meta</a><span class="QuoteInBody-inlineButton"><span class="AddToWatchlistButton-watchlistContainer" id="-WatchlistDropdown" data-analytics-id="-WatchlistDropdown"><button class="AddToWatchlistButton-watchlistButton" aria-label="Add To Watchlist" data-testid="dropdown-btn"><span class="AddToWatchlistButton-addWatchListFromTag"/></button></span></span></span>, said Thursday at the World Economic Forum in Davos, Switzerland, that DeepSeek, the leading Chinese AI lab, released an &#8220;earth-shattering model&#8221; on Christmas Day, then followed it up with a powerful reasoning-focused AI model, DeepSeek-R1, which competes with OpenAI&#8217;s recently released o1 model.</p>
<p>&#8220;What we&#8217;ve found is that DeepSeek &#8230; is the top performing, or roughly on par with the best American models,&#8221; Wang said.</p>
<p>In an interview with CNBC, Wang described the artificial intelligence race between the U.S. and China as an &#8220;AI war,&#8221; adding that he believes China has significantly more <span class="QuoteInBody-quoteNameContainer" data-test="QuoteInBody" id="RegularArticle-QuoteInBody-3"><a href="https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/NVDA/">Nvidia</a><span class="QuoteInBody-inlineButton"><span class="AddToWatchlistButton-watchlistContainer" id="-WatchlistDropdown" data-analytics-id="-WatchlistDropdown"><button class="AddToWatchlistButton-watchlistButton" aria-label="Add To Watchlist" data-testid="dropdown-btn"><span class="AddToWatchlistButton-addWatchListFromTag"/></button></span></span></span> H100 GPUs — AI chips that are widely used to build leading powerful AI models — than people may think, especially considering U.S. export controls.</p>
<p>Wang also said he believes the AI sector will reach a trillion dollars, on par with estimates that the generative AI market is poised to<a href="https://www.bloomberg.com/professional/insights/data/generative-ai-races-toward-1-3-trillion-in-revenue-by-2032/#:~:text=Generative%20AI%20is%20poised%20to,our%20proprietary%20market%2Dsizing%20model." target="_blank"> top $1 trillion</a> in revenue within a decade.</p>

<p>&#8220;The United States is going to need a huge amount of computational capacity, a huge amount of infrastructure,&#8221; Wang said, later adding, &#8220;We need to unleash U.S. energy to enable this AI boom.&#8221;</p>
<p>Earlier this week, President <a href="https://www.cnbc.com/donald-trump/">Donald Trump</a> announced a <a href="https://www.cnbc.com/2025/01/21/trump-ai-openai-oracle-softbank.html">joint venture</a> with <a href="https://www.cnbc.com/2024/05/14/openai-cnbc-disruptor-50.html">OpenAI</a>, <a href="https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/ORCL/">Oracle</a> and <a href="https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/9984.T-JP/">SoftBank</a> to invest billions of dollars in U.S. AI infrastructure. The project, Stargate, was unveiled at the White House by Trump, SoftBank CEO Masayoshi Son, Oracle co-founder Larry Ellison and OpenAI CEO Sam Altman. Key initial technology partners will include <a href="https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/MSFT/">Microsoft</a>, <a href="https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/NVDA/">Nvidia</a> and Oracle, as well as semiconductor company <a href="https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/ARM/">Arm</a>. They said they would invest $100 billion to start and up to $500 billion over the next four years.</p>
<p>In the interview Thursday, Wang said he believes that it&#8217;ll take two to four years to reach artificial general intelligence, or AGI, a widely cited but vaguely defined benchmark used in the AI sector to denote a branch of AI pursuing technology that equals or surpasses human intellect on a wide range of tasks. AGI is a hotly debated topic, with some leaders saying we&#8217;re close to attaining it and some saying it&#8217;s not possible at all. Wang said his own definition of AGI is &#8220;powerful AI systems that are able to use a computer just like you or I could &#8230; and basically be a remote worker in the most capable way.&#8221;</p>
<p>Anthropic, the <span class="QuoteInBody-quoteNameContainer" data-test="QuoteInBody" id="RegularArticle-QuoteInBody-13"><a href="https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/AMZN/">Amazon</a><span class="QuoteInBody-inlineButton"><span class="AddToWatchlistButton-watchlistContainer" id="-WatchlistDropdown" data-analytics-id="-WatchlistDropdown"><button class="AddToWatchlistButton-watchlistButton" aria-label="Add To Watchlist" data-testid="dropdown-btn"><span class="AddToWatchlistButton-addWatchListFromTag"/></button></span></span></span>-backed AI startup founded by ex-OpenAI research executives, ramped up its technology development throughout the past year, and in October, the startup <a href="https://www.cnbc.com/2024/10/22/anthropic-announces-ai-agents-for-complex-tasks-racing-openai.html">said</a> that its AI agents were able to use computers like humans can to complete complex tasks. Anthropic&#8217;s Computer Use capability allows its technology to interpret what&#8217;s on a computer screen, select buttons, enter text, navigate websites and execute tasks through any software and real-time internet browsing, the startup said.</p>
<p>The tool can &#8220;use computers in basically the same way that we do,&#8221; Jared Kaplan, Anthropic&#8217;s chief science officer, told CNBC in an interview at the time. He said it can do tasks with &#8220;tens or even hundreds of steps.&#8221;</p>
<p>OpenAI <a href="https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-11-13/openai-nears-launch-of-ai-agents-to-automate-tasks-for-users?embedded-checkout=true" target="_blank">reportedly plans</a> to introduce a similar feature soon.</p>
<p>When asked which U.S. artificial intelligence startups are leading the AI race right now, Wang said that models each have their own strengths — for instance, OpenAI&#8217;s models are great at reasoning, while Anthropic&#8217;s are great at coding.</p>
<p>&#8220;The space is becoming more competitive, not less competitive,&#8221; he said.</p>
