Few among us don’t recognize the bushy mustache and red and blue outfit that Mario dons. The dude’s an icon in every possible medium at this point, and whether you’re playing the latest game or just enjoying the movie, there’s fun to be had. MAR10 Day is nearly upon us, as an unofficial celebration of Mario on March 10, but there are already some great deals that can let you save money on some great games and more. In fact, Nintendo has announced discounts, preorder deals and retail partnerships to help you honor Mario. Keep checking back as we get closer to the big day, as we’ll continue updating this page with the best deals.

Free MAR10 Day trials, sweepstakes and perks
