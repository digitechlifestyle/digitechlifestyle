<h2 id="galaxy-s25-ultra-will-it-look-different-2">Galaxy S25 Ultra: Will it look different?</h2>
<p>If you&#8217;re familiar with what Samsung&#8217;s last couple of Ultra models look like, you probably have a good idea of the Galaxy S25 Ultra&#8217;s basic appearance. Samsung isn&#8217;t planning a design overhaul from what we&#8217;ve heard, though there are some changes compared to the previous year&#8217;s models.</p>
<p>A <a data-analytics-id="inline-link" href="https://x.com/UniverseIce/status/1877960935002009790" target="_blank">tweet by leaker Ice Universe</a> that shows a Galaxy S25 Ultra render next to its two predecessors underscores a pair of modest yet noticeable changes. For starters, the corners on the new phone look much more rounded. For another, the bezel around the Galaxy S25 Ultra&#8217;s screen is a little bit thinner. That could squeeze in some more useable screen space on the new Ultra, which Samsung may be trying to do in light of the iPhone 16 Pro Max&#8217;s larger screen.</p>

<p>Of all the rumored products likely to join the Galaxy S25 at Unpacked on Wednesday, the most likely choice would seemingly be <a data-analytics-id="inline-link" href="https://www.tomsguide.com/computing/vr-ar/samsung-headset-confirmed-as-google-unveils-new-android-xr-platform" data-before-rewrite-localise="https://www.tomsguide.com/computing/vr-ar/samsung-headset-confirmed-as-google-unveils-new-android-xr-platform">Project Moohan</a>, the mixed reality headset Samsung confirmed the existence of last month. <p>Usually for a Galaxy S launch, we don&#8217;t have the luxury of knowing how the chipset powering the new phones might perform. But that&#8217;s not the case with the Galaxy S25 and the <a data-analytics-id="inline-link" href="https://www.tomsguide.com/phones/android-phones/snapdragon-8-elite" data-before-rewrite-localise="https://www.tomsguide.com/phones/android-phones/snapdragon-8-elite">Snapdragon 8 Elite</a> silicon expected to be included in each model.</p>
<p>It&#8217;s not just the fact that <a data-analytics-id="inline-link" href="https://www.tomsguide.com/phones/android-phones/snapdragon-8-elite-benchmarks" data-before-rewrite-localise="https://www.tomsguide.com/phones/android-phones/snapdragon-8-elite-benchmarks">we&#8217;ve benchmarked Qualcomm&#8217;s new chip</a> in a reference device shortly after it was released — though we did. We&#8217;ve also had the chance to test it in two already announced phones — the <a data-analytics-id="inline-link" href="https://www.tomsguide.com/phones/android-phones/asus-rog-phone-9-pro-review-gaming-pro-and-battery-life-legend" data-before-rewrite-localise="https://www.tomsguide.com/phones/android-phones/asus-rog-phone-9-pro-review-gaming-pro-and-battery-life-legend">Asus ROG Phone 9 Pro</a> and the <a data-analytics-id="inline-link" href="https://www.tomsguide.com/phones/oneplus-phones/oneplus-13-review" data-before-rewrite-localise="https://www.tomsguide.com/phones/oneplus-phones/oneplus-13-review">OnePlus 13</a>. Both devices launched ahead of the new Galaxy S flagships and both offer Snapdragon 8 Elite silicon.</p>
<p>The ROG Phone 9 Pro actually turned in faster times on the Geekbench CPU test than the OnePlus 13 did, though you&#8217;d figure that would be the case for Asus&#8217; gaming-focused phone. Multicore scores were well ahead of what the A18 Pro in the <a data-analytics-id="inline-link" href="https://www.tomsguide.com/phones/iphones/apple-iphone-16-pro-max-review" data-before-rewrite-localise="https://www.tomsguide.com/phones/iphones/apple-iphone-16-pro-max-review">iPhone 16 Pro Max</a> posted, though Apple&#8217;s chip has the better single-core results on Geekbench. Graphics numbers for the new Qualcomm chip also set a new standard.</p>
<p>We don&#8217;t know whether Samsung will optimize the Snapdragon 8 Elite for performance like the ROG Phone 9 Pro or go for an approach similar to the OnePlus 13&#8217;s, but that should give you some idea of the performance the new phones will bring to the table ahead of their launch.</p>
