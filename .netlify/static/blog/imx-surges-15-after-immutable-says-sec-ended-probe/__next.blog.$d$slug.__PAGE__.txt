<p>The token tied to the crypto gaming giant Immutable surged 15% in the hours after it announced that the US Securities and Exchange Commission closed its investigation into the firm and would take no further action.</p>
<p>The Immutable (<a title="null" href="https://cointelegraph.com/immutable-price-index" target="null" rel="null" data-ct-non-breakable="null">IMX</a>) token rose around 15% on March 25 to reach just under $0.74 shortly after the firm announced that the SEC shut its inquiry <a title="null" href="https://cointelegraph.com/news/sec-closes-immutable-investigation-five-months-after-wells-notice" target="null" rel="null" data-ct-non-breakable="null">without any breach of violations, which Immutable said closed</a> “the loop on the Wells notice issued by the SEC last year.”</p>
<h2>IMX matched crypto market downtrend</h2>
<p>It is the highest price that IMX has reached since March 3, before a broader market decline — driven by prolonged uncertainty over US President Donald Trump’s tariffs and US interest rates — pushed it down to $0.46 on March 11.</p>
<p>At the time of publication, IMX had retraced back to $0.67, <a title="null" href="https://coinmarketcap.com/currencies/immutable-x/" target="null" rel="null" data-ct-non-breakable="null">according</a> to CoinMarketCap. A move back toward $0.70 would wipe approximately $449,500 in short positions, <a title="null" href="https://www.coinglass.com/pro/futures/LiquidationMap" target="null" rel="null" data-ct-non-breakable="null">according</a> to CoinGlass data.</p>
<figure><img decoding="async" title="" src="https://s3.cointelegraph.com/uploads/2025-03/0195d0fd-7f2b-7123-a30c-69c541530027" alt="Cryptocurrencies, Markets" /><figcaption style="text-align: center;"><em>IMX is up 0.34% over the past 30 days. Source: </em><a title="https://coinmarketcap.com/currencies/immutable-x/" href="https://coinmarketcap.com/currencies/immutable-x/" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener" data-ct-non-breakable="null"><em>CoinMarketCap</em></a></figcaption></figure>
<p>While the token price surged on the positive news, it barely moved when Immutable announced in November it had been issued a Wells notice. However, the broader market was already gaining momentum as Trump’s odds to win the election looked strong in the days before his eventual win on Nov. 5.</p>
<p>Immutable co-founder Robbie Ferguson <a title="null" href="https://x.com/0xferg/status/1904620128052142208" target="null" rel="null" data-ct-non-breakable="null">said</a> in a March 25 X post that the SEC’s dropped investigation was “an enormous win for Web3 gaming.”</p>
<p>“After a year of fighting, this threat to digital ownership rights has finally been put to rest,” Ferguson said.</p>
<p><em><strong>Related: </strong></em><a title="null" href="https://cointelegraph.com/news/crypto-influencer-ben-bitboy-armstrong-arrested-in-florida" target="null" rel="null" data-ct-non-breakable="null"><em><strong>Crypto influencer Ben ‘Bitboy’ Armstrong arrested in Florida</strong></em></a></p>
<p>Among the top gaming crypto tokens by market cap, several have seen an upswing over the past 24 hours. Gala (<a title="null" href="https://cointelegraph.com/gala-price-index" target="null" rel="null" data-ct-non-breakable="null">GALA</a>) is up 2.78%, The Sandbox (<a title="null" href="https://cointelegraph.com/the-sandbox-sand-price-index" target="null" rel="null" data-ct-non-breakable="null">SAND</a>) is up 3.78%, FLOKI (FLOKI) is up 1.91%, and Axie Infinity (<a title="null" href="https://cointelegraph.com/axie-infinity-axs-price-index" target="null" rel="null" data-ct-non-breakable="null">AXS</a>) is up 1.50%.</p>
<p>IMX hit its all-time high of $9.32 in November 2021 during a major rally in gaming tokens. There’s been speculation about when gaming <a title="null" href="https://cointelegraph.com/magazine/web3-crypto-gaming-tokens-chiliz-farm-tap-review-web3-gamer/" target="null" rel="null" data-ct-non-breakable="null">tokens will experience another significant</a> uptrend, as they’ve historically surged after the broader crypto market moves first.</p>
<p>However, over the past 30 days, the total market cap of gaming tokens has dropped 3.65% to $13.13 billion, while trading volume has taken a bigger hit, falling 33.45% to $1.75 billion.</p>
