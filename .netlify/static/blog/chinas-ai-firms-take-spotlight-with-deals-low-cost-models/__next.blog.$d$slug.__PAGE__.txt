<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->BEIJING (Reuters) &#8211; Alibaba&#8217;s announcement this week that it will partner with Apple to support iPhones&#8217; artificial intelligence services offering in China has again thrust the spotlight on the country&#8217;s AI industry on the heels of DeepSeek.<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->Following are some of the prominent AI services and their developers to watch in China.<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->DOUBAO<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->Developed by ByteDance, a latecomer to the large language model (LLM) market, the namesake chatbot is the most popular consumer AI app in China.<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->The company&#8217;s latest LLM, Doubao-1.5-pro, was released in January and outperforms ChatGPT&#8217;s GPT-4 in some metrics.<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->ByteDance is among the leaders in developing cost-effective LLMs, and its offering is the cheapest available in the market, even more affordable than DeepSeek, according to a Barclays report.<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->Doubao-1.5 was priced at only 3% to 4% of GPT-4&#8217;s pricing.<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->ByteDance&#8217;s low-cost advantage is attributed to its use of the &#8220;mixture of experts&#8221; (MoE) framework, which is common among various AI models in China, including those of DeepSeek.<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->QWEN<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->Developed by e-commerce giant Alibaba, Qwen is the company&#8217;s flagship LLM and chatbot.<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->It was released in March 2023 and has been rated among the top-tier in global benchmarks.<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->In January, Alibaba releasedQwen 2.5-Max and claimed its functionality surpassed that of highly-acclaimed DeepSeek-V3.<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->A strong advocate of open AI development, Alibaba announced in September that many of its AI models would be made open-source.<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->ERNIE<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->Chinese search giant Baidu was among the first in China to launch a ChatGPT equivalent, Ernie Bot.<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->Baidu claims its latest model, Ernie 4.0, matches the capabilities of GPT-4.<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->On February 14, Baidu announced a plan to make its next-generation LLM, Ernie 4.5, open-source from June 30, a major shift in strategy as competition heats up.<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->HUNYUAN<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->Tencent released its flagship model Hunyuan in September and launched its chatbot, Yuanbao, in May 2024.<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->The company&#8217;s massive user base, acquired through its popular consumer apps like WeChat, gives Tencent a significant potential advantage in pushing out consumer applications in the future.<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->Tencent also offers open-source models and advocates for open-source development. Another advantage of Hunyuan is its focus on multi-modality, which includes text, image, and video generation capabilities.<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->GLM<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->Zhipu AI developed GLM and claims its latest LLM, GLM4, outperforms GPT-4 in some metrics.<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->Initially born out of a Tsinghua University laboratory, Zhipu counts Alibaba, Tencent, and the state-owned fund Zhongguancun Science City Innovation Development among its backers.<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p><button class="secondary-btn fin-size-large readmore-button rounded   yf-15mk0m" data-ylk="elm:readmore;itc:1;sec:content-canvas;slk:Story%20Continues" aria-label="Story Continues" title="Story Continues"> <span>Story Continues</span> </button> </p>
<div class="read-more-wrapper" style="display: none" data-testid="read-more">
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->In January, the U.S. Commerce Department placed Zhipu on an export control entity list.<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->KIMI<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->It was developed by Moonshot, which is backed by the likes of Alibaba, Tencent, and Hongshan.<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->Its chatbot Kimi is known for its long-context processing technology and the company claims the model supports two million input tokens, or units of words, for each query.<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->On January 20, just two days before DeepSeek released the R1 model, Moonshot unveiled its own reasoning model, Kimi 1.5.<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->MINIMAX<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->Minimax is a pioneer among Chinese firms in researching the MoE framework and it open-sourced its most advanced model, MiniMax-01, in January.<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->MiniMax was founded in December 2021, well before the AI craze sparked by the launch of OpenAI&#8217;s ChatGPT. It is backed by Alibaba and other prominent investors, including Hongshan, Gaorong Capital, and IDG.<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->In addition to LLMs, MiniMax has gained recognition for its social consumer apps, such as Takie and Xingye.<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->01.AI<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->Similar to DeepSeek, 01.AI has adopted an open-source approach and pioneered the MoE framework.<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->The company claims that its models are trained with fewer resources and have a cost-effective advantage, achieving the lowest cost level of LLM inference in the industry, 1/40 less than GPT-4&#8217;s list price.<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->Its founder Kai-Fu Lee, a former head of Google China, said recently in a written interview with Reuters that 01.AI has formed a joint partnership with Alibaba to create a lab focused on continuing the development of LLM technologies.<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->The company is backed by companies like Alibaba and Sinovation Ventures.<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->BAICHUAN<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->Baichuan was founded in April 2023 by a team of former executives from the search engine Sogou, including founder and CEO Wang Xiaochuan, who previously served as the CEO of Sogou.<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
<p class="yf-1pe5jgt"><!-- HTML_TAG_START -->The company gained recognition in June 2023 by becoming the first in China to launch an open-source model.<!-- HTML_TAG_END --></p>
