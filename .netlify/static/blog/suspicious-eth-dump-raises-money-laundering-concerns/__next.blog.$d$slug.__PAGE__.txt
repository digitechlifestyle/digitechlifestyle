<h3>Spot On Chain Reports Anomalies</h3>
<p>Spot On Chain’s forensic analysis revealed that the funds were moved through newly created wallets. These &#8220;virgin&#8221; wallets had no prior activity, which is unusual for such large-scale <strong>transactions</strong>. The firm traced the funds to cross-chain bridges, raising further concerns.</p>
<p>One of the key red flags was the protocol-level origin of the funds. This pattern has been associated with illicit activities in the past. The <strong>analytics firm</strong> emphasized that such &#8220;fresh&#8221; transactions are often more suspicious than those involving mixed coins.</p>
<h3>Fresh Wallets and Unusual Patterns</h3>

<p>This event has drawn comparisons to the Lazarus Group’s $300 million laundering spree. Both cases involved the use of fresh wallets and cross-chain bridges. The similarities have heightened concerns within the <strong>crypto</strong> community.</p>
