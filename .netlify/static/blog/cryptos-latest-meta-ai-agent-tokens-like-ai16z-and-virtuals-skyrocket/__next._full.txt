<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://decrypt.co/288357/ai-agents-meme-coins-ianalysts" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">AI agents</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, associated meme coins, and the protocols used to create these agents are among the top five best performing crypto assets of the day, according to </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://www.coingecko.com/en/categories" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">CoinGecko</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">. The recent surge is driving speculation that AI will be the latest “meta” for crypto traders to latch onto, though some traders and analysts aren’t yet convinced.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">As a result, conversations have broken out across the industry about the role of </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://decrypt.co/resources/a-brief-history-of-artificial-intelligence-ai-from-turing-to-iot" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">artificial intelligence</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> in the world of crypto. Some traders believe it&#8217;s the start of the </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://x.com/djenn/status/1874860338979467660" target="_blank" rel="nofollow external noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">next crypto revolution</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, providing a fresh narrative to fuel potentially explosive gains—while others think it’s just another bubble waiting to pop.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Put simply, AI agents are models set out to perform specific tasks—such as </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://x.com/aixbt_agent" target="_blank" rel="nofollow external noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Aixbt</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, which analyzes projects—or simply have a quirky personality that viewers enjoy. Some agents are tied to a crypto token, either as a way to fundraise or to promote and brand itself.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Others have simply shilled meme coins. For example, </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://decrypt.co/289041/terminal-of-truths-developer-moves-all-his-goat-tokens-after-x-account-hack-nets-600000" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Truth Terminal</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">—the agent that started much of this wave—has promoted Goatseus Maximus (</span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://dexscreener.com/solana/9tb2ohu5p16bpbarqd3n27wnkf51ukfs8z1gzzldxvzw" target="_blank" rel="nofollow external noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">GOAT</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">) and Fartcoin (</span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/fartcoin" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">FARTCOIN</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">) causing both to skyrocket. Fartcoin <a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://decrypt.co/299328/solana-xrp-surge-dogecoin-bitcoin-weekly-highs" target="_blank" rel="noopener">surged to a new all-time high price</a> early Thursday.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Over the past 24 hours, </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://www.coingecko.com/en/categories/ai-agents" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">AI agent</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> tokens are up 18%, </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://www.coingecko.com/en/categories/ai-meme-coins" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">AI meme coins</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> jumped by 20%, and </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://www.coingecko.com/en/categories/ai-agent-launchpad" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">agent launchpad tokens</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> have collectively risen by over 21% as the best-performing category tracked by CoinGecko.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Of these tokens, </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/ai16z" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">AI16z</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> is one of the biggest winners of the week, rising 176% to a market cap over $2.2 billion—the third-largest of any AI-related token. This comes following Binance’s </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://x.com/BinanceFutures/status/1874776792462229971" target="_blank" rel="nofollow external noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">announcement</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> that it would launch perpetual futures contracts trading for ai16z, alongside AI agent musician </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://decrypt.co/price/zerebro" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Zerebro</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> and agent launchpad </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://x.com/griffaindotcom" target="_blank" rel="nofollow external noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Griffain</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">With this success, conversations about the actual purpose of these tokens have grown. One major gripe is that many agents are simply becoming non-human Twitter influencers that are, at times, </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://x.com/shawmakesmagic/status/1874733065249157334" target="_blank" rel="nofollow external noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">annoying</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> and </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://x.com/mdudas/status/1874441100783706329" target="_blank" rel="nofollow external noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">inconsistent</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">. </span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">“Chatbots can indeed replace a lot of influencers because chatbots never sleep, they&#8217;re always on-message, and they’re less greedy than human influencers,” Haseeb Qureshi, managing partner at venture fund <a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://www.dragonfly.xyz/" target="_blank" rel="nofollow external noopener">Dragonfly</a>, said on </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://x.com/hosseeb/status/1874288532686295058" target="_blank" rel="nofollow external noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">X</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">. “Plus, the majority of influencers aren’t very original anyway.”</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Other traders are </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://x.com/condzxyz/status/1874865470295470552" target="_blank" rel="nofollow external noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">concerned</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> that of the thousands of projects launching claiming to be the next big AI agent, a large portion eventually reveal themselves to </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://x.com/apixtwts/status/1874812640289305009" target="_blank" rel="nofollow external noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">not be as advertised</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">The </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://decrypt.co/294137/virtuals-protocol-tokens-skyrocket-as-ai-agent-demand-grows" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Virtuals Protocol</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, launched on </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://decrypt.co/resources/what-is-ethereum-quickly-explained-four-minute-guide" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Ethereum</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> layer-2 network </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://decrypt.co/resources/what-is-base-coinbases-layer-2-chain" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Base</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, has looked to solve this problem by becoming the place to build and launch tokenized AI agents. As such, the project’s associated token </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/virtual-protocol" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">VIRTUAL</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> has become the largest AI crypto token on the market thanks to explosive growth—up almost 35,000% over the last year.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Still, most </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://x.com/hosseeb/status/1874288532686295058" target="_blank" rel="nofollow external noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">agent autonomy is limited</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">. Truth Terminal, for example, still has creator </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://x.com/AndyAyrey" target="_blank" rel="nofollow external noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Andy Ayrey</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> looking over its shoulder before its thoughts and decisions go out to the public. But this level of oversight might not be a bad thing at this point, as </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://decrypt.co/293420/truth-terminal-integrates-other-ai-models" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Ayrey told </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Decrypt</span></i></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> that he had to prevent Truth Terminal from attempting to incite a riot during election season in the U.S.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Granting agents more autonomy is a long-term goal that many developers appear to be working towards. As such, the development and continued evolution of AI will likely dictate the growth of its intersection with crypto, with some traders admitting current agents are just “</span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://x.com/crypto_condom/status/1874837670960238602" target="_blank" rel="nofollow external noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">glorified chatbots</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">” that </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://x.com/blocknewsdotcom/status/1871331452148645945" target="_blank" rel="nofollow external noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">don’t need tokens</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> associated with them. </span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">“Right now these chatbots are fascinating to us because they are so novel. It’s like seeing an elephant paint,” Qureshi explained. “The first time you see it, you don’t really care that the painting is not very good—it’s spectacular to see. But the 1,000th time, the novelty wears thin.”</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">But as the AI agent buzz rages on, dominating the crypto industry’s </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://x.com/0xNairolf/status/1874729352660942958" target="_blank" rel="nofollow external noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">mindshare</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, market participants are speculating on what an agent offering lending via its wallet or </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://x.com/Loopifyyy/status/1874577229424046399" target="_blank" rel="nofollow external noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">autonomous on-chain transactions</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> could mean for the market.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">On the other hand, Truth Terminal—</span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://x.com/AndyAyrey/status/1874682488770953241" target="_blank" rel="nofollow external noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">according to Ayrey</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">—appears to have used its AI prowess to dream up a branded line of butt plugs.
