<p>HOUSTON–(BUSINESS WIRE)–<a class="keyword-link" href="https://boereport.com/tag/conocophillips/?utm_campaign=kw-link-name">ConocoPhillips</a> (<a class="ticker-link" href="https://boereport.com/ticker/NYSE:COP">NYSE: COP</a>) (the “Company”) announced today that the Company has received exemptive relief from the applicable Canadian securities regulatory authorities regarding future filing requirements. The Company became a reporting issuer for Canadian securities law purposes by virtue of the acquisition of <a class="keyword-link" href="https://boereport.com/tag/marathon-oil/?utm_campaign=kw-link-name">Marathon Oil Corporation</a> in November 2024, which made the Company subject to Canadian continuous disclosure and other reporting obligations under applicable Canadian securities laws.</p>
<p>Applicable Canadian securities regulatory authorities have issued a decision document (the “Decision”) granting the Company exemptive relief from the requirements contained in Canada’s National Instrument 51-101 <i>Standards of Disclosure for Oil and Gas Activities</i> (“NI 51-101”). As a result of the Decision, and provided that certain conditions set out in the Decision are met on an ongoing basis, the Company will not be required to comply with the Canadian requirements of NI 51-101 and the Canadian Oil and Gas Evaluation Handbook and, accordingly, will not be required to file Form 51-101F1 <i>Statement of Reserves Data and Other Oil and Gas Information</i> or related forms and disclosure as part of its annual filings. In lieu of such filings, the Decision permits the Company to provide disclosure in respect of its oil and gas activities in the form permitted by, and in accordance with, the legal requirements imposed by the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (“SEC”), the Securities Act of 1933, the Securities and Exchange Act of 1934, the Sarbanes-Oxley Act of 2002 and the rules of the New York Stock Exchange. The Decision also provides that the Company is required to file all such oil and gas disclosures with the Canadian securities regulatory authorities on <i><a href="https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&amp;url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.sedarplus.ca&amp;esheet=54206696&amp;newsitemid=20250213111376&amp;lan=en-US&amp;anchor=www.sedarplus.ca&amp;index=1&amp;md5=77c1616df16a61ab1ca4e14194279476" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener">www.sedarplus.ca</a></i> as soon as practicable after such disclosure is filed with the SEC.</p>
<p>A copy of the Decision may be found on the website of the Alberta Securities Commission at <i><a href="https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&amp;url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.asc.ca&amp;esheet=54206696&amp;newsitemid=20250213111376&amp;lan=en-US&amp;anchor=www.asc.ca&amp;index=2&amp;md5=0d24ccfb09590794f19e5a778a4bf12d" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener">www.asc.ca</a></i>.</p>

<p><b>About ConocoPhillips</b></p>
<p>ConocoPhillips is one of the world’s leading exploration and production companies based on both production and reserves, with a globally diversified asset portfolio. Headquartered in Houston, Texas, ConocoPhillips had operations and activities in 14 countries, $123 billion of total assets, and approximately 11,800 employees at Dec. 31, 2024.</p>
