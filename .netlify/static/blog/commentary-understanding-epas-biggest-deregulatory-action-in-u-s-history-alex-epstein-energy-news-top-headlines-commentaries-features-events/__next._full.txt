<h2><strong>18 important deregulatory actions EPA announced yesterday, and why they will make life better for all of us.</strong></h2>
<p><strong>By Alex Epstein</strong>
</p>
<p><strong>Did the EPA really just take the “Biggest Deregulatory Action in U.S. History”?</strong>
</p>
<p><strong>Arguably, yes.</strong>
</p>
<p><strong>Here are 18 important deregulatory actions EPA announced today, and why they will make life better for all of us. </strong>(I posted a version of these <a href="https://substack.com/redirect/3737464b-2f6e-46e0-9482-cb10be8aab14?j=eyJ1IjoiMTJ1cXZ0In0.rxDX6jpMgrIJ6DYyZnDHuIjpS1jWv2bcUWUGMdkuaiw" target="_blank" rel="noopener" data-saferedirecturl="https://www.google.com/url?q=https://substack.com/redirect/3737464b-2f6e-46e0-9482-cb10be8aab14?j%3DeyJ1IjoiMTJ1cXZ0In0.rxDX6jpMgrIJ6DYyZnDHuIjpS1jWv2bcUWUGMdkuaiw&amp;source=gmail&amp;ust=1741969992130000&amp;usg=AOvVaw0HzzZTp_-G2FVkHdCw1eNq">points on Twitter/X</a> yesterday; so far it has over a million views, and has been shared publicly by EPA Administrator Lee Zeldin.)
</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>“Reconsideration of regulations on power plants (Clean Power Plan 2.0)”</strong>These Biden regulations would effectively ban all coal plants and new natural gas plants by demanding impossible 90% carbon capture.¹Reconsidering them is essential to preserving the grid and unleashing electricity.</li>
<li><strong>“Reconsideration of light-duty, medium-duty, and heavy-duty vehicle regulations”</strong>These Biden regulations paved the way for the EV mandate by imposing unachievable emission standards on gas vehicles.²Reconsidering them is essential for preserving automotive choice.</li>
<li><strong>“Reconsideration of the 2009 Endangerment Finding”</strong>This Obama-era determination, by declaring CO2 dangerous, allowed Biden to shut down power plants, slow US oil growth, and lock up our limitless natural gas.³Reconsidering it is essential for American energy dominance.</li>
<li><strong>“Ending so-called ‘Good Neighbor Plan’”</strong>This Biden rule set ozone-related emissions levels so (unnecessarily) low that many vital coal power plants can’t comply—even though ozone levels are very low throughout the US.⁴Ending it is essential to preserving grid reliability.</li>
<li><strong>“Reconsideration of Particulate Matter National Ambient Air Quality Standards”</strong>These Biden regulations demand hugely costly cuts to US PM2.5 levels, which are already very low and safe.⁵Reconsidering them is crucial to keeping American energy and industry globally competitive.</li>
<li><strong>“Reconsideration of limitations, guidelines and standards (ELG) for the Steam Electric Power Generating Industry”</strong>These Biden regulations throttle coal power generation by imposing needlessly stringent wastewater standards.⁶Reconsidering them is important to preserving our grid.</li>
<li><strong>“Reconsideration of Mercury and Air Toxics Standards”</strong>These Biden regulations impose draconian mercury and other emission limits on coal plants, requiring costly retrofits or shutdowns with little or no health benefit.⁷Reconsidering them is essential to preserving grid reliability.</li>
<li><strong>“Restructuring the Regional Haze Program”</strong>This EPA program imposes major costs on electricity producers for minimal visibility improvements in low-populated areas.⁸Restructuring it is crucial for facilitating affordable and reliable electricity.</li>
<li><strong>“Prioritizing coal ash program to expedite state permit reviews and update coal ash regulations (CCR Rule)”</strong>These Biden regulations impose an impossible compliance timeline for coal ash disposal.⁹Fixing them is crucial will preserve the grid while responsibly managing coal ash.</li>
<li><strong>“Overhauling Biden-Harris Administration’s ‘Social Cost of Carbon’”</strong>This Biden method of calculating the costs of CO2 emissions allows EPA to vastly exaggerate the benefits of its regulations.¹⁰Overhauling it is essential for restoring real cost-benefit analyses to the EPA.</li>
<li><strong>“Reconstituting Science Advisory Board and Clean Air Scientific Advisory Committee”</strong>Biden stacked these scientific panels in favor of harsh regulations on energy producers.Making these panels more balanced is critical for the EPA’s ability to unleash American energy.</li>
<li><strong>“Reconsideration of exceptional events rulemaking”</strong>This EPA policy requires states to undergo complex procedures for exempting prescribed fires from strict air quality standards.¹¹Revisiting it is vital for enabling safe, controlled burns that prevent catastrophic wildfires.</li>
<li><strong>“Reconsideration of regulations throttling the oil and gas industry (OOOO b/c)”</strong>These Biden regulations would effectively shut down smaller producers by imposing high-cost methane capture requirements.¹²Reconsidering them is essential to maximizing American oil and gas production.</li>
<li><strong>“Reconsideration of multiple National Emission Standards for Hazardous Air Pollutants”</strong>These EPA regulations impose costly emission controls on industries like iron and steel, tire manufacturing, and chemicals.¹³Reconsidering them is crucial for unleashing American manufacturing.</li>
<li><strong>“Reconsideration of mandatory Greenhouse Gas Reporting Program”</strong>This Biden program demands pointlessly detailed emission tracking from emitters of over 25,000 metric tons/year of CO2.¹⁴Reconsidering it could save productive companies countless wasted millions and hours.</li>
<li><strong>“Working with states and tribes to resolve massive backlog with State Implementation Plans and Tribal Implementation Plans”</strong>These EPA backlogs deprive states of control over their electricity mix.Resolving them is essential to preserving local grid reliability.</li>
<li><strong>“Redirecting enforcement resources to EPA’s core mission”</strong>The Biden administration used its enforcement power to pressure energy producers into costly settlements.Recalibrating this is essential to protecting the rule of law and establishing American energy dominance.</li>
<li><strong>“Terminating Biden’s Environmental Justice and DEI arms of the agency”</strong>These Biden initiatives diverted resources from even-handed enforcement of the law to identity-based agendas.¹⁵Ending them is crucial to restoring the rule of law and ensuring EPA serves all Americans.</li>
</ol>
