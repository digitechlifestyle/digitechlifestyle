<p>The Godzilla Medallion in <a href="http://fortnite.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Fortnite</a> has stormed in as part of an exciting new crossover event. As you might have guessed, this brand new medallion brings a variety of valuable perks to the table that you can utilize to stay one step ahead of your opponents. </p>
<div class="wp-block-rank-math-toc-block" id="rank-math-toc">
<h2><strong>Fortnite Godzilla Medallion guide</strong></h2>
<nav/></div>
<p>However, the process to get the <a href="https://www.oneesports.gg/fortnite/where-to-find-godzilla-in-fortnite-kong/">Godzilla</a> Medallion in Fortnite is a bit different from other medallions and the worst part is the game doesn’t even mention how to obtain it. Taking this into account, we’ve got you covered with this step-by-step guide to help you claim this epic reward.</p>
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="how-to-obtain-godzilla-medallion-in-fortnite"><strong>How to obtain Godzilla Medallion in Fortnite</strong></h2>
<p>The only way to get the Godzilla Medallion is by dealing the most damage to the Godzilla boss itself during a Fortnite battle royale match. Unfortunately, it’s not as simple as it sounds because there are various twists.</p>
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img fetchpriority="high" data-lazyloaded="1" decoding="async" width="1024" height="576" src="https://cdn.oneesports.gg/cdn-data/2025/01/fortnite_godzilla_medallion_location-1024x576.jpg" alt="Godzilla Medallion in Fortnite" class="wp-image-474906" title="How to get important Godzilla Medallion in Fortnite" srcset="https://cdn.oneesports.gg/cdn-data/2025/01/fortnite_godzilla_medallion_location-1024x576.jpg 1024w, https://cdn.oneesports.gg/cdn-data/2025/01/fortnite_godzilla_medallion_location-300x169.jpg 300w, https://cdn.oneesports.gg/cdn-data/2025/01/fortnite_godzilla_medallion_location-768x432.jpg 768w, https://cdn.oneesports.gg/cdn-data/2025/01/fortnite_godzilla_medallion_location-1536x864.jpg 1536w, https://cdn.oneesports.gg/cdn-data/2025/01/fortnite_godzilla_medallion_location-190x107.jpg 190w, https://cdn.oneesports.gg/cdn-data/2025/01/fortnite_godzilla_medallion_location-450x253.jpg 450w, https://cdn.oneesports.gg/cdn-data/2025/01/fortnite_godzilla_medallion_location-600x338.jpg 600w, https://cdn.oneesports.gg/cdn-data/2025/01/fortnite_godzilla_medallion_location.jpg 1600w" data-sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px"/><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Screenshot by Tarun Sayal/ONE Esports</figcaption></figure>
<p>First of all, Godzilla isn’t your typical boss that spawns every match. Instead, a special portal known as Hollow Earth Rift Gate has a 20% chance of appearing during the match that allows any player to transform into giant Godzilla boss.</p>
<p>There’s no fixed spawn location for this portal but the game will mark its exact location on the minimap for you.</p>
<p>Once you encounter this portal, make sure you’re not the one who enters this portal as medallion is obtained by defeating Godzilla, not by becoming the titan itself. So all you have to do is wait for a random player to enter the portal.</p>
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="how-to-deal-maximum-damage-to-godzilla"><strong>How to deal maximum damage to Godzilla</strong></h2>
<p>Once a player transforms into Godzilla, the only thing you have to make sure is that you deal the most damage. Keep in mind that other players in the match can also participate in the fight and can score more damage. You can track your progress using the bar mentioned at the top of your screen.</p>
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img data-lazyloaded="1" decoding="async" width="1024" height="576" src="https://cdn.oneesports.gg/cdn-data/2025/01/fortnite_godzilla_fight-1-1024x576.jpg" alt="Fortnite Godzilla fight" class="wp-image-474908" title="How to get important Godzilla Medallion in Fortnite" srcset="https://cdn.oneesports.gg/cdn-data/2025/01/fortnite_godzilla_fight-1-1024x576.jpg 1024w, https://cdn.oneesports.gg/cdn-data/2025/01/fortnite_godzilla_fight-1-300x169.jpg 300w, https://cdn.oneesports.gg/cdn-data/2025/01/fortnite_godzilla_fight-1-768x432.jpg 768w, https://cdn.oneesports.gg/cdn-data/2025/01/fortnite_godzilla_fight-1-1536x864.jpg 1536w, https://cdn.oneesports.gg/cdn-data/2025/01/fortnite_godzilla_fight-1-190x107.jpg 190w, https://cdn.oneesports.gg/cdn-data/2025/01/fortnite_godzilla_fight-1-450x253.jpg 450w, https://cdn.oneesports.gg/cdn-data/2025/01/fortnite_godzilla_fight-1-600x338.jpg 600w, https://cdn.oneesports.gg/cdn-data/2025/01/fortnite_godzilla_fight-1.jpg 1600w" data-sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px"/><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Screenshot by Tarun Sayal/ONE Esports</figcaption></figure>
<p>The best way to deal maximum damage to Godzilla is by using Rail Gun and hitting at the weak points. The Rail Gun is a part of the ground loot, which means you can obtain it from the floor, inside chests, or even supply drops.</p>
<p>Keep in mind that the Godzilla boss can also attack you during the fight, so make sure you stay behind a solid cover.</p>
<p>Once you complete the primary objective, the medallion will appear right at your location.</p>
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="godzilla-medallion-ability-explained"><strong>Godzilla Medallion ability explained </strong></h2>
<p>The player who deals the most damage to Godzilla will be rewarded with the Godzilla Medallion, which gives a recharging dash ability (up to three charges at a time), as well as the exotic Burst Quad Launcher.</p>
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><a href="https://www.oneesports.gg/fortnite/godzillas-abilities-in-fortnite-guide/"><img data-lazyloaded="1" decoding="async" width="1024" height="576" src="https://cdn.oneesports.gg/cdn-data/2025/01/fortnite_godzilla-1024x576.jpg" alt="Fortnite Godzilla" class="wp-image-474848" title="How to get important Godzilla Medallion in Fortnite" srcset="https://cdn.oneesports.gg/cdn-data/2025/01/fortnite_godzilla-1024x576.jpg 1024w, https://cdn.oneesports.gg/cdn-data/2025/01/fortnite_godzilla-300x169.jpg 300w, https://cdn.oneesports.gg/cdn-data/2025/01/fortnite_godzilla-768x432.jpg 768w, https://cdn.oneesports.gg/cdn-data/2025/01/fortnite_godzilla-1536x864.jpg 1536w, https://cdn.oneesports.gg/cdn-data/2025/01/fortnite_godzilla-190x107.jpg 190w, https://cdn.oneesports.gg/cdn-data/2025/01/fortnite_godzilla-450x253.jpg 450w, https://cdn.oneesports.gg/cdn-data/2025/01/fortnite_godzilla-600x338.jpg 600w, https://cdn.oneesports.gg/cdn-data/2025/01/fortnite_godzilla.jpg 1600w" data-sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px"/></a><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Credit: Epic Games</figcaption></figure>

<p>You can use these dash moves to quickly reposition during combat or escape the heat of the battle. Thankfully, it also recharges over time, which means you shouldn’t worry about the mobility as long as you have Godzilla Medallion in your inventory.</p>
