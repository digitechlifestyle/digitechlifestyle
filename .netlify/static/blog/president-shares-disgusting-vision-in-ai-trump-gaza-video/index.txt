<p class="clay-paragraph" data-editable="text" data-uri="nymag.com/intelligencer/_components/clay-paragraph/instances/cm7m6jpop003l3b84zythtvjk@published" data-word-count="37">“I don’t want to be cute, I don’t want to be a wise guy, but the Riviera of the Middle East could be something,” Trump <a href="https://x.com/Acyn/status/1886936390950379729">said</a> at a February 4 press conference. “This could be so magnificent.”</p>
<p class="clay-paragraph" data-editable="text" data-uri="nymag.com/intelligencer/_components/clay-paragraph/instances/cm7m6wzz8003x3b84wwfec1wv@published" data-word-count="22">Many <a href="https://news.un.org/en/story/2025/02/1159821">world</a> <a href="https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/czj3wvyn0pno">leaders</a>, <a href="https://www.hrw.org/news/2025/02/05/trump-indicates-intent-escalate-ethnic-cleansing-gaza">activists</a>, and <a href="https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2025/feb/13/rabbis-ad-trump-gaza-plan">religious figures</a> agreed that Trump’s plan “could be something” … and that something is called <a href="https://nymag.com/intelligencer/article/trump-take-over-gaza-israel-ethnic-cleansing-netanyahu.html">ethnic cleansing</a>.</p>
<p class="clay-paragraph" data-editable="text" data-uri="nymag.com/intelligencer/_components/clay-paragraph/instances/cm7m73yuo00423b84y03pthym@published" data-word-count="79">It’s hard to imagine what could be worse than that, but somehow Trump found a way to make his vision for Gaza even more disgusting. Late on Tuesday, Trump shared an AI-generated video to Truth Social and Instagram that depicts “what’s next” for Gaza. Apparently, the president dreams of turning the territory into a Dubai-esque city of skyscrapers where he will revel in his wealth with pals Elon Musk and Benjamin Netanyahu and be worshipped as a golden god.</p>
