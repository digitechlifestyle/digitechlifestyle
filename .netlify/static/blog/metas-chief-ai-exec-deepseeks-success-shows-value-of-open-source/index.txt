<ul class="summary-list">
<li>DeepSeek, an open-source Chinese AI company, has riled Silicon Valley with its rapid rise.</li>
<li>Meta&#8217;s chief AI scientist said DeepSeek has benefited from the open-source community.</li>
<li>Meta&#8217;s AI program has remained open-source, while OpenAI has shifted to closed-source.</li>
</ul>
<p>Silicon Valley was on edge this week after <a target="_blank" class="" href="https://www.businessinsider.com/openai-o1-ai-model-rivals-google-deepseek-reasoning-inference-2024-12" data-analytics-product-module="body_link" rel="">DeepSeek</a>, a Chinese AI company, released its R1 model. In third-party benchmarks, it outperformed leading American AI companies like OpenAI, Meta, and Anthropic.</p>
<p><!-- Excluded mobile ad on desktop --></p>
<p>For Meta&#8217;s chief AI scientist, Yann LeCun, the biggest takeaway from DeepSeek&#8217;s success was not the heightened threat posed by Chinese competition but the value of keeping <a target="_blank" class="" href="https://www.businessinsider.com/china-startup-deepseek-openai-america-ai-2025-1" data-analytics-product-module="body_link" rel="">AI models open source</a> so that anyone can benefit.</p>
<p>It&#8217;s not that China&#8217;s AI is &#8220;surpassing the US,&#8221; but rather that &#8220;open source models are surpassing proprietary ones,&#8221; LeCun said in a post on Threads.</p>
<p><!-- Excluded mobile ad on desktop --></p>
<p>DeepSeek&#8217;s R1 is itself open source, as is Meta&#8217;s Llama. OpenAI, which was originally founded as an open-source AI company with a mission to create technology that benefits all of humanity, has on the other hand more recently shifted to closed-source.</p>
<p>LeCun said DeepSeek has &#8220;profited from open research and open source.&#8221;</p>
<p>&#8220;They came up with new ideas and built them on top of other people&#8217;s work. Because their work is published and open source, everyone can profit from it,&#8221; LeCun said. &#8220;That is the power of open research and open source.&#8221;</p>
<p><!-- Excluded mobile ad on desktop --></p>
<p>When DeepSeek unveiled R1 on January 20, which it said &#8220;demonstrates remarkable reasoning capabilities,&#8221; the company said it was &#8220;pushing the boundaries&#8221; of open-source AI.</p>
<p>The announcement took Silicon Valley by surprise and was easily the most talked-about development in the tech industry during a week that included the <a target="_blank" class="" href="https://www.businessinsider.com/donald-trump-davos-speech-world-economic-forum-eu-2025-1" data-analytics-product-module="body_link" rel="">World Economic Forum</a>, <a target="_blank" class="" href="https://www.businessinsider.com/tiktok-ban-supreme-court-executive-order-timeline" data-analytics-product-module="body_link" rel="">TikTok uncertainty</a>, and President Donald Trump&#8217;s busy <a target="_blank" class="" href="https://markets.businessinsider.com/news/stocks/stock-market-outlook-correction-trump-rally-inflation-tariffs-interest-rates-2025-1" data-analytics-product-module="body_link" rel="">first few days</a> in office.</p>
<p>Days after DeepSeek&#8217;s announcement, Meta CEO <a target="_blank" class="" href="https://www.businessinsider.com/mark-zuckerberg" data-analytics-product-module="body_link" rel="">Mark Zuckerberg</a> said <a target="_blank" class="" href="https://www.businessinsider.com/meta" data-analytics-product-module="body_link" rel="">Meta</a> planned to <a target="_blank" class="" href="https://www.businessinsider.com/mark-zuckerberg-meta-ai-investment-capital-expenditure-datacenter-deepseek-2025-1" data-analytics-product-module="body_link" rel="">spend over $60 billion</a> in 2025 as it doubles down on AI. Zuckerberg has been an outspoken advocate of open-source models.</p>
<p><!-- Excluded mobile ad on desktop --></p>
<p>&#8220;Part of my goal for the next 10-15 years, the next generation of platforms, is to build the next generation of open platforms and <a target="_blank" class="" href="https://www.businessinsider.com/mark-zuckerberg-open-source-ai-platforms-future-competition-apple-llama-2024-9" data-analytics-product-module="body_link" rel="">have the open platforms win</a>,&#8221; he said in September. &#8220;I think that&#8217;s going to lead to a much more vibrant tech industry.&#8221;</p>
<p>Those who support open source say it allows technology to develop rapidly and democratically since anyone can modify and redistribute the code. On the other hand, advocates for closed-source models argue that they are more secure because the code is kept private.</p>
<p>OpenAI CEO Sam Altman said the <a target="_blank" class="" href="https://www.businessinsider.com/sam-altman-why-openai-closed-source-ai-models-2024-11" data-analytics-product-module="body_link" rel="">closed-source approach</a> offers his company &#8220;an easier way to hit the safety threshold&#8221; in an AMA on Reddit last November. He added, however, that he &#8220;would like us to open source more stuff in the future.&#8221;</p>
