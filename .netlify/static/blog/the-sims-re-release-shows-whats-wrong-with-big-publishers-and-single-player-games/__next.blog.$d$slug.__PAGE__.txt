<h2>Despite all this, <em>The Sims</em> is worth a look</h2>
<p>It&#8217;s telling that in a market with too many options, I still put the effort in to get the game working, and I spent multiple evenings this week immersed in the lives of my sims.</p>
<p>Even after 25 years, this game is unique. It has the emergent wackiness of something like <em>RimWorld</em> or <em>Dwarf Fortress</em>, but it has a fast-acting, addictive hook and is easy to learn. There have been other games besides <em>The Sims</em> that are highly productive engines for original player stories, but few have achieved these heights while remaining accessible to virtually everyone.</p>
<p>Like so many of the best games, it&#8217;s hard to stop playing once you start. There&#8217;s always one more task you want to complete—or you&#8217;re about to walk away when something hilariously unexpected happens.</p>
<p>The problems I had getting <em>The Sims</em> to run aren&#8217;t that much worse than what I surely experienced on my PC back in 2002—it&#8217;s just that the standards are a lot higher now.</p>
<p>I&#8217;ve gotten $20 out of value out of the purchase, despite my gripes. But it&#8217;s not just about my experience. More broadly, <em>The Sims</em> deserved better. It could have had a moment back in the cultural zeitgeist, with tens of thousands of Twitch viewers.</p>
<h2>Missed opportunities</h2>
<p>The moment seems perfect: The world is stressful, so people want nostalgia. Cozy games are ascendant. Sandbox designs are making a comeback. <em>The Sims</em> slots smoothly into all of that.</p>
<p>But go to those Twitch streams, and you&#8217;ll see a lot of complaining about how the game didn&#8217;t really get everything it deserved and a sentiment that whatever moment EA was hoping for was undermined by this lack of commitment.</p>
<p>Instead, the cozy game du jour on Twitch is the <em>Animal Crossing</em>-like <em>Hello Kitty Island Adventure</em>, a former Apple Arcade exclusive that made its way to Steam recently. To be clear, I&#8217;m not knocking <em>Hello Kitty Island Adventure</em>; it&#8217;s a great game for fans of the modern cozy genre, and I&#8217;m delighted to see an indie studio seeing so much success.</p>
