<p><img decoding="async" src="https://img.decrypt.co/insecure/rs:fill:1024:512:1:0/plain/https://cdn.decrypt.co/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/bitcoin-japanese-yen-japan-gID_7.jpg@png" /></p>
<div style="position: relative; overflow: visible; font-size: 1.2em; line-height: 1.58;">
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Metaplanet Inc. plans to increase its Bitcoin holdings by 470%, aiming to reach 10,000 BTC this year as it continues to build on an accumulation strategy first undertaken in April.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">CEO Simon Gerovich says Metaplanet will </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">expand its BTC holdings</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> by &#8220;utilizing the most accretive capital market tools available.&#8221;</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100">&#8220;Last year was transformational, as we broke records, expanded our Bitcoin treasury, and reinforced our position as Asia’s leading Bitcoin Treasury Company,&#8221; Gerovich <a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://x.com/gerovich/status/1875777564184342786" target="_blank" rel="nofollow external noopener">tweeted</a> Saturday. &#8220;This year, we’re laser-focused on execution and driving even greater value for our shareholders.&#8221;</p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">The Tokyo-based investment firm currently holds 1,761.98 BTC worth 27.2 billion yen (US$173.6 million), according to a December 23 regulatory </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://contents.xj-storage.jp/xcontents/33500/83ccb67f/4ee2/4f76/a2c8/dbeaed64901f/140120241223542705.pdf" target="_blank" rel="nofollow external noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">filing</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">. </span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">It follows a series of 19 purchases last year, with an average acquisition price of 11,846,002 yen per Bitcoin (roughly $75,000 each).  </span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Its most recent purchase of 619.70 BTC for 9.5 billion yen in December marked its largest single acquisition, representing about 35% of its total holdings.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">The firm’s Bitcoin holdings began with 97.8 BTC on April 22, growing rapidly in just over six months, with the fastest pace occurring between October and December 2024.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">BTC Yield, which measures how much Bitcoin the company owns compared to its total shares, jumped from 42% to 310% in the last quarter of 2024, showing the company acquired Bitcoin faster than it issued new shares.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">The transaction came as Bitcoin traded near $100,000, amid predictions from VanEck and </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://decrypt.co/291492/bitcoin-hit-93000-bitwise-projects-500k" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Bitwise</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> of prices between $180,000 and $200,000 in 2025.</span></p>
<h2 class="sc-b2a202e4-2 bmropA gg-dark:text-white scene:font-itc-avant-garde-gothic-pro scene:font-light" style="margin-top: 2em; text-align: left; padding-bottom: 16px; margin-bottom: 16px; border-bottom: 1px solid #dfe2e4;"><b>Bitcoin strategy</b></h2>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Metaplanet reorganized its share structure in mid-2024 to meet Tokyo Stock Exchange rules before expanding its Bitcoin purchasing power. </span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">On June 28, the company first consolidated its shares 10-to-1, effectively raising their trading price to meet the exchange&#8217;s 50,000 yen minimum trading unit requirement.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">It then launched a rights offering on August 6 effectively doubling the company&#8217;s shares from 18.17 million to 36.27 million while raising 10 billion yen (US$63.7 million)  from 13,774 shareholders.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">The company secured additional funds through two bond sales totaling 9.5 billion yen (US$60.3 million) in December, while setting up a new stock rights program in November for future fundraising needs.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">By December 18, Metaplanet formalized its Bitcoin Treasury Operations as an official business line, shifting from one-off purchases to systematic accumulation. </span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">&#8220;We specifically note that we have acquired Bitcoin using proceeds from various financing activities,&#8221; the company stated in its filing with the Tokyo Stock Exchange, outlining plans to continue leveraging capital markets for future purchases.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Financial data reveals a shifting acquisition strategy for Metaplanet, which has been touted as “Asia&#8217;s answer to MicroStrategy,” as it followed the latter’s </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://decrypt.co/288247/metaplanet-adopts-microstrategys-bitcoin-metric-to-boost-shareholder-transparency" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">playbook for corporate Bitcoin acquisitions</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">. </span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">The ambitious expansion target comes as analysts forecast potential </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://decrypt.co/294154/bitcoin-national-reserve-countries" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">government adoption of Bitcoin reserves</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> could trigger broader institutional investment in 2025.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">That has prompted other </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://decrypt.co/298813/matador-technologies-adopts-bitcoin-reserve" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">corporate entities</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> to begin aggressively accumulating Bitcoin with an eye on long-term growth as traders and institutions anticipate rising prices for crypto this year.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><i>Edited by <a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://decrypt.co/author/sebastian" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Sebastian Sinclair</a></i></p>
