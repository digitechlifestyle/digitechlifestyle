<p><a href="https://coincodecap.com/what-is-blockchain-a-simple-guide-for-dummies/">Blockchain technology</a> is a breakthrough for a new era of decentralized technologies. One of the main classifications of blockchain technology is the Permissionless and Permissioned blockchain.</p>
<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow"><p><a href="https://coincodecap.com/what-is-blockchain-a-simple-guide-for-dummies/"><strong>What is Blockchain? A Simple Guide for Dummies</strong></a></p></blockquote>
<p>In this article, we will explain the following-</p>
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>What is a Permissioned blockchain?</li>
<li>What is a Permissionless blockchain?</li>
<li>Difference and similarity between Permissioned and Permissionless blockchain</li>
<li>Use cases of Permissioned and Permissionless blockchain</li>
</ul>
<p>For an intuitive understanding, let’s consider the public and private transports system. The best example of public transports is a public bus service, and that of private transports is your car.</p>
<p>Everyone can use the public bus service, no permission required. But a person will require your consent to use your car.</p>
<p>Permissioned blockchain is similar to private transports, and Permissionless blockchain is identical to public transports.</p>
<div class="code-block code-block-1 ai-track" style="margin: 8px auto; text-align: center; display: block; clear: both;" data-ai="WzEsMCwiQmxvY2sgMSIsIiIsMl0="><a href="https://coincodecap.com/go/inline-ads" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener"> <img fetchpriority="high" class="ewww_webp" width="1000" height="90" border="0" data-ping-nopin="true" data-eio="j" /><img decoding="async" src="https://coincodecap.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Frame-2104-1.jpg" width="1000" height="90" border="0" data-ping-nopin="true" /> </a></div>
<p>Now let’s have a close look at both of them.</p>
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span id="What_is_a_Permissionless_Blockchain" class="ez-toc-section"></span><strong>What is a Permissionless Blockchain?</strong></h2>
<p>Permissionless blockchains are also known as public blockchains. As the name suggests, it doesn’t require anyone’s permission to participate in the network.</p>
<p>Some of the popular public blockchains include Bitcoin, Ethereum, Litecoin, etc. Public blockchains are transparent. Most of the public blockchain has a cryptocurrency associated with it.</p>
<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow"><p><a href="https://coincodecap.com/a-candid-explanation-of-bitcoin/"><strong>Also Read: A Candid Explanation of Bitcoin</strong></a></p></blockquote>
<p>Public blockchains are governed through consensus (Algorithm and community), and no single entity or a group of entities have control over the network.</p>
<p><strong>Properties of a Permissionless blockchain</strong> –</p>
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Censorship resistance – No one can censor an entity from the network</li>
<li>Transparency – Data, development, and governance is public.</li>
<li>Immutability – Can not edit or update past transaction or other data</li>
<li>Openness – Anyone can join the network</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span id="What_is_a_Permissioned_Blockchain" class="ez-toc-section"></span><strong>What is a Permissioned Blockchain?</strong></h2>
<p>It is clear from the name that you need permission to join a Permissioned network. Sometimes, it is also known as a private blockchain.</p>
<p>In this network, each participant is assigned a specific access level. Let us consider an organization uses built upon a private blockchain.</p>
<p>In that case, the board of directors will have access to all the transactions taking place in the blockchain. That may not be the case with regular employees.</p>
<p>The central authority decides the transparency level of the Permissioned blockchain. Permissioned blockchains have consensus just like a public blockchain, and it followed by everyone in the network.</p>
<p>A Permissioned Blockchain may or may not have cryptocurrency associated with them. Some of the well known Permissioned Blockchain frameworks are <a href="https://www.hyperledger.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Hyperledger</a>, <a href="https://www.corda.net/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Corda</a>.</p>
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span id="Differences_between_Permissioned_and_Permissionless_blockchain" class="ez-toc-section"></span><strong>Differences between Permissioned and Permissionless blockchain</strong></h2>
<ol class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Participants</strong>: Anyone can be a participant in a Permissionless blockchain. One has to be approved by a central authority to become a participant in the case of a Permissioned blockchain.</li>
<li><strong>Assets</strong>: No crypto asset/token needed in Permissioned blockchain. Permissionless blockchains involved one or more crypto assets/tokens.</li>
<li><strong>Transparency</strong>: Transparency is maintained in the Permissionless Blockchain as anyone can see the transactions. Access level assigned to the participants decides the transparency in the case of Permissioned Blockchain.</li>
<li><strong>Privacy</strong>: Permissionless blockchains try to optimize for privacy as technology allows. Permissioned blockchains have controlled privacy.</li>
</ol>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span id="Similarities_between_both_the_blockchain" class="ez-toc-section"></span><strong>Similarities between both the blockchain</strong></h2>
<ol class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Ledger System: </strong>In both cases, a copy of the blockchain is distributed among all the participants and the data is stored in the form of an immutable ledger.</li>
<li><strong>Consensus: </strong>Both have a consensus protocol to validate a transaction.</li>
</ol>
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span id="Use_cases_of_both_blockchains" class="ez-toc-section"></span><strong>Use cases of both blockchains</strong></h2>
<ol class="wp-block-list">
<li>Permissioned Blockchain: They are mainly used in the supply chain management system, banking sector.</li>
<li>Permissionless Blockchain: They are mainly used as an open network for commerce, finance, data, identity, and other use cases.</li>
</ol>
<p>Usage of Permissoned and Permissionless blockchain depends on the subject of implementation. If you want to use blockchain in a system that needs more decentralization and transparency then the Permissionless blockchain is the best. On the other side, if you want only a handful of people to have full access to the data then the Permissioned blockchain is the best.</p>
