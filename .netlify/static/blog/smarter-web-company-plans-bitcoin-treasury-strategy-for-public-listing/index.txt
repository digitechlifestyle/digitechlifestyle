									<p>In 2009, Andrew Webley founded a small web services firm in the UK. Fast forward to today, and this 15-year-old company is making headlines for its bold move into the world of digital assets. By 2023, they began accepting payments in <strong>bitcoin</strong>, setting the stage for a groundbreaking strategy.</p>
<p>Now, they’re preparing for a <em>public listing</em> on the AQUIS Stock Exchange through a reverse takeover of a company established in 1907. This innovative approach accelerates their entry into the public market, planned for April 2025. Their dual focus? Maintaining excellence in web services while pioneering <strong>crypto treasury strategies</strong>.</p>
<p>With a lean team and proprietary CMS technology, this firm is betting big on the future of digital assets. Their journey shows how traditional businesses can embrace innovation while staying true to their roots.</p>
<h3>Key Takeaways</h3>
<ul>
<li>A 15-year-old UK web services firm is making corporate history with digital assets.</li>
<li>They began accepting bitcoin payments in 2023, two years before their planned public listing.</li>
<li>A reverse takeover strategy will accelerate their entry into the public market by April 2025.</li>
<li>Their proprietary CMS technology supports their innovative approach.</li>
<li>They aim to maintain web services excellence while pioneering crypto treasury strategies.</li>

<h2>The Smarter Web Company’s Path to Going Public</h2>
<p>From crafting websites to embracing digital currencies, this company’s journey is remarkable. What started as a traditional web services provider has now positioned itself as a leader in digital asset innovation. Their evolution reflects a bold vision for the future.</p>
<h3>From Web Services to Bitcoin Adoption</h3>
<p>Originally focused on web design, development, and online marketing, the firm has always prioritized excellence. In 2023, they took a groundbreaking step by allowing clients to pay with digital assets. This move not only modernized their operations but also attracted a new wave of tech-savvy customers.</p>
<p>By integrating digital currencies into their payment options, they’ve demonstrated a commitment to innovation. This decision has strengthened their reputation as a forward-thinking organization. Their <strong>organic growth</strong> continues to fuel their strategic investments in digital assets.</p>
<h3>Reverse Takeover and AQUIS Stock Exchange Plans</h3>
<p>To accelerate their entry into the public market, the company opted for a <strong>reverse takeover</strong>. They acquired Uranium Energy Exploration Plc, a shell company, to streamline the process. This approach saves time compared to a traditional IPO, allowing them to focus on their core mission.</p>
<p>Their listing on the <strong>AQUIS Stock Exchange</strong> is set for April 2025, with a £2M IPO target. This exchange was chosen for its liquidity and accessibility, making it an ideal platform for their growth. The ticker SWC will represent their shares, marking a new chapter in their story.</p>
<p>This strategic move not only benefits the company but also provides existing clients with enhanced opportunities. By leveraging their web service revenue, they’re funding acquisitions in the digital asset space, ensuring long-term value for stakeholders.</p>
<h2>Smarter Web Company Plans Public Listing With Bitcoin Treasury Strategy</h2>
<p>Innovative firms are turning to digital assets to secure long-term value. The Smarter Web Company is no exception, with a formal <strong>digital assets treasury policy</strong> in development. This policy aims to balance BTC holdings with traditional cash reserves, ensuring financial stability while embracing innovation.</p>

<h3>Why Bitcoin? Inflation Hedge and Long-Term Value</h3>
<p>Bitcoin is increasingly seen as a reliable <strong>hedge</strong> against inflation. Unlike traditional assets like gold, BTC offers portability and decentralization. The company’s undisclosed BTC holdings reflect this belief, positioning them to benefit from long-term value appreciation.</p>
<p>Their approach mirrors successful strategies by firms like <strong>MicroStrategy</strong>, which has integrated BTC into its <strong>balance sheet</strong>. By adopting a similar model, they aim to protect their <strong>capital</strong> from economic volatility while maximizing growth potential.</p>
<h3>Digital Assets Treasury Policy: Balancing BTC and Cash</h3>
<p>Their treasury strategy is built on three pillars: cash reserves, BTC holdings, and equity raises. This balanced approach ensures liquidity while leveraging the benefits of digital assets. Tyler Evans, who plays a dual role at the company and UTXO Management, is instrumental in shaping this policy.</p>
<p>Compared to Metaplanet’s 3,350 BTC stash, their holdings are undisclosed but strategically aligned. This focus on portable, non-sovereign assets is particularly relevant for UK mid-caps navigating economic uncertainty.</p>
<h3>Following the Footsteps of MicroStrategy and Metaplanet</h3>
<p>By mirroring <strong>MicroStrategy’s</strong> convertible note approach, they plan to raise additional <strong>capital</strong> in the future. This strategy allows them to fund acquisitions and expand their digital asset portfolio without diluting equity.</p>
<p>Their journey underscores the growing importance of digital assets in corporate finance. As they prepare for their public listing, their innovative approach sets a new standard for mid-sized businesses.</p>
<h2>Bitcoin-Native Investors Backing the Vision</h2>
<p>The rise of digital assets has attracted a new wave of <strong>investors</strong> with a shared vision. These backers are not just funding projects; they’re shaping the future of innovation. Their focus on mission-aligned strategies sets them apart from traditional venture capital.</p>

<p><strong>UTXO Management</strong>, a significant player in the digital asset space, has committed £1M in pre-IPO funding. This move highlights their confidence in the company’s strategy. With a stake exceeding 3%, UTXO meets the U.K.’s threshold for a significant investor.</p>
<p>Their involvement goes beyond capital. UTXO brings expertise from their work with firms like <strong>Metaplanet</strong>, ensuring a robust foundation for growth. This partnership underscores the importance of aligned capital in driving long-term success.</p>
<h3>The Role of 210k Capital and Mission-Aligned Backers</h3>

<p>Mission-aligned backers like 210k and UTXO provide more than just financial support. They offer strategic guidance, governance, and a shared commitment to innovation. This alignment prevents strategic drift and ensures sustained growth.</p>

<h3>Bitcoin as a Capital Efficiency Tool for Growth</h3>
<p>Bitcoin is proving to be a powerful tool for <strong>capital efficiency</strong>. By allocating a portion of their reserves to BTC, businesses can protect against inflation and reduce reliance on traditional cash reserves. This strategy is particularly beneficial for <strong>mid-sized businesses</strong> looking to optimize their financial performance.</p>
<p>For example, a £1M BTC allocation could outperform traditional cash reserves in terms of long-term value appreciation. This approach not only enhances financial stability but also positions businesses to take advantage of opportunities in the <strong>crypto markets</strong>.</p>
<h3>Transparency and Investor Disclosures Post-Listing</h3>
<p>Transparency is crucial for maintaining investor trust, especially in the volatile world of digital assets. Post-listing, businesses must commit to regular updates on their BTC holdings and <strong>acquisitions</strong>. This includes disclosing BTC wallet addresses and acquisition criteria in quarterly reports.</p>
<p>Such <strong>investor disclosures</strong> ensure that stakeholders are well-informed and can make decisions based on accurate data. This level of transparency is essential for fostering confidence and attracting institutional investors.</p>
