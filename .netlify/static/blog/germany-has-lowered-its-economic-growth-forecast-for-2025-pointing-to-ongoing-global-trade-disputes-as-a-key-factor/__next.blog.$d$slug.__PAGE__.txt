<p>Germany&#8217;s economic growth forecast for 2025 has been revised downward due to several key factors:</p>

<h3>B. Impact of ongoing global trade disputes</h3>
<p>Global trade disputes continue to play a significant role in shaping Germany&#8217;s economic outlook:</p>
<ol>
<li>Unpredictable economic policies of the new U.S. administration</li>
<li>Potential trade tensions with key partners</li>
<li>Anticipated introduction of higher tariffs on imports from Mexico, Canada, and China</li>
</ol>
<p>These factors are particularly impacting German exports:</p>
<ul>
<li>Exports to China, once a vital market, fell 23% from 2021 levels</li>
<li>Manufacturing sector struggling to maintain competitiveness against rising Chinese competition</li>
<li>Energy costs challenging the competitiveness of energy-intensive industries</li>
</ul>
<p>Despite these challenges, domestic demand is forecasted to become the primary growth driver in 2025 and 2026. However, the negative contribution from net exports is expected to persist due to ongoing trade issues.</p>
