<p>Publisher <a class="link-relationship" title="Companies &gt; Koei Tecmo Games [3,045 articles]" href="https://www.gematsu.com/companies/koei-tecmo-games">Koei Tecmo</a> and developer <a class="link-relationship" title="Companies &gt; Team NINJA [645 articles]" href="https://www.gematsu.com/companies/koei-tecmo-games/team-ninja">Team NINJA</a> will release <a class="link-relationship" title="Genres &gt; Open-World [552 articles]" href="https://www.gematsu.com/genres/open-world">open-world</a> <a class="link-relationship" title="Genres &gt; Action RPG [1,476 articles]" href="https://www.gematsu.com/genres/rpg/action-rpg">action RPG</a> <em><a class="link-relationship" title="Games &gt; Rise of the Ronin [18 articles]" href="https://www.gematsu.com/games/rise-of-the-ronin">Rise of the Ronin</a></em> for <a class="link-relationship" title="Platforms &gt; PC [22,207 articles]" href="https://www.gematsu.com/platforms/pc">PC</a> via <a class="link-external" href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/1340990/Rise_of_the_Ronin/" target="_blank">Steam</a> on March 11 for $49.99, the companies announced. <span id="more-940531"/></p>
<p><em>Rise of the Ronin</em> first launched for PlayStation 5 on March 22, 2024.</p>
<p>Users who purchase the PC version by April 2 will receive the same bonus content included with the PlayStation 5 version as pre-order incentives:</p>
<ul>
<li>Four combat styles:
<ul>
<li>Hayabusa-ryu for Katana</li>
<li>Hayabusa-ryu for Naginata</li>
<li>Nioh-ryu for Katana</li>
<li>Aisu Kage-ryu for Katana</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Iga Ninja’s Katana</li>
<li>Iga Ninja Armor Set (a four-piece set)</li>
</ul>
<p><!-- AD: content --></p>
<p>Here is an overview of the game, via its <a class="link-external" href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/1340990/Rise_of_the_Ronin/" target="_blank">Steam page</a>:</p>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>About</strong></p>
<p>An open-world <a class="link-relationship" title="Genres &gt; RPG [16,950 articles]" href="https://www.gematsu.com/genres/rpg">RPG</a> set in the Bakumatsu period, where players take on the role of a ronin forging their own destiny. The game features multiple possible endings, allowing players to choose which faction to support—such as the anti-shogunate faction, pro-shogunate faction, or Western forces—which significantly alters the story’s outcome.</p>
<p>The game will support various PC features, including ultra-wide monitor compatibility, and include enhancements such as 8K resolution, 120fps, 3D audio, and customizable keyboard and mouse settings, enriching the gaming experience.</p>
<p><strong>Story</strong></p>
<p>Japan, 1863. After three centuries of the Tokugawa Shogunate’s reign, the Black Ships of the West descend upon the nation’s borders and the country falls into a state of turmoil. Amidst the chaos of war, disease, and political unrest, a nameless warrior forges their own path, holding the very fate of Japan in their hands.</p>
<p><strong>New Features Exclusive to the PC Version</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>8K resolution support.</li>
<li>DirectX 12 Ultimate support.</li>
<li>Ultra-wide and super ultra-wide monitor compatibility.</li>
<li>120 frames per second support.</li>
<li>Ray tracing support.</li>
<li>3D audio support.</li>
<li>Customizable keyboard and mouse controls.</li>
<li>AMD Fidelity FX Super Resolution support.</li>
<li>NVIDIA DLSS and Reflex support.</li>
<li>UI menu with mouse clickability.</li>
<li>Intel XeSS graphics technology support.</li>
</ul>
</blockquote>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>Key Features</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Shape Your Own Story</strong> – As a nameless warrior—a ronin—your destiny evolves based on the choices you make. You will encounter historical figures and factions with various ideologies, such as the anti-shogunate faction leader Ryoma Sakamoto and the shogunate-affiliated organization Shinsengumi led by Isami Kondo, allowing you to experience the tumultuous Bakumatsu period. You will face significant decisions, such as whether to assassinate or protect key figures like Naosuke Ii, and craft your own history through a multi-ending system.</li>
<li><strong>Customize Your Weapons and Combat Styles</strong> – You can choose from various melee weapons like katana, spear, and odachi, each with multiple combat styles. By freely combining these based on enemies’ weapons and your own combat style, you can enjoy a wide range of tactics. There are also ranged weapons like guns and bows, which you must use strategically according to the situation.</li>
<li><strong>Explore Beautiful Cities in an Open World</strong> – In the mixed cultural landscape of Edo, Yokohama, and Kyoto during the Bakumatsu period, you can explore stunning scenery with seasonal changes like cherry blossoms and autumn leaves. You can use a grappling rope to climb onto rooftops and the glider Avicula to glide through the air. Additionally, you can capture your favorite views using Photo Mode.</li>
<li><strong>Customize Your Character</strong> – From their facial features, hair, and makeup to their body shape and voice, the protagonist—your Veiled Edge—is yours to create. As you progress through the story, you’ll acquire new clothing and gear to customize their appearance further.</li>
</ul>
</blockquote>
