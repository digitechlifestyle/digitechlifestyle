<p><i>2024 Non-GAAP Free Cash Flow Up Approximately 50% Year-Over-Year</i></p>
<p><b>Highlights:</b></p>
<p><b>Full Year 2024</b></p>
<ul type="disc">
<li>Generated cash from operating activities of <span class="xn-money">$3.7 billion</span>, Non-GAAP Cash Flow of <span class="xn-money">$4.0 billion</span> and Non-GAAP Free Cash Flow of <span class="xn-money">$1.7 billion</span> after capital expenditures of <span class="xn-money">$2.3 billion</span></li>
<li>Produced average total volumes of 585 thousand barrels of oil equivalent per day (“MBOE/d”), including 211 thousand barrels per day (“Mbbls/d”) of oil and condensate, 91 Mbbls/d of other NGLs (C2 to C4) and 1,698 million cubic feet per day (“MMcf/d”) of natural gas</li>
<li>Returned more than <span class="xn-money">$900 million</span> to shareholders through the combination of base dividend payments and share buybacks</li>
<li>Announced the acquisition of oil-rich Montney assets, expected to add approximately 70 MBOE/d of production, 900 net 10,000-foot equivalent well locations, and approximately 109,000 net acres of land for approximately <span class="xn-money">$2.3 billion</span> before closing adjustments. The acquisition closed on <span class="xn-chron">January 31, 2025</span>.</li>
<li>Announced the sale of its Uinta assets for approximately <span class="xn-money">$2.0 billion</span> before closing adjustments. The divestiture closed on <span class="xn-chron">January 22, 2025</span>.</li>
<li>Reduced Non-GAAP Net Debt by <span class="xn-money">$323 million</span></li>
</ul>
<p><b>Fourth Quarter 2024</b></p>
<ul type="disc">
<li>Generated fourth quarter cash from operating activities of <span class="xn-money">$1.0 billion</span>, Non-GAAP Cash Flow of <span class="xn-money">$1.0 billion</span> and Non-GAAP Free Cash Flow of <span class="xn-money">$452 million</span> after capital expenditures of <span class="xn-money">$552 million</span></li>
<li>Delivered average quarterly production volumes of 580 MBOE/d, including 210 Mbbls/d of oil and condensate, 90 Mbbls/d of other NGLs and 1,680 MMcf/d of natural gas</li>
</ul>
<p><b>2025 Outlook</b></p>
<ul type="disc">
<li>Announced full year 2025 capital program of approximately <span class="xn-money">$2.15</span> to <span class="xn-money">$2.25 billion</span>, which is expected to deliver total production volumes of 595 to 615 MBOE/d, including oil and condensate volumes of 202 to 208 Mbbls/d</li>
</ul>
<p><span class="legendSpanClass"><span class="xn-location">DENVER</span></span>, <span class="legendSpanClass"><span class="xn-chron">Feb. 26, 2025</span></span> /CNW/ – Ovintiv Inc. (NYSE: OVV) (TSX: OVV) (“Ovintiv” or the “Company”) today announced its fourth quarter and year-end 2024 financial and operating results. The Company plans to hold a conference call and webcast at <span class="xn-chron">9:00 a.m. MT</span> (<span class="xn-chron">11:00 a.m. ET</span>) on <span class="xn-chron">February 27, 2025</span>. Please see dial-in details within this release, as well as additional details on the Company’s website at <a href="https://c212.net/c/link/?t=0&amp;l=en&amp;o=4371004-1&amp;h=3599644378&amp;u=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.ovintiv.com%2F&amp;a=www.ovintiv.com" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">www.ovintiv.com</a> under <a href="https://c212.net/c/link/?t=0&amp;l=en&amp;o=4371004-1&amp;h=1894554224&amp;u=https%3A%2F%2Finvestor.ovintiv.com%2Fpresentations-events&amp;a=Presentations+and+Events+%E2%80%93+Ovintiv" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">Presentations and Events – Ovintiv</a>.</p>
<p>“2024 was another year of exceptional delivery on our durable returns strategy,” said Ovintiv President and CEO, <span class="xn-person">Brendan McCracken</span>. “Our team’s continued focus on value creation drove strong financial results, created lasting efficiencies and boosted shareholder returns. We enhanced our capital efficiency through our focus on execution excellence, which led to multiple quarters of positive guidance revisions on production and costs without an increase in capital spending. We have entered 2025 with an even deeper premium inventory runway and a more profitable and focused portfolio following the acquisition of top tier oil-rich <span class="xn-person">Alberta Montney</span> assets and the sale of our Uinta assets.”</p>
<p><b>Full Year 2024 Financial and Operating Results</b></p>
<ul type="disc">
<li>The Company recorded full year net earnings of <span class="xn-money">$1.1 billion</span>, or <span class="xn-money">$4.21</span> per share diluted, including a non-cash ceiling test impairment of <span class="xn-money">$350 million</span>, after tax, and net gains of <span class="xn-money">$156 million</span> from net settlement proceeds related to previous dispositions of certain legacy assets.</li>
<li>Full year net gains on risk management in revenues totaled <span class="xn-money">$135 million</span>, before tax.</li>
<li>Full year capital investment of <span class="xn-money">$2,303 million</span> was in line with the full year 2024 guidance range of approximately <span class="xn-money">$2,275 million</span> to <span class="xn-money">$2,325 million</span>.</li>
<li>Full year upstream operating expense was <span class="xn-money">$4.24</span> per barrel of oil equivalent (“BOE”). Upstream transportation and processing costs were <span class="xn-money">$7.25</span> per BOE. Production, mineral and other taxes were <span class="xn-money">$1.56</span> per BOE, or 4.5% of upstream product revenue. These costs were below the bottom end of guidance on a combined basis.</li>
<li>Including the impact of hedges, full year average realized prices for oil and condensate was <span class="xn-money">$72.31</span> per barrel (95% of WTI), <span class="xn-money">$19.70</span> per barrel for other NGLs, and <span class="xn-money">$2.17</span> per Mcf (96% of NYMEX) for natural gas, resulting in a total average realized price of <span class="xn-money">$35.47</span> per BOE.</li>
</ul>
<p><b>Fourth Quarter 2024 Financial and Operating Results</b></p>
<ul type="disc">
<li>Fourth quarter net loss totaled <span class="xn-money">$60 million</span>, or <span class="xn-money">($0.23)</span> per share diluted, including a non-cash ceiling test impairment of <span class="xn-money">$350 million</span>, after tax.</li>
<li>Fourth quarter net losses on risk management in revenues totaled <span class="xn-money">$16 million</span>, before tax.</li>
<li>Fourth quarter capital investment of <span class="xn-money">$552 million</span> was in line with the guidance range of approximately <span class="xn-money">$525 million</span> to <span class="xn-money">$575 million</span>.</li>
<li>Fourth quarter upstream operating expense was <span class="xn-money">$3.99</span> per BOE. Upstream transportation and processing costs were <span class="xn-money">$7.30</span> per BOE. Production, mineral and other taxes were <span class="xn-money">$1.41</span> per BOE, or 4.2% of upstream product revenue. These costs were below the bottom end of guidance on a combined basis.</li>
<li>Including the impact of hedges, fourth quarter average realized prices for oil and condensate was <span class="xn-money">$67.50</span> per barrel (96% of WTI), <span class="xn-money">$20.88</span> per barrel for other NGLs, and <span class="xn-money">$2.42</span> per Mcf (87% of NYMEX) for natural gas, resulting in a total average realized price of <span class="xn-money">$34.65</span> per BOE.</li>
</ul>

<p>Ovintiv expects production in the first quarter to be the low point for the year, reflecting the timing impacts of the transaction close dates for the Montney acquisition and Uinta disposition of approximately 3 Mbbls/d in the quarter. Oil and condensate production is expected to stabilize in the second quarter and remain largely flat through the end of the year for an annual average of 202 to 208 Mbbls/d.</p>
<p><b>Returns to Shareholders</b></p>
<p>Full year shareholder returns totaled approximately <span class="xn-money">$913 million</span>, consisting of share buybacks of approximately <span class="xn-money">$597 million</span>, or approximately 12.7 million shares of common stock, and base dividend payments of approximately <span class="xn-money">$316 million</span>.</p>
<p>The Company’s share buyback program has been temporarily paused to fund <span class="xn-money">$377 million</span> for the Montney acquisition. By the end of the first quarter, Ovintiv expects that approximately <span class="xn-money">$368 million</span> will have been redirected to debt reduction from the buyback pause. The Company expects to resume share buybacks in the second quarter of 2025.</p>
<p><b>Continued Balance Sheet Focus</b></p>
<p>Ovintiv had approximately <span class="xn-money">$3.6 billion</span> in total liquidity as of <span class="xn-chron">December 31, 2024</span>, which included available credit facilities of <span class="xn-money">$3.5 billion</span>, available uncommitted demand lines of <span class="xn-money">$91 million</span>, and cash and cash equivalents of <span class="xn-money">$42 million</span>. Ovintiv reported total debt of <span class="xn-money">$5.45 billion</span> at year end.</p>
<p>Non-GAAP Debt to EBITDA was 1.3 times and Non-GAAP Debt to Adjusted EBITDA was 1.2 times as of <span class="xn-chron">December 31, 2024</span>.</p>
<p>The Company remains committed to maintaining a strong balance sheet and is currently rated investment grade by four credit rating agencies. Ovintiv maintains a long-term leverage target of 1.0 times Non-GAAP Debt to Adjusted EBITDA at mid-cycle prices, with an associated long-term total debt target of <span class="xn-money">$4.0 billion</span>.</p>
<p><b>Dividend Declared</b></p>
<p>On <span class="xn-chron">February 26, 2025</span>, Ovintiv’s Board declared a quarterly dividend of <span class="xn-money">$0.30</span> per share of common stock payable on <span class="xn-chron">March 31, 2025</span>, to shareholders of record as of <span class="xn-chron">March 14, 2025</span>.</p>
<p><b>Asset Highlights</b></p>
<p><b>Permian</b></p>
<p>Permian production averaged 208 MBOE/d (80% liquids) in the fourth quarter. The Company had 42 net wells turned in line (TIL). In 2025, Ovintiv plans to invest approximately <span class="xn-money">$1.2 billion</span> to <span class="xn-money">$1.3 billion</span> in the play to bring on 130 to 140 net wells.</p>
<p><b>Montney  </b></p>
<p>Montney production averaged 235 MBOE/d (20% liquids) in the fourth quarter. The Company had 13 net wells TIL. In 2025, Ovintiv plans to invest approximately <span class="xn-money">$575 million</span> to <span class="xn-money">$625 million</span> in the play to bring on 75 to 85 net wells.</p>
<p><b>Anadarko</b></p>
<p>Anadarko production averaged 100 MBOE/d (56% liquids) in the fourth quarter. The Company did not bring on any wells in the quarter. Ovintiv plans to invest approximately <span class="xn-money">$300 million</span> to <span class="xn-money">$325 million</span> in the play in 2025 to bring on 25 to 35 net wells.</p>
<p><b>Year-End 2024 Reserves</b></p>
<p>SEC proved reserves at year-end 2024 were 2.1 billion BOE, of which approximately 59% were liquids and 59% were proved developed. Total proved reserves replacement excluding the impact of commodity prices and acquisitions and divestitures was 200% of 2024 production. Ovintiv’s reserve life index at year end was approximately 10 years.</p>

<ul type="disc">
<li><b>Non-GAAP Cash Flow</b> is a non-GAAP measure defined as cash from (used in) operating activities excluding net change in other assets and liabilities, and net change in non-cash working capital.</li>

<li><b>Net Debt</b> is defined as long-term debt, including the current portion, less cash and cash equivalents.</li>
<li><b>Adjusted EBITDA, Debt to EBITDA and Debt to Adjusted EBITDA (Leverage Target/Ratio)</b> are non-GAAP measures. EBITDA is defined as trailing 12-month net earnings (loss) before income taxes, depreciation, depletion and amortization, and interest. Adjusted EBITDA is EBITDA adjusted for impairments, accretion of asset retirement obligation, unrealized gains/losses on risk management, foreign exchange gains/losses, gains/losses on divestitures and other gains/losses. Debt to EBITDA is calculated as long-term debt, including the current portion, divided by EBITDA. Debt to Adjusted EBITDA is calculated as long-term debt, including the current portion, divided by Adjusted EBITDA. Adjusted EBITDA, Debt to EBITDA and Debt to Adjusted EBITDA are a non-GAAP measures monitored by management as indicators of the Company’s overall financial strength.</li>
