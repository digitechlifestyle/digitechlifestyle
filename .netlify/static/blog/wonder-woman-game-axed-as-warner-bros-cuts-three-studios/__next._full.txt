<div data-component="text-block" class="sc-18fde0d6-0 dlWCEZ">
<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fYAfXe">Warner Bros Discovery has confirmed plans to shut three of its gaming development studios.<!-- --></p>
<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fYAfXe">The decision means the anticipated Wonder Woman game, which has been in production for three years at one of the affected studios, Monolith, will not be released. <!-- --></p>
<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fYAfXe">Player First Games, which made MultiVersus, and Warner Bros. Games San Diego will also be shuttered.<!-- --></p>
<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fYAfXe">The announcement comes after a difficult time in the industry, with widespread job losses as it deals with a slump in sales since the Covid pandemic. <!-- --></p>
</div>
<div data-component="text-block" class="sc-18fde0d6-0 dlWCEZ">
<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fYAfXe">A spokesperson from Warner Bros told the Reuters news agency the decision was aimed at boosting profitability.<!-- --></p>
<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fYAfXe">On Wonder Woman, they said: &#8220;Our hope was to give players and fans the highest quality experience possible for the iconic character, and unfortunately this is no longer possible within our strategic priorities.&#8221;<!-- --></p>
<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fYAfXe">Last month, MultiVersus <!-- --><a target="_blank" href="https://x.com/multiversus/status/1885327981758865568" class="sc-c9299ecf-0 bZUiKB">said there would be no more updates for the game<!-- --></a>, which would have to be played offline &#8220;for the foreseeable future&#8221;.<!-- --></p>
<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fYAfXe">Warner Bros said it will instead focus on developing its core franchises including Harry Potter, Mortal Kombat, DC and Game of Thrones. <!-- --></p>
</div>
<div data-component="text-block" class="sc-18fde0d6-0 dlWCEZ">
<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fYAfXe">Last year was a challenging one for the games sector, seeing games delayed, studios closed around the world and thousands of layoffs including at <!-- --><a target="_self" href="https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/newsbeat-68093970" class="sc-c9299ecf-0 bZUiKB">Microsoft&#8217;s Xbox<!-- --></a> and <!-- --><a target="_self" href="https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/technology-68404704" class="sc-c9299ecf-0 bZUiKB">Sony&#8217;s PlayStation<!-- --></a> . <!-- --></p>
<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fYAfXe">Warner Bros Games had big expectations for Suicide Squad: Kill the Justice League but the game was <!-- --><a target="_self" href="https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/newsbeat-68173718" class="sc-c9299ecf-0 bZUiKB">panned by fans and critics<!-- --></a>.<!-- --></p>
<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fYAfXe">Updates for the game ended in January after weak sales and poor reviews. <!-- --></p>
<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fYAfXe">And last month, the company announced the departure of David Haddad, the chief of the gaming unit, after 12 years.<!-- --></p>
<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fYAfXe">Wider trends in the industry have seen sales struggle as gamers spend less on new games, choosing instead to stick with long-running online games like Fortnite or yearly franchises such as Call of Duty and EA Sports FC.<!-- --></p>
<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fYAfXe">As well as people spending less on games since the pandemic, major video game makers including Warner Bros have been affected by <!-- --><a target="_self" href="https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c4ngy53qyq8o" class="sc-c9299ecf-0 bZUiKB">a rumbling row over the use of artificial intelligence in production<!-- --></a>.<!-- --></p>
<p class="sc-eb7bd5f6-0 fYAfXe">BBC Newsbeat has contacted Warner Bros Games for comment. <!-- --></p>
