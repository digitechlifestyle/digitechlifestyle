<p><img decoding="async" src="https://img.decrypt.co/insecure/rs:fill:1024:512:1:0/plain/https://cdn.decrypt.co/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Cynthia-Lummis-NBC-e1648850407380-gID_7.png@png" /></p>
<div style="position: relative; overflow: visible; font-size: 1.2em; line-height: 1.58;">
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100">Wyoming Senator Cynthia Lummis is reportedly being tapped to lead a new subcommittee focusing on digital assets.</p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://www.foxbusiness.com/politics/senate-banking-committee-launch-subcommittee-digital-assets" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><i>According to FOX Business,</i></a> this would be an offshoot of the Senate Banking Committee. It has a new chairman in the form of South Carolina&#8217;s Tim Scott, who has previously said &#8220;crypto has the potential to democratize the financial world.&#8221;</p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100">Scott <a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://www.banking.senate.gov/newsroom/minority/scott-meets-with-incoming-white-house-ai-and-crypto-czar-david-sacks" target="_blank" rel="noopener">recently held a meeting</a> with Donald Trump&#8217;s incoming crypto czar David Sacks, and appeared with Lummis at a panel of the Wyoming Blockchain Symposium last September.</p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100">At the time, he had said: &#8220;Wouldn&#8217;t it be kind of cool if we had a subcommittee on the banking committee that focuses on the industry, so that we bring more light to the conversation, more hearings on the industry, so we get things done faster?&#8221;</p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100">Such a subcommittee already exists in the larger House of Representatives, which is also undergoing a leadership change.</p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100">On Thursday, <a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://financialservices.house.gov/news/documentsingle.aspx?DocumentID=409431" target="_blank" rel="noopener">it was announced</a> that Wisconsin&#8217;s Bryan Steil will chair the House Subcommittee on Digital Assets, Financial Technology and Artificial Intelligence—with jurisdiction over crypto, stablecoins and CBDCs.</p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100">He succeeds Arkansas congressman French Hill, who in turn becomes chairman of the overarching House Financial Services Committee.</p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100">The <a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://www.standwithcrypto.org/politicians/person/bryan---steil" target="_blank" rel="nofollow external noopener">Stand With Crypto</a> campaign group has given Steil an &#8220;A&#8221; rating, indicating he strongly supports the digital assets sector, with the congressman previously warning that a lack of regulation risks &#8220;pushing opportunities overseas.&#8221;</p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100">After his new position was announced, <a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://steil.house.gov/media/press-releases/steil-named-chairman-of-the-subcommittee-on-digital-assets-financial-technology-and-artificial-intelligence" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Steil said</a>: &#8220;Innovation in the financial services sector provides an exciting opportunity for consumers and entrepreneurs, as technologies like financial apps, digital assets, and machine learning revolutionize our economy.&#8221;</p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100">The selection of Lummis and Steil to lead these two crypto-focused subcommittees is a sign that Donald Trump looks to capitalize on the Republicans&#8217; complete control of the government—especially when it comes to pushing through his pro-Bitcoin promises.</p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100">A regular on the crypto conference circuit, Lummis was an early adopter of BTC—and has led calls for the U.S. to create a strategic Bitcoin reserve through her proposed bills in the Senate.</p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://www.lummis.senate.gov/press-releases/lummis-introduces-strategic-bitcoin-reserve-legislation/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Last July,</a> she unveiled the Boosting Innovation, Technology and Competitiveness through Optimized Investment (BITCOIN) Act, which would require the government to purchase 1 million BTC over a five-year period. Lummis has stressed this $95 billion investment would not be taxpayer funded, and instead use existing funds across the Federal Reserve and the Treasury.</p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100">That goes further than what Trump himself has proposed, with the president-elect suggesting that about 200,000 BTC seized by criminals (with a current market value of $19.2 billion) could be converted into such a reserve.</p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><i>Edited by <a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://decrypt.co/author/stacy" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Stacy Elliott</a>.</i></p>
