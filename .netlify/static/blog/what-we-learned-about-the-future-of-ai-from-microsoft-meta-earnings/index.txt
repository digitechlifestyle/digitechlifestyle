<div id="mntl-sc-block_2-0" class="comp mntl-sc-block mntl-sc-block-callout mntl-block theme-whatyouneedtoknow" data-tracking-id="mntl-sc-block-callout" data-tracking-container="true">
<h3 id="mntl-sc-block-callout-heading_1-0" class="comp mntl-sc-block-callout-heading mntl-text-block">Key Takeaways</h3>
<div id="mntl-sc-block-callout-body_1-0" class="comp mntl-sc-block-callout-body mntl-text-block">
<ul>
<li>Microsoft, Meta, and IBM all highlighted healthy growth in their AI businesses in the last three months of 2024.</li>
<li>Microsoft and Meta, which cumulatively expect to invest nearly $150 billion in infrastructure in their respective 2025 fiscal years, stood by their aggressive spending plans.</li>
<li>Executives expressed confidence that Chinese start-up DeepSeek&#8217;s incredibly cost-efficient AI model, which spooked investors earlier this week, was ultimately a good thing for their businesses.</li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>
</div>
<p id="mntl-sc-block_3-0" class="comp mntl-sc-block finance-sc-block-html mntl-sc-block-html"> Artificial intelligence was the focus on Wednesday when tech giants Microsoft (<a href="https://www.investopedia.com/markets/quote?tvwidgetsymbol=MSFT" data-component="link" data-source="inlineLink" data-type="internalLink" data-ordinal="1">MSFT</a>) and Meta (<a href="https://www.investopedia.com/markets/quote?tvwidgetsymbol=META" data-component="link" data-source="inlineLink" data-type="internalLink" data-ordinal="2">META</a>) kicked off the first round of Big Tech earnings of 2025.
</p>
<p id="mntl-sc-block_5-0" class="comp mntl-sc-block finance-sc-block-html mntl-sc-block-html"> Analysts peppered executives with questions about their AI investments, how much AI is contributing to revenue, and their thoughts on Chinese start-up <a href="https://www.investopedia.com/what-is-deepseek-chinese-ai-startup-that-spurred-a-stock-selloff-nvidia-update-8780896" data-component="link" data-source="inlineLink" data-type="internalLink" data-ordinal="1">DeepSeek</a>, which shook up Wall Street earlier this week with its super-efficient AI model.
</p>
<p><span class="heading-toc" id="toc-ai-business-is-booming"/></p>
<h2 id="mntl-sc-block_7-0" class="comp mntl-sc-block finance-sc-block-heading mntl-sc-block-heading"> <span class="mntl-sc-block-heading__text"> AI Business Is Booming </span> </h2>
<p id="mntl-sc-block_8-0" class="comp mntl-sc-block finance-sc-block-html mntl-sc-block-html"> Executives were eager to show investors that artificial intelligence is contributing to their bottom lines.</p>
<p id="mntl-sc-block_10-0" class="comp mntl-sc-block finance-sc-block-html mntl-sc-block-html"> Meta CFO Susan Li said Meta AI reached 700 million monthly active users in the fourth quarter while the company’s AI-powered Advantage+ marketing service grew 70%. Meta&#8217;s quarterly revenue and earnings both <a href="https://www.investopedia.com/meta-earnings-q4-fy-2024-ai-spending-trump-lawsuit-update-8782681" data-component="link" data-source="inlineLink" data-type="internalLink" data-ordinal="1">surpassed analysts&#8217; expectations</a>.
</p>
<p id="mntl-sc-block_12-0" class="comp mntl-sc-block finance-sc-block-html mntl-sc-block-html"> Microsoft’s fiscal second-quarter cloud revenue <a href="https://www.investopedia.com/microsoft-earnings-q2-fy-2025-intelligent-cloud-azure-ai-update-nadella-8782490" data-component="link" data-source="inlineLink" data-type="internalLink" data-ordinal="1">fell slightly short of expectations</a>, but CEO Satya Nadella noted the company’s AI revenue run rate hit $13 billion in the quarter, well above the $10 billion mark he forecast in October.<span class="ql-inline-citation" data-cite="2"/>
</p>
<p id="mntl-sc-block_14-0" class="comp mntl-sc-block finance-sc-block-html mntl-sc-block-html"> IBM (<a href="https://www.investopedia.com/markets/quote?tvwidgetsymbol=IBM" data-component="link" data-source="inlineLink" data-type="internalLink" data-ordinal="1">IBM</a>) CEO Arvind Krishna said the company <a href="https://www.investopedia.com/ibm-stock-jumps-on-better-than-estimated-results-open-source-ai-strategy-8782925" data-component="link" data-source="inlineLink" data-type="internalLink" data-ordinal="2">booked nearly $2 billion</a> of AI business in the final quarter of 2024; that accounts for about 40% of the $5 billion booked since inception.<span class="ql-inline-citation" data-cite="1"/> 
</p>
<p><span class="heading-toc" id="toc-tech-titans-are-committed-to-their-ai-spending"/></p>
<h2 id="mntl-sc-block_16-0" class="comp mntl-sc-block finance-sc-block-heading mntl-sc-block-heading"> <span class="mntl-sc-block-heading__text"> Tech Titans Are Committed to Their AI Spending </span> </h2>
<p id="mntl-sc-block_17-0" class="comp mntl-sc-block finance-sc-block-html mntl-sc-block-html"> Executives from Microsoft and Meta stood by their plans to spend tens of billions of dollars this year on AI infrastructure.
</p>
<p id="mntl-sc-block_19-0" class="comp mntl-sc-block finance-sc-block-html mntl-sc-block-html"> “We expect to continue investing against strong demand signals,” Microsoft CFO Amy Hood said of its capital expenditures plan on the company’s earnings call. “However, the growth rate will be lower than FY2025.” 
</p>
<p id="mntl-sc-block_21-0" class="comp mntl-sc-block finance-sc-block-html mntl-sc-block-html"> Microsoft forecast spending $80 billion on infrastructure in its 2025 fiscal year. So far, two quarters in, the company has spent $30.7 billion, a 56% increase from a year ago.<span class="ql-inline-citation" data-cite="3"/> 
</p>
<p id="mntl-sc-block_23-0" class="comp mntl-sc-block finance-sc-block-html mntl-sc-block-html"> Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg also defended his company’s massive AI investments, which could total <a href="https://www.investopedia.com/meta-plans-to-spend-as-much-as-usd65b-in-defining-year-for-ai-says-zuckerberg-8780176" data-component="link" data-source="inlineLink" data-type="internalLink" data-ordinal="1">$65 billion</a> this year. “I continue to think that investing very heavily in CapEx and infra is going to be a strategic advantage over time,” he told analysts on Wednesday evening. 
</p>
<p id="mntl-sc-block_25-0" class="comp mntl-sc-block finance-sc-block-html mntl-sc-block-html"> Big tech’s AI spending was already a source of anxiety on Wall Street, where for half a year questions have swirled about whether demand will match the scale of infrastructure investments. Those doubts were amplified this week when markets took notice of DeepSeek, whose open-source AI model reportedly performs as well as leading U.S. models and runs at a fraction of the cost. 
</p>
<p id="mntl-sc-block_27-0" class="comp mntl-sc-block finance-sc-block-html mntl-sc-block-html"> Heading into Wednesday’s earnings reports, <a href="https://www.investopedia.com/will-msft-meta-amzn-goog-slow-ai-spending-deepseek-8782313" data-component="link" data-source="inlineLink" data-type="internalLink" data-ordinal="1">analysts doubted</a> that hyperscalers like Microsoft and Amazon (<a href="https://www.investopedia.com/markets/quote?tvwidgetsymbol=AMZN" data-component="link" data-source="inlineLink" data-type="internalLink" data-ordinal="2">AMZN</a>) would change their investment outlooks. DeepSeek’s efficiencies could fuel the development of AI applications and stoke demand further, giving cloud providers even more reason to continue aggressively adding computing capacity. 
</p>
<p><span class="heading-toc" id="toc-silicon-valley-impressed-by-deepseek-not-scared"/></p>
<h2 id="mntl-sc-block_29-0" class="comp mntl-sc-block finance-sc-block-heading mntl-sc-block-heading"> <span class="mntl-sc-block-heading__text"> Silicon Valley Impressed by DeepSeek, Not Scared </span> </h2>
<p id="mntl-sc-block_30-0" class="comp mntl-sc-block finance-sc-block-html mntl-sc-block-html"> Markets may have been shaken by DeepSeek, but tech executives weren’t. 
</p>
<p id="mntl-sc-block_32-0" class="comp mntl-sc-block finance-sc-block-html mntl-sc-block-html"> “In some sense, what&#8217;s happening with AI is no diﬀerent than what was happening with the regular compute cycle,” said Microsoft&#8217;s Nadella of DeepSeek’s efficiency. “We ourselves have been seeing significant efficiency gains in both training and inference for years now.” 
</p>
<p id="mntl-sc-block_34-0" class="comp mntl-sc-block finance-sc-block-html mntl-sc-block-html"> Nadella admitted that DeepSeek, whose model rivals the capabilities of Microsoft-backed OpenAI, “has had some real innovations” that he said should benefit his firm. “When token prices fall, inference computing prices fall. That means people can consume more, and there&#8217;ll be more apps written” that are supported by Microsoft’s infrastructure and services. 
</p>
<p id="mntl-sc-block_36-0" class="comp mntl-sc-block finance-sc-block-html mntl-sc-block-html"> Zuckerberg echoed Nadella’s praise for DeepSeek, saying he and his team were still digesting “a number of novel things that they did” that “we will hope to implement in our systems.”
</p>
<p id="mntl-sc-block_38-0" class="comp mntl-sc-block finance-sc-block-html mntl-sc-block-html"> Zuckerberg added that DeepSeek’s success vindicated Meta’s decision to make its large language model, Llama, open-source. DeepSeek was evidence, he said, that “there&#8217;s going to be an open source standard globally. And I think for our kind of own national advantage, it&#8217;s important that it&#8217;s an American standard.”
</p>
