<li><strong>Europe:</strong> 11pm CET / 12am (Thu) EET</li>
<li class=" last"><strong>Asia/Oceania:</strong> 7am (Thu) JST / 6am (Thu) AWST / 9am (Thu) AEDT</li>
</ul>
