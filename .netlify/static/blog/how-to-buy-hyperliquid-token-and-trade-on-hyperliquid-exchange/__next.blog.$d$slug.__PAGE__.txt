<p>With the rise of decentralized perpetual exchanges, <strong>Hyperliquid</strong> has positioned itself as a frontrunner for professional-grade, on-chain trading. Whether you’re looking to explore <strong>Hyperliquid crypto</strong>, acquire the <strong>Hyperliquid token</strong> (HYPE), or actively trade on the platform, this guide will walk you through everything from <strong>how to buy Hyperliquid</strong> to <strong>how to trade on Hyperliquid</strong>.</p>
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span id="What_Is_Hyperliquid" class="ez-toc-section"></span>What Is Hyperliquid?</h2>
<p><strong>Hyperliquid</strong> is a decentralized, high-performance trading platform built on its own custom Layer-1 blockchain (<strong>Hyperliquid L1</strong>). It offers <strong>perpetual futures</strong> on over 100 markets, zero gas fees, low latency, and features that rival the <a href="https://coincodecap.com/best-crypto-exchanges" data-type="post" data-id="6351">top centralized exchanges</a> — all while keeping funds in your control.</p>
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span id="How_to_Buy_Hyperliquid_Token_HYPE" class="ez-toc-section"></span>How to Buy Hyperliquid Token (HYPE)?</h2>
<p>The <strong>Hyperliquid token</strong>, often referred to as <strong>HYPE</strong>, is central to the ecosystem. While it’s not yet available on every major exchange, here’s how to buy it:</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><span id="Step-by-Step_How_to_Buy_Hyperliquid_Token" class="ez-toc-section"></span>Step-by-Step: How to Buy Hyperliquid Token</h3>
<ol class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Check Supported Exchanges</strong><br />
As of 2025, HYPE may be available on <strong>decentralized platforms</strong>, like Hyperliquid itself or DEX aggregators such as <a href="https://coincodecap.com/go/gmgn" target="_blank" rel="noopener">GMGN</a> or <a href="https://coincodecap.com/go/bullx" target="_blank" rel="noopener">BullX</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Connect Your Wallet</strong><br />
Use a supported Web3 wallet (e.g., <strong>MetaMask</strong>, <strong>Hyperliquid wallet app</strong>, or <strong>Rabby</strong>). Make sure you’re connected to <strong>Arbitrum</strong> if you’re using bridge access, or directly to <strong>Hyperliquid L1</strong> if using the native trading environment.</li>
<li><strong>Bridge Funds</strong><br />
If purchasing on the Hyperliquid platform, you’ll likely need to bridge <strong>USDC</strong> or <strong>ETH</strong> to Hyperliquid from <strong>Arbitrum</strong> or <strong>Ethereum</strong> using Hyperliquid’s native <strong>bridge</strong>.</li>
<li><strong>Buy via Hyperliquid or Supported DEX</strong><br />
Once funds are on Hyperliquid, use the <strong>spot trading interface</strong> (currently in beta) to purchase HYPE. Alternatively, buy HYPE on supported DEXes and transfer it to your <a href="https://coincodecap.com/ledger-vs-ngrave-zero" data-wpil-monitor-id="1887">wallet</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Store Safely</strong><br />
Store HYPE in your <strong>Hyperliquid wallet</strong> or a compatible wallet with support for <strong>Hyperliquid L1</strong> tokens.</li>
</ol>
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span id="How_to_Trade_on_Hyperliquid" class="ez-toc-section"></span>How to Trade on Hyperliquid</h2>
<p>Whether you’re a day trader or a professional market maker, <strong>trading on Hyperliquid</strong> is fast, intuitive, and packed with advanced features.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><span id="1_Supported_Markets" class="ez-toc-section"></span>1. Supported Markets</h3>
<p>Hyperliquid offers over <strong>100 perpetual markets</strong>, including:</p>
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Major cryptocurrencies: <strong>BTC, ETH, SOL, BNB</strong></li>
<li>DeFi tokens: <strong>UNI, AAVE, DYDX</strong></li>
<li>Cross-chain assets and selected <strong>forex</strong> pairs</li>
</ul>
<p>There is no <strong>spot trading</strong> in the traditional sense, but recently, <strong>spot functionality</strong> is being gradually rolled out for ETH and BTC.</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><span id="2_Trading_Fees_on_Hyperliquid" class="ez-toc-section"></span>2. Trading Fees on Hyperliquid</h3>
<p>Hyperliquid boasts some of the <strong>lowest trading fees</strong> in the decentralized exchange space:</p>
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Taker Fee</strong>: 0.045%</li>
<li><strong>Maker Fee</strong>: 0.015%</li>
<li>High-volume users qualify for <strong>tiered discounts</strong> or even <strong>maker rebates</strong></li>
<li><strong>Zero gas fees</strong> for trades and order placements — unlike Ethereum-based DEXs</li>
</ul>
<p>This makes Hyperliquid ideal for <strong>high-frequency trading</strong>, scalping, and algorithmic strategies.</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><span id="3_Leverage_and_Margin" class="ez-toc-section"></span>3. Leverage and Margin</h3>
<p>Hyperliquid supports up to <strong>50× leverage</strong> on most major assets. Some assets may be capped at lower levels depending on volatility. Users benefit from <strong>cross-margin</strong> accounts, and subaccounts can be used for <strong>isolated margin-like behavior</strong>.</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><span id="4_Deposit_and_Withdrawal" class="ez-toc-section"></span>4. Deposit and Withdrawal</h3>
<p>Depositing and withdrawing funds on Hyperliquid is seamless:</p>
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Deposits:</strong> USDC (via Arbitrum), BTC (via Bitcoin bridge), ETH (now supported for spot)</li>
<li><strong>Withdrawals:</strong> Flat $1 fee; sent back to your wallet on Arbitrum or Bitcoin</li>
<li>No third-party custodians involved</li>
</ul>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><span id="5_Chains_Supported" class="ez-toc-section"></span>5. Chains Supported</h3>
<p>Hyperliquid runs on its own <strong>Hyperliquid L1 chain</strong>, which is custom-built for trading:</p>
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>High throughput</strong> (sub-second finality)</li>
<li><strong>Gasless</strong> for all trading actions</li>
<li><strong>Bridges supported</strong>: Arbitrum (USDC), Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH for spot)</li>
</ul>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><span id="6_Trading_Tools_and_Order_Types" class="ez-toc-section"></span>6. Trading Tools and Order Types</h3>
<p>Hyperliquid offers a professional-grade suite of trading tools:</p>
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Central limit order book</strong> with real-time matching</li>
<li>Order Types:
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Market</strong></li>
<li><strong>Limit</strong></li>
<li><strong>Stop-loss</strong></li>
<li><strong>Take-profit</strong></li>
<li><strong><a class="wpil_keyword_link" title="TWAP" href="https://coincodecap.com/twap-trading-ii-the-art-of-twap-trading" data-wpil-keyword-link="linked" data-wpil-monitor-id="1892">TWAP</a></strong></li>
<li><strong>Scale orders</strong></li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
<p>Hyperliquid also offers an <strong>API (WebSocket &amp; REST)</strong> for developers and quants.</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><span id="7_Interface_and_User_Experience" class="ez-toc-section"></span>7. Interface and User Experience</h3>
<p>The interface is designed for performance and precision:</p>
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Resembles <a href="https://www.binance.com/en/activity/referral-entry/CPA?ref=CPA_00JDHMDHBA" data-type="post" data-id="7726">Binance</a> or <a href="https://www.bydfi.com/invite?ru=jvIn7g" data-type="post" data-id="99128">Bybit</a> with fully modular layout</li>
<li>Displays real-time <strong>order books</strong>, <strong>depth charts</strong>, and <strong>funding rates</strong></li>
<li>Users can toggle between <strong>spot</strong>, <strong>perp</strong>, and <strong>portfolio views</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Hyperliquid also supports <strong>subaccounts</strong>, useful for fund managers and strategy separation.</p>
