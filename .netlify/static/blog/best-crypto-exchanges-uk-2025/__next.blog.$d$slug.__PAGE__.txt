<h2>#1 Coinbase — Best for Beginners</h2>
<p>Coinbase is the world&#8217;s largest publicly-traded crypto exchange and is registered with the FCA. It&#8217;s the most beginner-friendly option available to UK users — the app is clean, buying is straightforward, and customer support is better than most competitors.</p>
<p><strong>Fees:</strong> Coinbase charges a spread of around 0.5–2% on simple buys through the main app. If you use Coinbase Advanced Trade (formerly Coinbase Pro, free to access), fees drop to 0.6% maker / 1% taker — significantly cheaper.</p>
<p><strong>Pros:</strong> FCA-registered, excellent app, 250+ coins, good security (97% of assets in cold storage), strong educational resources.</p>
<p><strong>Cons:</strong> Standard app fees are high. Customer support can be slow. Not the most coins available.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Anyone buying Bitcoin or Ethereum for the first time. If you just want a simple, safe place to buy crypto with a debit card or bank transfer, Coinbase is hard to beat.</p>

<h2>#2 Kraken — Best for Intermediate Users</h2>
<p>Kraken is one of the oldest and most respected exchanges in crypto. It&#8217;s FCA-registered, has never been hacked, and offers lower fees than Coinbase through its Pro interface (Kraken Pro, free to access).</p>
<p><strong>Fees:</strong> 0% maker / 0.26% taker on Kraken Pro. Standard Kraken fees are similar to Coinbase&#8217;s, so use the Pro view to save money.</p>
<p><strong>Pros:</strong> Rock-solid security record, low fees via Kraken Pro, wide coin selection (350+), staking available, good for GBP deposits via bank transfer.</p>
<p><strong>Cons:</strong> Interface less intuitive for beginners. App feels clunkier than Coinbase.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> UK users who&#8217;ve bought Bitcoin before and want lower fees or access to a wider range of altcoins. Also great if you want to stake Ethereum.</p>
<h2>#3 Crypto.com — Best for Rewards</h2>
<p>Crypto.com stands out for its Visa card, which gives cashback in crypto on every purchase — up to 8% depending on your card tier. If you&#8217;re spending money anyway, earning crypto back on it is genuinely useful.</p>
<p><strong>Fees:</strong> 0.075% for high-volume traders, 0.4% taker for most users. Competitive. Buying with card is more expensive (up to 2.99%).</p>
<p><strong>Pros:</strong> FCA-registered, Visa card with crypto cashback, crypto Earn (interest on holdings), large coin selection, regular promotions.</p>
<p><strong>Cons:</strong> Higher-tier card benefits require staking CRO (their native token). Some earn rates have been cut compared to earlier years.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<h3>Which crypto exchange is safest for UK beginners?</h3>
<p>Coinbase. It&#8217;s FCA-registered, publicly listed (NASDAQ: COIN), has never suffered a major hack, and stores 97% of customer assets in cold storage. It&#8217;s not perfect — fees can be high — but for safety, it&#8217;s the benchmark.</p>
<h3>Do I need ID to use a UK crypto exchange?</h3>
<p>Yes. All FCA-registered exchanges require identity verification (passport or driving licence + proof of address). This is a legal requirement under anti-money laundering rules, not something the exchange can opt out of.</p>
<h3>What&#8217;s the cheapest crypto exchange in the UK?</h3>
<p>For regular traders, Kraken Pro (0% maker fee) and Crypto.com are among the cheapest. Coinbase Advanced Trade is also much cheaper than the standard Coinbase app. Bitrue has very low fees but isn&#8217;t FCA-registered.</p>
