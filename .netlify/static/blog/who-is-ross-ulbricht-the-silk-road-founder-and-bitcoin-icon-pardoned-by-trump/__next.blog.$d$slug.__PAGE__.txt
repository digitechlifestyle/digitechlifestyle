<p><img decoding="async" src="https://img.decrypt.co/insecure/rs:fill:1024:512:1:0/plain/https://cdn.decrypt.co/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/shutterstock_354469922-scaled-gID_7.jpeg@png" /></p>
<div style="position: relative; overflow: visible; font-size: 1.2em; line-height: 1.58;">
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">You may have heard of Ross Ulbricht and how President Donald Trump just </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://decrypt.co/302197/trump-pardons-former-silk-road-founder-and-bitcoin-icon-ross-ulbricht" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">pardoned</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> him. </span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">But who is he, and what’s it got to do with </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://decrypt.co/resources/what-is-bitcoin-four-minute-instant-guide-explainer" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Bitcoin</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">? </span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">For years, the crypto community had been calling for Ulbricht to be freed after a judge in 2015 </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://www.ice.gov/news/releases/ross-ulbricht-aka-dread-pirate-roberts-sentenced-life-federal-prison-creating" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">sentenced</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> him to life in prison. </span></p>
<p class="sc-5a71bf1f-3 fdWwrx gg-dark:text-white scene:font-itc-avant-garde-gothic-pro scene:font-light" style="margin-top: 2em; text-align: left;">Who’s Ross?</p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Ross William Ulbricht, 40, grew up in Austin, Texas, and studied physics at the University of Texas before later getting a Master&#8217;s in material science at Pennsylvania State University, </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://freeross.org/" target="_blank" rel="nofollow external noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">according</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> to his website’s bio. </span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">He is described as having an early interest in “liberty, privacy, and free markets,” which later led him to discover Bitcoin. </span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">However, according to Ulbricht, he was also passionate about individual liberties, leading him to create a website called Silk Road.</span></p>
<p class="sc-5a71bf1f-3 fdWwrx gg-dark:text-white scene:font-itc-avant-garde-gothic-pro scene:font-light" style="margin-top: 2em; text-align: left;"><b>What was Silk Road? </b></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Silk Road was an online marketplace that could only be accessed via Tor—an anonymous web browser. The idea was that users could buy stuff anonymously and have it delivered—a bit like Amazon—but with no signup details needed to use the website.</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> </span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">But Silk Road wasn’t just any e-commerce website—it sold goods that one couldn’t find with a simple Google search.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Customers could search the website for anything from heroin and cocaine to all different kinds of strains of cannabis and have them delivered straight to their door. </span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">The site became synonymous with the “dark web” and everything that detractors thought was wrong with it. </span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">After running the website for years, The Feds in 2013 caught Ulbricht—who used the alias Dread Pirate Roberts when running the website—in a San Francisco library.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">He went on to spend the next two years in jail before getting hit with two life sentences plus 40 years for seven charges, including distributing narcotics, engaging in a continuing criminal enterprise, conspiring to commit computer hacking, and conspiring to commit money laundering.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Preet Bharara, U.S. Attorney for the Southern District of New York, said at the time that Ulbricht was “a drug dealer and criminal profiteer who exploited people’s addictions and contributed to the deaths of at least six young people” when he was jailed in 2015. </span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Perhaps most interestingly, authorities also </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://archives.fbi.gov/archives/newyork/press-releases/2013/manhattan-u.s.-attorney-announces-seizure-of-additional-28-million-worth-of-bitcoins-belonging-to-ross-william-ulbricht-alleged-owner-and-operator-of-silk-road-website" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">seized</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> 173,991 Bitcoins in connection with the site, which was worth over $33.6 million back then. (That stash is now valued at a lot more—over $18 billion—and is still in the hands of the Feds.) </span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">It was the most significant Bitcoin seizure by the U.S. government in history. </span></p>
<p class="sc-5a71bf1f-3 fdWwrx gg-dark:text-white scene:font-itc-avant-garde-gothic-pro scene:font-light" style="margin-top: 2em; text-align: left;"><b>Crypto community interest and President Trump’s pardon</b></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">To use Silk Road, customers could pay using Bitcoin—and Bitcoin only. The digital coin’s pseudonymous nature (where transactions are linked to wallet addresses made up of numbers and letters rather than names) was well suited for buying illegal goods. </span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">To this day, despite the illegality of the Silk Road case, the crypto community argues that Silk Road was a perfect use case of the orange coin’s flawless free market technology: a permissionless, censorship-resistant way of buying things on the web. </span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">For that reason, the Ulbricht case grabbed the attention of Bitcoin enthusiasts and OGs, from Roger “Bitcoin Jesus” Ver and Strike CEO Jack Mallers to Senator Rand Paul. </span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Ulbricht has talked extensively about Bitcoin—even behind bars. </span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">“Today, I take inspiration from Bitcoin. I will keep going, day by day, just taking the next step over and over. I will keep adding the next block,” he </span><a class="sc-adb616fe-0 bJsyml" href="https://rossulbricht.substack.com/p/block-by-block-facing-life-in-prison" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span style="font-weight: 400;">wrote</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> in one 2022 post. </span></p>

<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">It is a possible move to keep the crypto donations coming, or maybe for personal reasons. </span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Following Ulbricht’s unconditional pardon in January, Trump claimed the “scum” that locked up Ulbricht was the same “lunatics” that went after himself, hinting at the multiple criminal and civil cases filed against the then former president following the end of his first term.</span></p>
<p class="font-meta-serif-pro scene:font-noto-sans scene:text-base scene:md:text-lg font-normal text-lg md:text-xl md:leading-9 tracking-px text-body gg-dark:text-neutral-100"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Still, whatever the motive, the industry will likely be thanking the Republican leader for years to come. </span></p>
