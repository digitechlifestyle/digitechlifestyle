<p data-cy="paragraph" class="paragraph jsx-2269604527">Whether you&#8217;re talking about its fandom or its stars, <a class="link jsx-1337145738 jsx-3925284146 underlined" data-cy="styled-link" href="https://www.ign.com/games/monster-hunter-wilds"><u>Monster Hunter Wilds</u></a> is one of the biggest games of 2025, and it&#8217;s not even had its official release yet. The development team at Capcom aren&#8217;t slacking though, and IGN sat down with Producer Ryozo Tsujimoto and Director Yuya Tokuda at <a class="link jsx-1337145738 jsx-3925284146 underlined" data-cy="styled-link" href="https://www.ign.com/articles/ign-fan-fest-is-back-with-monster-hunter-wilds-daredevil-born-again-idxbox-and-more"><u>IGN Fan Fest</u></a> to find out what&#8217;s next for Monster Hunter Wilds once the game launches on February 28. There&#8217;s a new area that will have you wanting to pull on the thermal underwear, a special item pack for Fan Fest viewers, and a live-action trailer that will leave you more excited than a Lala Barina at a floristry convention. </p>
<p><output class="box-wrapper jsx-2673806401" data-cy="article-video"/></p>
<p data-cy="paragraph" class="paragraph jsx-2269604527">Brand new to the series, Iceshard Cliffs region is described as &#8220;a frigid land swept with snowy blizzards.&#8221; If you missed <a class="link jsx-1337145738 jsx-3925284146 underlined" data-cy="styled-link" href="https://www.ign.com/videos/monster-hunter-wilds-new-monster-official-reveal-trailer-capcom-spotlight-2025"><u>the trailer that gave us a sneak peek at it</u></a>, think gorges constructed of ice and rock, ruins peeking through glaciers, and treacherous looking caves.</p>
<p data-cy="paragraph" class="paragraph jsx-2269604527">&#8220;The new locale is called the Iceshard Cliffs, which has [a lot of] cold weather, and during the inclemency, which is the harsh weather in the game, it kind of looks very dark, especially in the trailers that were revealed, but when you&#8217;re actually playing it&#8217;s very dynamic in the colors, and you can see a different side of the locale as you&#8217;re playing,&#8221; promises Tokuda.</p>
<p data-cy="paragraph" class="paragraph jsx-2269604527">The scenery might be desktop wallpaper worthy, but what hardened Monster Hunters really care about is the wildlife roaming around those snowy sites, and Monster Hunter Wilds&#8217; Iceshard Cliffs sounds like they won&#8217;t be disappointing anyone on that front. </p>
<p data-cy="paragraph" class="paragraph jsx-2269604527">&#8220;There are, outside of the returning monsters that have the ice element, there are some other monsters that were also shown in the trailer, such as the flying Leviathan-type monster, Hirabami,&#8221; says Tokuda.</p>
<p data-cy="paragraph" class="paragraph jsx-2269604527">&#8220;Hirabamis move in herds, and you can see in the trailer, but there&#8217;s also a larger monster behind the herd of the Hirabami, which is the apex monster of the Ice Shard Cliffs, and it&#8217;s a very large monster and has various attacks, and we hope everybody enjoys it.&#8221;</p>
<p><output class="box-wrapper jsx-2673806401" data-cy="article-video"/></p>
<h2 data-cy="title2" class="title2 jsx-1903782357 jsx-3735650234"><strong>Gaga for Gore Magala</strong></h2>
<p data-cy="paragraph" class="paragraph jsx-2269604527">Since the interview, it&#8217;s been revealed that the new apex monster is called <a class="link jsx-1337145738 jsx-3925284146 underlined" data-cy="styled-link" href="https://www.ign.com/wikis/monster-hunter-wilds/Jin_Dahaad"><u>Jin Dahaad</u></a>, which looks kind of like if a dragon fell into a silverware drawer and then had a nap in your freezer. Another monster fans might have spotted is our old friend Gore Magala, who appeared in Monster Hunter 4. </p>
<p data-cy="paragraph" class="paragraph jsx-2269604527">&#8220;We also had a popularity poll with the monsters, and it was in fifth place, and it&#8217;s also very popular in the North American region, and we hope everybody looks forward to fighting this monster,&#8221; added Tsujimoto.</p>
<p data-cy="paragraph" class="paragraph jsx-2269604527">Tsujimoto and Tokuda also had some advice for players facing a couple of the tougher monsters in Monster Hunter Wilds &#8211; the &#8216;White Wraith&#8217; Arkveld and Nerscylla &#8211; either during the beta or when the game is released in a few days.</p>
<p data-cy="paragraph" class="paragraph jsx-2269604527">&#8220;So in Monster Hunter Wilds, we have the flagship monster, Arkveld,&#8221; says Tokuda.</p>
<p data-cy="paragraph" class="paragraph jsx-2269604527">&#8220;It wasn&#8217;t completely tricky, but it sometimes goes on with full-strength attacks. It has, like, a mix of both. The attacks that Arkveld uses, it uses its wings, which can stretch from short to pretty long. And it has a wide range of attacks, so it&#8217;s really hard to get used to at first. Hopefully, during the open beta test, too, everybody enjoyed fighting Arkveld. I think that using SOS flares to have other people play with you and help you hunt, enjoying multiplayer, really helps with this monster.</p>
<p><output class="box-wrapper jsx-2673806401"></p>
<figure class="jsx-313219616"><a href="https://assets-prd.ignimgs.com/2025/02/22/monster-hunter-1740253087659.png" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><img decoding="async" alt="undefined" src="https://assets-prd.ignimgs.com/2025/02/22/monster-hunter-1740253087659.png?width=1280&amp;fit=bounds&amp;height=720&amp;quality=20&amp;dpr=0.05" data-cy="progressive-image" class="jsx-2920405963 progressive-image article-image article-image-full-size jsx-1809694635 jsx-2338608387 loading"/></a><figcaption data-cy="caption" class="caption jsx-1762799490 jsx-479945570 article-image-caption">An exclusive look at Uth Duna from Monster Hunter Wilds revealed at IGN Fan Fest 2025. </figcaption></figure>
<p></output></p>
<p data-cy="paragraph" class="paragraph jsx-2269604527">&#8220;This also applies for other large monsters, but when the large monsters have big attacks, they tend to be a little bit more vulnerable to focus strikes, which then you can use to deal more damage to it. So it&#8217;s a good chance for you to deal more damage when the monsters do a very big attack. And on top of that, you can also carry two weapons in Monster Hunter Wilds, so you can have one weapon as your choice and the secondary weapon to be an element weapon so that you can deal damage according to the monster.&#8221;</p>
<p data-cy="paragraph" class="paragraph jsx-2269604527">There was also some advice for the arachnophobes dealing with the Temnoceran Nerscylla, an agile prey with sleep and poison attacks. </p>
<p data-cy="paragraph" class="paragraph jsx-2269604527">&#8220;Nerscylla uses many elements that can put players to sleep, and that can be difficult sometimes,” explains Tsujimoto. “So when you face a monster that uses elements, it&#8217;s nice if you can use items, but I also think that because we have Seikret introduced in Monster Hunter Wilds, while you&#8217;re riding on the Seikret, Seikret can automatically evade monster attacks, some of the monster attacks.”</p>
<p data-cy="paragraph" class="paragraph jsx-2269604527">&#8220;So when your health is running low, you can hop on your Seikret and use potions while riding it and also fix your status, so using Seikret to your benefit is also a very strong point when fighting monsters.&#8221;</p>
<p data-cy="paragraph" class="paragraph jsx-2269604527">Monster Hunter Wilds is available for pre-order now and will be released on February 28, 2025, for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and Steam, and there&#8217;s already a treasure trove of extra content waiting to keep the adventure going. The first is a spring update that will add water monster Mizutsune to the Forbidden Lands, with Event Quests and unspecified additional updates to follow. Another monster will join the party in summer, and Capcom has some other delights up its armored sleeves, too. </p>
