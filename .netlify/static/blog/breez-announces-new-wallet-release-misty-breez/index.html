Security Enhancements in the New Wallet Release

Your digital asset safety is our mission. Misty Breez builds wallet security into every layer of its design. We start with full self-custody on the Liquid sidechain. Protect your crypto like a fortress—without sacrificing convenience. See how we turned security into a seamless experience:

Multi-factor authentication options

Lock your funds with layers of protection. Choose from biometric scans, one-time codes, or hardware keys to verify each login. Think of it like needing a key AND a password to open your digital safe.

Encryption technology

We encrypt your data using military-grade FROST algorithms. This Schnorr-based threshold signing system scrambles transactions so hackers can’t crack the code—even if they try 10,000 guesses a second. Your keys stay private, your funds stay locked tight.

Backup and recovery solutions

Lost your phone? No problem. Our backup solutions let you recover access without losing your public identity. Using Frostr’s trustless key rotation, you can reset passwords or replace keys instantly—all while keeping your NFTs and crypto history intact. No more “ forgotten password” panic attacks.

“Security isn’t just a feature—it’s our foundation.”

Every choice from PIN codes to cold storage backups follows one rule: you control your keys, we handle the math. That’s how Misty Breez makes digital asset safety feel as simple as locking your front door.
