<p><a class="editor-rtfLink" href="https://www.google.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span data-preserver-spaces="true">Google</span></a><span data-preserver-spaces="true"> said Wednesday (March 5) that it launched two updates for its artificial intelligence features: expanded AI Overviews and a limited, experimental offering called </span><a class="editor-rtfLink" href="https://labs.google.com/search/experiment/22" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span data-preserver-spaces="true">AI Mode</span></a><span data-preserver-spaces="true">.</span></p>
<p><span data-preserver-spaces="true">“With our new AI features, people are using Google Search more than ever as they get help with new and more complex questions,” </span><a class="editor-rtfLink" href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/robbystein" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span data-preserver-spaces="true">Robby Stein</span></a><span data-preserver-spaces="true">, vice president of product for Google Search, wrote in a Wednesday </span><a class="editor-rtfLink" href="https://blog.google/products/search/ai-mode-search/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span data-preserver-spaces="true">blog post</span></a><span data-preserver-spaces="true">. “AI Overviews are one of our most popular Search features — now used by more than a billion people — and we’re continuing to advance and improve the experience to make them even better.”</span></p>
<p><span data-preserver-spaces="true">The newly launched </span><a class="editor-rtfLink" href="https://blog.google/technology/google-deepmind/google-gemini-ai-update-december-2024/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span data-preserver-spaces="true">Gemini 2.0</span></a><span data-preserver-spaces="true"> for AI Overviews is now available in the United States to help with </span><span data-preserver-spaces="true">harder</span><span data-preserver-spaces="true"> questions like coding, advanced math and multimodal queries</span><span data-preserver-spaces="true">, according to the post</span><span data-preserver-spaces="true">.</span></p>
<p><span data-preserver-spaces="true">This</span><span data-preserver-spaces="true"> expanded AI Overviews provides faster and higher quality responses, shows AI Overviews more often for </span><span data-preserver-spaces="true">harder</span><span data-preserver-spaces="true"> questions, is available to teens and no longer requires users to sign in to get access, the post said.</span></p>
<p><span data-preserver-spaces="true">The new, experimental Search mode, AI Mode, which uses a custom version of Gemini 2.0, can help with nuanced questions, answer follow-up questions and provide relevant web links, according to the post.</span></p>
<p><span data-preserver-spaces="true">AI Mode can access web content, shopping data and other real-time </span><span data-preserver-spaces="true">information;</span><span data-preserver-spaces="true"> issue multiple related searches </span><span data-preserver-spaces="true">concurrently;</span><span data-preserver-spaces="true"> and bring the results together in its response, per the post.</span></p>
<p><span data-preserver-spaces="true">This</span><span data-preserver-spaces="true"> enables AI Mode to answer queries like, per the post, “What’s the difference in sleep tracking features between a smart ring, smartwatch and tracking mat?”</span></p>
<p><span data-preserver-spaces="true">AI Mode will </span><span data-preserver-spaces="true">be made</span><span data-preserver-spaces="true"> available, beginning Wednesday, to </span><a class="editor-rtfLink" href="https://one.google.com/about/ai-premium/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span data-preserver-spaces="true">Google One AI Premium</span></a><span data-preserver-spaces="true"> subscribers who receive an invitation, per the post.</span></p>
<p><span data-preserver-spaces="true">“We’ve been getting feedback internally and from trusted testers, and they’ve found AI Mode incredibly helpful — they particularly appreciate the speed, quality and freshness of responses,” Stein wrote in the post.</span></p>
<p><span data-preserver-spaces="true">AI Overviews was launched in the U.S. in May to provide an </span><a class="editor-rtfLink" href="https://www.pymnts.com/google/2024/google-updates-ai-overviews-with-more-prominent-links-expanded-availability/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span data-preserver-spaces="true">AI-generated summary</span></a><span data-preserver-spaces="true"> of information from several sources </span><span data-preserver-spaces="true">along with</span><span data-preserver-spaces="true"> search results.</span></p>
<p><span data-preserver-spaces="true">Google reported in August that this feature was leading users to visit a greater diversity of websites for help with complex questions and was providing higher-quality clicks for websites.</span></p>
