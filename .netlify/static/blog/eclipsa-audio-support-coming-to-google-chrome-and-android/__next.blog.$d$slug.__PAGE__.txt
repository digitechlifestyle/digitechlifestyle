<p>Following the <a href="https://9to5google.com/2025/01/03/samsung-debut-eclipsa-audio-on-2025-tvs/">announcement by Samsung</a> at the start of 2025, Google <a href="https://opensource.googleblog.com/2025/01/introducing-eclipsa-audio-immersive-audio-for-everyone.html">earlier this week shared </a>more details about Eclipsa Audio. </p>
<p><span id="more-657774"/></p>
<p>This is Google and Samsung’s “open source spatial audio format for everyone.”</p>
<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p>Eclipsa Audio is based on <a href="https://aomediacodec.github.io/iamf/v1.0.0-errata.html">Immersive Audio Model and Formats (IAMF)</a>, an audio format developed by Google, Samsung, and other key contributors within the Alliance for Open Media (AOM), and released under the AOM royalty-free license.</p>
</blockquote>
<p>The Open Audio team at Google explains how “spatial audio technologies were first developed over 50 years ago, and playback has been available to consumers for over a decade.” However, “creating spatial audio has been mostly limited to professionals in the movie or music industries.”</p>
<p>“Eclipsa Audio files can be created by anyone using <a href="https://github.com/AOMediaCodec/iamf-tools">freely available audio tools</a>,” while Google plans to release a free Eclipsa Audio plugin for AVID Pro Tools Digital Audio Workstation in the spring. In the meantime:</p>

<p class="has-text-align-center"><em>“wide variety of workflows” supported</em></p>
<p>YouTube previously announced that “creators will be able to upload videos with Eclipsa Audio tracks.” </p>
<p>In addition to Samsung’s 2025 TV lineup (Crystal UHD series and Neo QLED 8K models), native Eclipsa Audio playback is coming to Chrome this year. Google did not specify a (desktop and/or mobile) form factor. Meanwhile, Eclipsa Audio support will “arrive in an upcoming Android AOSP release.” </p>
<p>Google also says to expect support from “TVs and Soundbars from multiple manufacturers later in 2025.” Samsung and Google will be launching a certification and brand licensing program to “provide quality assurance to manufacturers and consumers for products that support Eclipsa Audio.”</p>
