<p data-cy="paragraph" class="paragraph jsx-2269604527"><a class="link jsx-1337145738 jsx-3925284146 underlined" data-cy="styled-link" href="https://www.ign.com/games/assassins-creed-shadows">Assassin’s Creed Shadows</a> saw over 1 million players on launch day, Ubisoft has announced.</p>
<p data-cy="paragraph" class="paragraph jsx-2269604527">The game released on PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X and S on March 20, and just before 4pm in Canada, <a href="https://x.com/assassinscreed/status/1902802072640295067" class="link jsx-1337145738 jsx-3925284146 underlined" data-cy="styled-link" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Ubisoft took to social media</a> to say it had crossed the 1 million player mark.</p>
<p data-cy="paragraph" class="paragraph jsx-2269604527">“It&#8217;s not even 4pm here in Canada and Assassin&#8217;s Creed Shadows has already passed 1 million players!” Ubisoft said.</p>
<p data-cy="paragraph" class="paragraph jsx-2269604527">“Thank YOU from the bottom of our hearts for joining this adventure in Feudal Japan. We are beyond excited to start this journey with you!”</p>
<p><output class="box-wrapper jsx-2673806401" data-cy="article-video"/></p>
<p data-cy="paragraph" class="paragraph jsx-2269604527">While 1 million players on launch day is an impressive achievement, it’s difficult to gauge success when Ubisoft itself hasn’t offered a sales figure or indeed a sales target.</p>
<p data-cy="paragraph" class="paragraph jsx-2269604527">What we do know is that Assassin’s Creed Shadows is the top-selling video game globally on Steam right now. Valve’s chart sorts products by revenue, so we can say Ubisoft’s game is currently bringing in more money than any other on the platform.</p>
<p data-cy="paragraph" class="paragraph jsx-2269604527">We also have early days Steam concurrent figures. According to official stats from Valve’s platform, Assassin’s Creed Shadows hit a peak player concurrent figure of 41,412 on Steam on launch day. Assassin’s Creed Shadows released on a Thursday, so it is expected that that figure will grow as we head into the game’s first weekend on sale. We’ll have a better picture of its performance on Steam next week and in the weeks ahead. Neither Sony nor Microsoft make player numbers public.</p>
<p data-cy="paragraph" class="paragraph jsx-2269604527">For some context, BioWare’s single-player RPG <a class="link jsx-1337145738 jsx-3925284146 underlined" data-cy="styled-link" href="https://www.ign.com/games/dragon-age-the-veilguard">Dragon Age: The Veilguard</a> launched on Steam on Thursday, October 31, 2024, and saw a peak of 70,414 players on Valve’s platform.</p>

<p data-cy="paragraph" class="paragraph jsx-2269604527">There is enormous pressure on Assassin&#8217;s Creed Shadows to do well for Ubisoft globally after multiple delays and the sales failure of last year’s <a class="link jsx-1337145738 jsx-3925284146 underlined" data-cy="styled-link" href="https://www.ign.com/games/star-wars-outlaws"><u>Star Wars Outlaws</u></a>. Indeed, Ubisoft has suffered a number of <a class="link jsx-1337145738 jsx-3925284146 underlined" data-cy="styled-link" href="https://www.ign.com/articles/ubisoft-admits-star-wars-outlaws-underperformed"><u>high-profile flops</u></a>, <a class="link jsx-1337145738 jsx-3925284146 underlined" data-cy="styled-link" href="https://www.ign.com/articles/ubisoft-cuts-45-jobs-in-its-us-offices"><u>layoffs</u></a>, <a class="link jsx-1337145738 jsx-3925284146 underlined" data-cy="styled-link" href="https://www.ign.com/articles/ubisoft-is-discontinuing-xdefiant-in-2025-new-purchases-disabled"><u>studio closures</u></a>, and <a class="link jsx-1337145738 jsx-3925284146 underlined" data-cy="styled-link" href="https://www.ign.com/articles/ubisoft-says-delays-cancellations-were-because-it-was-making-too-many-games-at-once"><u>game cancellations</u></a> in the run up to Assassin&#8217;s Creed Shadows&#8217; release. Things have got so bad at Ubisoft that its founding Guillemot family and largest shareholder <a class="link jsx-1337145738 jsx-3925284146 underlined" data-cy="styled-link" href="https://www.ign.com/articles/ubisoft-shareholder-plots-protest-outside-paris-hq-accuses-company-of-failing-to-reveal-discussions-with-microsoft-ea-and-others-allegedly-interested-in-acquiring-ips">have reportedly been exploring talks with Chinese mega-corp Tencent and other investors</a> on a buyout deal that would let them preserve control.</p>
<p data-cy="paragraph" class="paragraph jsx-2269604527">Assassin&#8217;s Creed Shadows has also endured a number of controversies, not least in Japan. Yesterday, IGN confirmed that <a class="link jsx-1337145738 jsx-3925284146 underlined" data-cy="styled-link" href="https://www.ign.com/articles/amid-japan-concern-about-assassins-creed-shadows-ubisoft-confirms-day-one-patch-that-makes-tables-and-racks-in-temples-and-shrines-indestructible">Ubisoft had quietly released a day-one patch for <u>Assassin’s Creed Shadows</u></a> that made a number of important changes seemingly in response to concern from some Japanese politicians about in-game temples and shrines.</p>
<p data-cy="paragraph" class="paragraph jsx-2269604527">In a remarkable exchange this week, Japanese politician and member of the House of Councillors of Japan, Hiroyuki Kada, raised the issue of Assassin’s Creed Shadows in an official government meeting, and <a class="link jsx-1337145738 jsx-3925284146 underlined" data-cy="styled-link" href="https://www.ign.com/articles/the-prime-minister-of-japan-responded-to-a-question-about-assassins-creed-shadows-today-heres-whats-actually-going-on">Shigeru Ishiba, the Prime Minister of Japan, responded</a>.</p>
