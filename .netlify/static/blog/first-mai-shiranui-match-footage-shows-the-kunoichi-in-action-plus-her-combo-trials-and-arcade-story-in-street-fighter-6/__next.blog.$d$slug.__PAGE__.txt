<p>&#13;<br />

<p>Street Fighter 6 players need to wait almost two more weeks to get their hands on <a href="https://www.eventhubs.com/news/2025/jan/13/mai-shiranui-sf6-gameplay/">Mai Shiranui</a> themselves, but they can get a taste of what&#8217;s to come now. &#13;<br />
&#13;<br />
    &#13;<br />
                &#13;
                    </p>
<div id="abcddim2abcd">
<!-- Tag ID: EventHubs_300x250_300x600_ATF --></p></div>
<p>&#13;<br />
               &#13;<br />
        &#13;<br />
      &#13;<br />
&#13;</p>
<p>After getting to see <a href="https://www.eventhubs.com/news/2025/jan/23/mai-movelist-sf6/">Mai&#8217;s full movelist</a>, multiple Japanese publications are sharing out actual match footage (including some in 4K) and demo details for SF6&#8217;s new DLC character, which reveals quite a bit of what she&#8217;s capable of (as well as some of her potential weaknesses). </p>
<p>&#13;<br />
	  &#13;<br />
	&#13;<br />
	&#13;<br />
     &#13;<br />
          &#13;<br />
     &#13;<br />
     &#13;<br />
&#13;</p>
<p>Here are some of our big takeaways from the videos thus far:</p>
<p>&#13;<br />
&#13;</p>
<p>Kachousen fan toss can be negated by physical attacks and destroyed by other projectiles unless she holds down the charge, which also pops up for the follow-up hit if it makes contact with the opponent.&#13;
</p>
<p>&#13;<br />
&#13;</p>
<p>It doesn&#8217;t appear as though the falling fan hits as an overhead.&#13;
</p>
<p>&#13;<br />
&#13;</p>
<p>Pressing forward plus punch after her Overdrive fan toss gives her Midare Kachousen that throws two more additional fans that both bounce up into the air.&#13;
</p>
<p>&#13;<br />
&#13;</p>
<p>Ryuuenbu is shown to destroy projectiles with the heavy and OD versions allowing for follow-ups. &#13;
</p>
<p>&#13;<br />
&#13;</p>
<p>OD Hishou Ryuuenjin is a launcher that allows her to continue combos in the air like Musasabi no Mai and her level 2 Super.&#13;
</p>
<p>&#13;<br />
&#13;</p>
<p>Musasabi no Mai can be made safe on block if spaced correctly and knocks down on hit while the OD version allows for more follow-ups.&#13;
</p>
<p>&#13;<br />
&#13;</p>
<p>It appears as though only Mai&#8217;s level 1 Super grants her Flame Stocks while her level 2 Super doesn&#8217;t seem to have invincibility against projectiles (unless she has stocks to burn).&#13;
</p>
<p>&#13;<br />
&#13;</p>
<p>All of Mai&#8217;s specials and first two Supers get buffed by consuming Flame Stocks like giving her fans more hits and more damage on most of the rest of her moves.&#13;
</p>
<p>&#13;<br />
&#13;</p>
<p>Mai&#8217;s Fire Stocks she receives from level 1 and 2 Supers do not carry over between rounds&#13;
</p>
<p>&#13;<br />
&#13;</p>
<p>On top of the match footage, <a href="https://www.youtube.com/@4GamerMovie">4Gamer</a> and <a href="https://www.youtube.com/@dengekionline">Dengeki Online</a> were able to capture over a dozen of Mai&#8217;s combo trials in both Classic and Modern controls.</p>
<p>&#13;<br />
&#13;</p>
<p>And they showcase her Arcade Mode story scenes as well chasing down Andy Bogard and getting into a fight with Juri.</p>
