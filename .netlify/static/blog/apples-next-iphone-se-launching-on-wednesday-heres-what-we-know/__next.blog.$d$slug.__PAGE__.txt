<div data-io-article-url="/2025/02/14/iphone-se-coming-wednesday-rumor-roundup/">
<p>Apple CEO <a href="https://www.macrumors.com/guide/tim-cook/">Tim Cook</a> <a href="https://www.macrumors.com/2025/02/13/apple-launch-february-19/">teased an Apple announcement</a> that&#8217;s coming on Wednesday, February 19, and it&#8217;s looking like that mystery announcement will be the next-generation <a href="https://www.macrumors.com/roundup/iphone-se/">iPhone SE</a>.</p>
<p><img fetchpriority="high" decoding="async" src="https://images.macrumors.com/t/cw8ukggoUZrvm_DfdptTGqGEADY=/400x0/article-new/2024/05/iPhone-SE-4-Thumb-1.jpg?lossy" srcset="https://images.macrumors.com/t/cw8ukggoUZrvm_DfdptTGqGEADY=/400x0/article-new/2024/05/iPhone-SE-4-Thumb-1.jpg?lossy 400w,https://images.macrumors.com/t/aI0j9WQTi5DjPnEL5sdIucbU4MI=/800x0/article-new/2024/05/iPhone-SE-4-Thumb-1.jpg?lossy 800w,https://images.macrumors.com/t/0bVfclhEOD9H8xPSCp9RDHbBYgc=/1600x0/article-new/2024/05/iPhone-SE-4-Thumb-1.jpg 1600w,https://images.macrumors.com/t/uGPnamfZwFMKnN2WpED3l_uP17Q=/2500x0/filters:no_upscale()/article-new/2024/05/iPhone-SE-4-Thumb-1.jpg 2500w" sizes="(max-width: 900px) 100vw, 697px" alt="iPhone SE 4 Thumb 1" width="2500" height="1406" class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-951978"/><br />We&#8217;ve been hearing about the ‌iPhone SE‌ 4 for quite some time now, and we essentially know everything to expect. If you want a sneak peek at what&#8217;s coming, read on.</p>
<h2>Naming</h2>
<p>Apple first introduced the ‌iPhone SE‌ in 2016, and the &#8220;SE&#8221; stands for Special Edition. Apple has used the SE naming for the last three models, but it&#8217;s possible that&#8217;s going to change this year.</p>
<p>There have been rumors that the new low-cost <a href="https://www.macrumors.com/guide/iphone/">iPhone</a> <a href="https://www.macrumors.com/2025/01/01/iphone-se-4-rumored-to-be-named-iphone-16e/">could be called</a> the &#8220;‌iPhone‌ 6E&#8221; instead of the ‌iPhone SE‌ 4, but the information came from a source without a well-established track record.</p>
<p>The low-cost ‌iPhone‌ will have the same chip as the <a href="https://www.macrumors.com/roundup/iphone-16/">iPhone 16</a>, along with a few other ‌iPhone 16‌ features, so it would not be a shock for Apple to give it a new name to better tie it into current flagship models. When announcing the upcoming product launch, Cook also said &#8220;get ready to meet the newest member of the family,&#8221; which could be a reference to the ‌iPhone 16‌ family. At the same time, &#8220;family&#8221; could also just be the ‌iPhone‌ family.</p>
<h2>Design</h2>
<p>We&#8217;re expecting the ‌iPhone SE‌ to have an ‌iPhone‌ 14-style design, with a 6.1-inch display. Its launch will mark the end of smaller iPhones, and 6.1 inches will be the smallest screen size that Apple offers.</p>
<p>While the current ‌iPhone SE‌ is 4.7 inches, the size difference between the 4.7-inch model and the new 6.1-inch model isn&#8217;t as extreme as you might think. The updated size will be noticeable, but getting rid of those thick bezels means there&#8217;s a limited increase in physical size despite the larger display.</p>

<p>An ‌iPhone SE‌ 4 dummy model</p>
<p></em><br />The ‌iPhone SE‌ is 5.45 inches tall and 2.65 inches wide with a thickness of 7.3mm, and the new model will be 5.78 inches tall, 2.82 inches wide, and a thickness of 7.8mm. Weight will increase just an ounce.</p>
<p>Apple used to sell a 5.4-inch ‌iPhone‌ &#8220;mini,&#8221; but it was discontinued after the ‌iPhone‌ 13 mini because it didn&#8217;t sell well. In the future, Apple may appease those who want a small ‌iPhone‌ with a clamshell device that folds in half, but rumors suggest that such a device is <a href="https://www.macrumors.com/guide/foldable-iphone/">a year or more off</a>.</p>
<p>If you want to know what the ‌iPhone SE‌ will look like, take a look at the <a href="https://www.macrumors.com/roundup/iphone-14/">iPhone 14</a>. It&#8217;ll have the same general design with an all-display design, aluminum and glass build, mute switch, volume buttons, notch, power button, and 5G antenna/SIM tray. The ‌iPhone SE‌ 4 is expected to have a single-lens rear camera, though, rather than the dual-lens setup of the ‌iPhone 14‌.</p>

<h3>Display</h3>
<p>The ‌iPhone 14‌ introduced Ceramic Shield, which is stronger than typical smartphone glass and better able to hold up to drops and scratches, and it&#8217;s likely the low-cost ‌iPhone‌ will adopt the Ceramic Shield material.</p>
<p>Apple is updating the ‌iPhone SE‌ with an OLED display, which means the end of LCD display technology in the ‌iPhone‌ lineup when the current model is discontinued. LCDs are still used for some Macs and iPads, but the ‌iPhone‌ lineup will be all OLED when the SE 4 launches. </p>

There were rumors suggesting Apple could use the <a href="https://www.macrumors.com/guide/dynamic-island/">Dynamic Island</a> for the ‌iPhone SE‌, but that&#8217;s another feature that looks like it&#8217;ll be reserved for the more expensive ‌iPhone‌ options.</p>
<h3>No More Touch ID</h3>
<p>With an all-display design like the ‌iPhone 14‌, Apple is eliminating <a href="https://www.macrumors.com/guide/touch-id/">Touch ID</a>. When the current-generation ‌iPhone SE‌ is discontinued in favor of the new model, there will be no ‌iPhone‌ with ‌Touch ID‌ in the lineup.</p>
<p><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" alt="iphone se 7" width="3840" height="2160" class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-841340 lazyload" src="https://images.macrumors.com/t/s7IaJcVXLJFwShd9MC-ueTj1Pg0=/400x0/article-new/2022/03/iphone-se-7.jpg?lossy" srcset="https://images.macrumors.com/t/s7IaJcVXLJFwShd9MC-ueTj1Pg0=/400x0/article-new/2022/03/iphone-se-7.jpg?lossy 400w,https://images.macrumors.com/t/srT2wlJXq_SfKuucT5D6RwYnJUE=/800x0/article-new/2022/03/iphone-se-7.jpg?lossy 800w,https://images.macrumors.com/t/tSZQ9xa59ykOG1ewQiZHCQ4Sne0=/1600x0/article-new/2022/03/iphone-se-7.jpg 1600w,https://images.macrumors.com/t/vQAm4a1UHey3AiygzyGbLr9n3xA=/2500x0/filters:no_upscale()/article-new/2022/03/iphone-se-7.jpg 2500w" data-sizes="auto" loading="lazy"/><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" src="https://images.macrumors.com/t/s7IaJcVXLJFwShd9MC-ueTj1Pg0=/400x0/article-new/2022/03/iphone-se-7.jpg?lossy" srcset="https://images.macrumors.com/t/s7IaJcVXLJFwShd9MC-ueTj1Pg0=/400x0/article-new/2022/03/iphone-se-7.jpg?lossy 400w,https://images.macrumors.com/t/srT2wlJXq_SfKuucT5D6RwYnJUE=/800x0/article-new/2022/03/iphone-se-7.jpg?lossy 800w,https://images.macrumors.com/t/tSZQ9xa59ykOG1ewQiZHCQ4Sne0=/1600x0/article-new/2022/03/iphone-se-7.jpg 1600w,https://images.macrumors.com/t/vQAm4a1UHey3AiygzyGbLr9n3xA=/2500x0/filters:no_upscale()/article-new/2022/03/iphone-se-7.jpg 2500w" sizes="(max-width: 900px) 100vw, 697px" alt="iphone se 7" width="3840" height="2160" class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-841340"/><em></p>
<p>The current ‌iPhone SE‌</p>
<p></em><br />‌Touch ID‌ is still used for Macs and some iPads, but Apple will transition fully to <a href="https://www.macrumors.com/guide/mask-face-id/">Face ID</a> for its ‌iPhone‌ models with the ‌iPhone SE‌ 4.</p>
<p>There are people who prefer ‌Touch ID‌ to ‌Face ID‌, but Apple is all-in on facial recognition, and it&#8217;s more secure than ‌Touch ID‌. There&#8217;s a less than <a href="https://support.apple.com/guide/security/optic-face-touch-passcodes-passwords-sec9479035f1/web">1 in 1,000,000 chance</a> that a random person could unlock your ‌iPhone‌ with ‌Face ID‌, but a <a href="https://support.apple.com/en-us/105095">1 in 50,000 chance</a> for ‌Touch ID‌.</p>
<p>Eliminating the ‌Touch ID‌ home button provides much more viewable screen space, which is a major bonus. Android manufacturers have married large display sizes with fingerprint recognition by putting sensors on the back of their smartphones, but Apple has never adopted that kind of design.</p>

<h3>MagSafe</h3>
<p>The ‌iPhone 14‌ added support for <a href="https://www.macrumors.com/guide/magsafe/">MagSafe</a>, and since the ‌iPhone SE‌ 4 is using an ‌iPhone 14‌ design, it will work with ‌MagSafe‌ accessories and will support ‌MagSafe‌ wireless charging. ‌MagSafe‌ is not an option for the current ‌iPhone SE‌, but ‌MagSafe‌ charging and ‌MagSafe‌ accessories have become an increasingly important part of the Apple ecosystem over the last few years.</p>

<h2>A18 Chip and 8GB RAM</h2>
<p>Apple plans to equip the ‌iPhone SE‌ 4 with the same A18 chip that&#8217;s in the ‌iPhone 16‌, which means it&#8217;s going to have flagship performance at a much more affordable price. The A18 chip will allow it to last for years to come, and it&#8217;s going to be the most affordable way to get <a href="https://www.macrumors.com/guide/apple-intelligence/">Apple Intelligence</a> in a smartphone.</p>

<h2>Storage Space</h2>

<h2>Camera</h2>
<p>The ‌iPhone SE‌ could get the same 48-megapixel Wide lens that&#8217;s in the ‌iPhone 16‌, which will bring a major leap in photo quality compared to the 12-megapixel camera in the ‌iPhone SE‌ 3.</p>

<h2>Battery Life</h2>
<p>Rumors suggest the ‌iPhone SE‌ 4 will have the same battery as the ‌iPhone 14‌, which means it&#8217;ll offer up to 20 hours of video playback, up to 16 hours of streaming video, and up to 80 hours of audio playback.</p>
<p>There&#8217;s quite a bit more space in a 6.1-inch device for a battery than there is in a 4.7-inch device, so it should come as no surprise that there will be a major improvement in overall battery life.</p>
<p>The current ‌iPhone SE‌ offers 15 hours of video playback and up to 10 hours of streaming video playback.</p>
<h2>Pricing</h2>
<p>With higher-end components, the ‌iPhone SE‌ 4 could be more expensive than the current model, but Apple is still aiming for a price point under $500. The ‌iPhone SE‌ 3 is priced starting at $429.</p>
