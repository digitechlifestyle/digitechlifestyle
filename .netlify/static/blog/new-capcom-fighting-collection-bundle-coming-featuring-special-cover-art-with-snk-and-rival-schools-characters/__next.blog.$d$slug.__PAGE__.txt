<h2 class="sub_headline">And Ingrid is there too</h2>
<p>&#13;<br />
       &#13;</p>
<div id="bylineholder" style="padding-left:5px; width:100%;">
<div class="innerbyline" style="border: none;">
<div class="bylinepic">
        <img decoding="async" src="https://media.eventhubs.com/images/dakota-hills.jpg" width="50" height="50" alt="None"/>
      </div>
</p></div>
</div>
<p>       &#13;<br />
    &#13;<br />
    &#13;<br />
    &#13;<br />
    &#13;</p>
<p class="center">&#13;<br />
        <img decoding="async" src="https://media.eventhubs.com/images/2025/02/05-capcom-fighting-pack-special.webp" width="100%" height="auto" border="0" alt=""/>&#13;
        </p>
<p>&#13;<br />
     &#13;</p>
<p>It only took like 20 plus years for Capcom vs. SNK to make a comeback, and soon you&#8217;ll be able to pick up the games alongside most of Capcom&#8217;s large pool of arcade era fighting games.&#13;<br />
&#13;<br />
    &#13;<br />
                &#13;
                    </p>
<p>&#13;<br />
               &#13;<br />
        &#13;<br />
      &#13;<br />
&#13;</p>
<p>Capcom recently revealed a new <a href="https://www.eventhubs.com/news/2025/feb/04/capcom-collection-release-date/">Capcom Fighting Collection</a> bundle that&#8217;ll contain 18 different games in total and features some pretty sweet cover art. </p>
<p>&#13;<br />
	  &#13;<br />
	&#13;<br />
	&#13;<br />
     &#13;<br />
          &#13;<br />
     &#13;<br />
     &#13;<br />
&#13;</p>
<p>The Capcom Fighting Collection 1 &amp; 2 Bundle is just as the name suggests since it features the <a href="https://www.eventhubs.com/news/2022/jun/20/capcom-fighting-collection-review/">previous compilation focused around Darkstalkers</a> plus the upcoming second collection.</p>
<p>&#13;<br />
&#13;</p>
<p>It&#8217;ll be available digitally for the PlayStation 4, Nintendo Switch, Xbox One and PC via Steam though it&#8217;s probably not going to get a physical release.</p>
<p>&#13;<br />
&#13;</p>
<p>That means it won&#8217;t have the option to get the <a href="https://www.eventhubs.com/news/2024/dec/03/capcom-snk-comic-book/">Capcom vs. SNK mini-comic book</a> nor the pre-order bonus music.</p>
<p>&#13;<br />
&#13;</p>
<p>All other content will remain the same next to the individual releases.</p>
<p>&#13;<br />
&#13;</p>
<p>The special cover art features six characters from across a multitude of series with of course Street Fighter&#8217;s Ryu, Rival Schools&#8217; Hinata, Fatal Fury&#8217;s Mai Shiranui and The King of Fighters&#8217; Kyo Kusanagi.</p>
<p>&#13;<br />
&#13;</p>
<p>And Ingrid is up there at the very top as well since it seems like she&#8217;s down to appear anywhere besides a new game.</p>
<p>&#13;<br />
&#13;</p>
<p>We&#8217;re not 100% sure, but the cover looks to have been created by Capcom artist Hinanana, who previously made the <a href="https://www.eventhubs.com/news/2021/apr/20/tarot-cards-rose-victory-sf5ce/">Street Fighter 5 tarot cards</a> and last Capcom Fighting Bundle cover.</p>
<p>&#13;<br />
&#13;</p>
<p>It&#8217;s not stated when the new bundle is coming out, but it&#8217;ll presumably get here at the same time as the Capcom Fighting Collection 2 on May 16.</p>
<p>&#13;<br />
&#13;</p>
<p>A price for the United States isn&#8217;t available yet either, however, the Japanese site lists the bundle at 6,990 yen, which equates to around $45 USD. </p>
<p>&#13;<br />
&#13;</p>
<p>It&#8217;ll probably be at least $50 or more here, though, considering the Capcom Fighting Collection 2 by itself is going to have a retail price of $40.</p>
<p>&#13;<br />
&#13;</p>
<p>The Capcom vs. SNK games are going to be the big draw of the bundle, but fans can also pick up Power Stone, Project Justice and so much more with rollback netcode and new features.</p>
<p>&#13;<br />
&#13;<br />
&#13;</p>
<p>And the new bundle will allow you to nab pretty much every available Capcom fighting game that&#8217;s not a mainline Street Fighter or Marvel vs. Capcom title.</p>
<p>&#13;<br />
&#13;</p>
<p>You can check out the new artwork below as well as the full games list for both collections to see if it&#8217;s something you want to pick up.</p>
