<p>Tonight&#8217;s PlayStation State of Play brought us a look at what awaits the PS5 in the grand old year of 2025. Alongside a peek at some upcoming titles &#8211; some we all knew about, others we didn&#8217;t &#8211; we also got a look at updates for games which have already hit the shelves. <h3>Monster Hunter Wilds</h3>
<p>This State of Play started us off with a look at <a data-keyword="true" href="https://www.eurogamer.net/games/monster-hunter-wilds">Monster Hunter Wilds</a>! The next installment in the Monster Hunter franchise will be arriving on 28th February for PS5, Xbox Series X/S and PC. This trailer gave us a look at some of the many monsters you&#8217;ll be tasked with slaying.</p>
<p>We also got a little look at the first free update coming in spring 2025, which will include facing a really cool looking monster called Mizutsune. Another free update will arrive in the summer.</p>
