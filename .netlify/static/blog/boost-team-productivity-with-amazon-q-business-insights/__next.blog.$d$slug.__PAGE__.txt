									<p></p><div id=""><p>Employee productivity is a critical factor in maintaining a competitive advantage. <a href="https://aws.amazon.com/q/business/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Amazon Q Business</a> offers a unique opportunity to enhance workforce efficiency by providing AI-powered assistance that can significantly reduce the time spent searching for information, generating content, and completing routine tasks. Amazon Q Business is a fully managed, generative AI-powered assistant that lets you build interactive chat applications using your enterprise data, generating answers based on your data or large language model (LLM) knowledge. At the core of this capability are native data source connectors that seamlessly integrate and index content from multiple data sources like Salesforce, Jira, and SharePoint into a unified index.</p><p>Key benefits for organizations include:</p><ul><li><strong>Simplified deployment and management</strong> – Provides a ready-to-use web experience with no machine learning (ML) infrastructure to maintain or manage</li><li><strong>Access controls</strong> – Makes sure users only access content they have permission to view</li><li><strong>Accurate query responses</strong> – Delivers precise answers with source citations, analyzing enterprise data</li><li><strong>Privacy and control</strong> – Offers comprehensive guardrails and fine-grained access controls</li><li><strong>Broad connectivity</strong> – <a href="https://docs.aws.amazon.com/amazonq/latest/qbusiness-ug/connectors-list.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Supports over 45 native data source connectors</a> (at the time of writing), and provides the ability to create custom connectors</li></ul><p>Data privacy and the protection of intellectual property are paramount concerns for most organizations. At Amazon, “Security is Job Zero,” which is why Amazon Q Business is designed with these critical considerations in mind. Your data is not used for training purposes, and the answers provided by Amazon Q Business are based solely on the data users have access to. This makes sure that enterprises can quickly find answers to questions, provide summaries, generate content, and complete tasks across various use cases with complete confidence in data security. Amazon Q Business supports encryption in transit and at rest, allowing end-users to use their own encryption keys for added security. This robust security framework enables end-users to receive immediate, permissions-aware responses from enterprise data sources with citations, helping streamline workplace tasks while maintaining the highest standards of data privacy and protection.</p><p>Amazon Q Business Insights provides administrators with details about the utilization and effectiveness of their AI-powered applications. By monitoring utilization metrics, organizations can quantify the actual productivity gains achieved with Amazon Q Business. Understanding how employees interact with and use Amazon Q Business becomes crucial for measuring its return on investment and identifying potential areas for further optimization. Tracking metrics such as time saved and number of queries resolved can provide tangible evidence of the service’s impact on overall workplace productivity. It’s essential for admins to periodically review these metrics to understand how users are engaging with Amazon Q Business and identify potential areas of improvement.</p><p>The dashboard enables administrators to track user interactions, including the helpfulness of generated answers through user ratings. By visualizing this feedback, admins can pinpoint instances where users aren’t receiving satisfactory responses. With Amazon Q Business Insights, administrators can diagnose potential issues such as unclear user prompts, misconfigured topics and guardrails, insufficient metadata boosters, or inadequate data source configurations. This comprehensive analytics approach empowers organizations to continuously refine their Amazon Q Business implementation, making sure users receive the most relevant and helpful AI-assisted support.</p><p>In this post, we explore Amazon Q Business Insights capabilities and its importance for organizations. Refer to <a href="https://docs.aws.amazon.com/amazonq/latest/qbusiness-ug/monitoring-overview.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Monitoring Amazon Q Business and Q Apps</a> for more details.</p><h3>Set up log delivery with CloudWatch as a destination</h3><p>Complete the following steps to set up log delivery with CloudWatch as the destination:</p><ol><li>Open the Amazon Q Business console and sign in to your account.</li><li>In <strong>Applications</strong>, choose the name of your application environment.</li><li>In the navigation pane, choose <strong>Enhancements</strong> and choose <strong>Admin Controls and Guardrails</strong>.</li><li>In <strong>Log delivery</strong>, choose <strong>Add</strong> and select the option <strong>To Amazon CloudWatch Logs</strong>.</li><li>For <strong>Destination log group</strong>, enter the log group where the logs will be stored.</li></ol><p>Log groups prefixed with <code>/aws/vendedlogs/</code> will be created automatically. Other log groups must be created prior to setting up a log delivery.</p><ol start="6"><li>To filter out sensitive or personally identifiable information (PII), choose <strong>Additional settings – optional</strong> and specify the fields to be logged, output format, and field delimiter.</li></ol><p>If you want the users’ email recorded in your logs, it must be added explicitly as a field in <strong>Additional settings</strong>.</p><ol start="7"><li>Choose <strong>Add</strong>.</li><li>Choose <strong>Enable logging</strong> to start streaming conversation and feedback data to your logging destination.</li></ol><h3>Set up log delivery with Amazon S3 as a destination</h3><p>To use Amazon S3 as a log destination, you will need an S3 bucket and grant Amazon Q Business the appropriate permissions to write your logs to Amazon S3.</p><ol><li>Open the Amazon Q Business console and sign in to your account.</li><li>In <strong>Applications</strong>, choose the name of your application environment.</li><li>In the navigation pane, choose <strong>Enhancements</strong> and choose <strong>Admin Controls and Guardrails</strong>.</li><li>In <strong>Log delivery</strong>, choose <strong>Add</strong> and select the option <strong>To Amazon S3</strong></li><li>For <strong>Destination S3 bucket</strong>, enter your bucket.</li><li>To filter out sensitive or PII data, choose <strong>Additional settings – optional</strong> and specify the fields to be logged, output format, and field delimiter.</li></ol><p>If you want the users’ email recorded in your logs, it must be added explicitly as a field in <strong>Additional settings</strong>.</p><ol start="7"><li>Choose <strong>Add</strong>.</li><li>Choose <strong>Enable logging</strong> to start streaming conversation and feedback data to your logging destination.</li></ol><p>The logs are delivered to your S3 bucket with the following prefix: <code>AWSLogs//AmazonQBusinessLogs///year/month/day/hour/</code> The placeholders will be replaced with your AWS account, Region, and Amazon Q Business application identifier, respectively.</p><h3>Set up Data Firehose as a log destination</h3><p>Amazon Q Business application event logs can also be streamed to Data Firehose as a destination. This can be used for real-time observability. We have excluded setup instructions for brevity.</p><p>To use Data Firehose as a log destination, you need to create a Firehose delivery stream (with Direct PUT enabled) and grant Amazon Q Business the appropriate permissions to write your logs to Data Firehose. For example <a href="https://aws.amazon.com/iam/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">AWS Identity and Access Management</a> (IAM) policies with the required permissions for your specific logging destination, see <a href="https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonCloudWatch/latest/logs/AWS-logs-and-resource-policy.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Enable logging from AWS services</a>.</p><h3>Protecting sensitive data</h3><p>You can prevent an AWS console user or group of users from viewing specific CloudWatch log groups, S3 buckets, or Firehose streams by applying specific deny statements in their IAM policies. AWS follows an explicit deny overrides allow model, meaning that if you explicitly deny an action, it will take precedence over allow statements. For more information, see <a href="https://docs.aws.amazon.com/IAM/latest/UserGuide/reference_policies_evaluation-logic.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Policy evaluation logic</a>.</p><h2>Real-world use cases</h2><p>This section outlines several key use cases for Amazon Q Business Insights, demonstrating how you can use Amazon Q Business operational data to improve your operational posture to help Amazon Q Business meet your needs.</p><h3>Measure ROI using Amazon Q Business Insights</h3><p>The dashboards offered by Amazon Q Business Insights provide powerful metrics that help organizations quantify their ROI.
